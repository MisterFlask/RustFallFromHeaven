VIBE: "Unmade Angel". Holding one long shed white feather across his left palm and drawing his right thumb down its vane, face calm and unreadable, eyes straight on the viewer; alone on the palace roof at midnight, a faint outline of starlight behind him where vast wings once were; cold blue-silver light.

CASSIEL, the archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: once an archangel, now wingless and mortal; male; ageless, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair with the first grey at the temples, deep grey eyes. For this portrait he wears the stripped remains of his angelic war-gear: a breastplate of pale white bronze, its holy sigils chiselled off and its gem settings empty, over a plain charcoal-grey undertunic. Fine HAIRLINE CRACKS like those in an old marble statue run across his ivory skin at the temples, throat and backs of the hands, a faint cold white light showing in them. What is left of the angel in him is uncanny, though he has no wings and his eyes are human grey: his face is too symmetrical and too calm for a mortal, like a statue that has learned to breathe; he never blinks; and the air around him is wrong: dust motes, smoke and candle flames near him hang motionless, as if he were the still centre of the room. Pose, light and setting: Holding one long shed white feather across his left palm and drawing his right thumb down its vane, face calm and unreadable, eyes straight on the viewer; alone on the palace roof at midnight, a faint outline of starlight behind him where vast wings once were; cold blue-silver light. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Unmade Angel".
