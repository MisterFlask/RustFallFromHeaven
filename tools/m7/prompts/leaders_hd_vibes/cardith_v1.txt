CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, a small straight nose, smooth unlined skin, short neat dark-brown hair. For this portrait he wears a boy's light summer tunic of white linen embroidered at the neck with tiny green leaves, a pale green silk sash, a slender gold circlet. His eyes are always cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils, the one thing in his soft boyish face that is not a child's. He holds himself with an old man's stillness: no fidgeting, no wasted movement, his small hands slow and precise. Pose, light and setting: Holding a fallen sparrow chick cupped in both small hands at his chest, a sweet child's smile, his gold-green slit-pupilled eyes lifted straight to the viewer, cold and glinting; a white marble garden court with a fountain, bright clear morning sun. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Serpent Eyes".
