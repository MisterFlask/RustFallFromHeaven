ARENDEL PHAEDRA, the Summer Queen of the Seelie court, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks about forty; tall and regal. Fair skin, long wavy copper-auburn hair, long pointed elf ears, soft grey-green eyes with faint fine lines at their corners. For this portrait she wears a heavy winter cloak of pale grey wool lined with white fox fur, the hood down, over a plain high-necked gown of moss green. Her delicate GOLDEN CROWN OF INTERLACED LEAVES AND BRANCHES is always with her, on her brow or in her hands.
