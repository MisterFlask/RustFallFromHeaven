VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-seeing sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, a long straight nose, fine lines at the eyes and mouth, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. For this portrait she wears a plain black wool gown buttoned high to the throat, with long tight sleeves and no ornament but the circlet. On her brow, whatever else she wears, a slim silver circlet holds a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE. Her face gives almost nothing away: brows level, mouth closed and even, her head held very straight. Pose, light and setting: Turning a small painted seashell over in the fingers of her right hand, her left hand cupped beneath it, never looking at it; eyes level on the rain-streaked window, the barest nod; her study on a grey rainy afternoon, flat cold light, the door just closed. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Necessary Murder".
