VIBE: "Endless City". On a terrace, his right hand pressing his jade seal into green wax on a new charter, his left holding the parchment flat on the balustrade, chin lifted, a satisfied smile, eyes turned out over the city; green domes and gold roofs to every horizon under a clear noon sky.

CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, a small straight nose, smooth unlined skin, short neat dark-brown hair. For this portrait he wears state regalia: a long mantle of cloth of gold over robes of pale jade-green silk, the jade seal on a heavy gold chain, a slender gold circlet. His eyes are always cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils, the one thing in his soft boyish face that is not a child's. He holds himself with an old man's stillness: no fidgeting, no wasted movement, his small hands slow and precise. Pose, light and setting: On a terrace, his right hand pressing his jade seal into green wax on a new charter, his left holding the parchment flat on the balustrade, chin lifted, a satisfied smile, eyes turned out over the city; green domes and gold roofs to every horizon under a clear noon sky. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Endless City".
