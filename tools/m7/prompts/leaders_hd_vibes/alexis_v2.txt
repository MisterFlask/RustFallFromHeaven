ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, fine dark brows, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. For this portrait she wears a long white linen nightgown buttoned to the throat and wrists, a heavy dove-grey wool shawl wrapped tight around her shoulders. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES is always on her brow. She has the stillness of something that no longer breathes: she does not blink and her shoulders never rise or fall. Whatever she wears is closed high at the throat, and no blood and no fangs show anywhere in the picture. Pose, light and setting: Both hands cupped around a candle burned down to a stub, shielding its shrinking flame from the wind, shoulders drawn in, lips pressed tight, gold eyes wide and fixed on the flame; an open palace balcony in the cold blue hour before dawn. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Fear of Dying".
