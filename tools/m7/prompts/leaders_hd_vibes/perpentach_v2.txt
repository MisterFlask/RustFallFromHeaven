PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though centuries old; lean and wiry, with long restless fingers. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. For this portrait he wears a long patchwork robe sewn from scraps of many people's clothes, a farmer's brown homespun, a soldier's red, a priest's white, a child's blue, no two patches alike. His smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK, one black tear painted under its eye, is always with him, pushed up on his forehead or in his hand. Pose, light and setting: Fingertips of both hands pressed to his temples as if tuning many voices at once; a soft rapt smile, eyes unfocused past the viewer, listening; the court inside his own mind, a hall without walls in timeless grey-violet half-light, pale faces of every age crowding around his head. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Swallowed Minds". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
