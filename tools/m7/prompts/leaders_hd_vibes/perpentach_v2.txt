PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though centuries old; lean and wiry, with long restless fingers. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. For this portrait he wears a long patchwork robe sewn from scraps of many people's clothes, a farmer's brown homespun, a soldier's red, a priest's white, a child's blue, no two patches alike. His smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK, one black tear painted under its eye, is always with him, pushed up on his forehead or in his hand. Pose, light and setting: Fingertips of both hands pressed to his temples as if tuning many voices at once; a soft rapt smile, eyes unfocused past the viewer, listening; the court inside his own mind, a hall without walls in timeless grey-violet half-light, pale faces of every age crowding around his head. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Swallowed Minds".
