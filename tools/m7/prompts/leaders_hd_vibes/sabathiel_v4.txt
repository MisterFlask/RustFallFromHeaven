SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. For this portrait he wears ceremonial white plate polished like porcelain, without a visible seam, a crimson cloak hanging perfectly still. Great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded high behind him. He is an angel, terrible and unknowable: his eyes are pure white light with no pupils, his face is smooth and still as a mask with no human feeling in it, a second and a third pair of wings unfold behind the first with open eyes among the feathers, and any people near him reach only to his chest, kneeling or shielding their eyes. Pose, light and setting: Holding a full cup of wine perfectly level, untouched for hours, his other hand resting motionless on the arm of his throne; candles burned to stubs; a frozen court at midnight. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Inhuman Stillness".
