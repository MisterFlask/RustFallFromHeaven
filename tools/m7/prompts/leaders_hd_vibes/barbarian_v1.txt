THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc, goblin and ogre hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old pale scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, a flattened nose, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. For this portrait he wears light raiding gear: a black leather harness over bare painted arms, a shaggy grey wolf-pelt mantle, strings of bone charms rattling at his chest. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL is always on his head, worn as a helmet. No blood anywhere on him, his gear or his weapons.
