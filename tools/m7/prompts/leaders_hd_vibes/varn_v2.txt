VARN GOSAM, the elven prince turned sun-priest, leader of the Malakim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an elf living among desert men; male; ageless, looks about forty; tall, lean and wiry. Sun-bronzed skin, a narrow face with high cheekbones, long straight black hair, long pointed elf ears, bright amber-gold eyes. For this portrait he wears the clothes of an elven prince of the Shadowed Vale: a close-fitting coat of charcoal-black silk with silver leaf clasps, a long dark grey cloak beaded with mist. A GOLDEN SUN-DISC with straight pointed rays, the holy sign of Lugus, always hangs on a heavy chain at his chest. He never squints: even in the brightest light his amber-gold eyes stay wide open and his brows relaxed.
