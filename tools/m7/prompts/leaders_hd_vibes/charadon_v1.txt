CHARADON, the wolf-father who founded the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old but still hugely powerful; massive and broad, nearly a giant. Weather-beaten pale skin, old pale claw scars across one cheek and both forearms, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. For this portrait he wears a vast shaggy cloak of many grey wolf pelts sewn together, their tails still hanging at the hem, over a plain charcoal felt tunic. His GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD is always on him, the snarling wolf's head on top of his own and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. His face never moves, whatever he is doing: no snarl, no smile, no anger, mouth a flat line in the beard, his yellow-grey eyes level and unblinking like a wolf watching the weakest of a herd. Pose, light and setting: Holding a wolf pup up by the scruff at eye level in his right hand, eyes on it, looking it over, his left hand heavy on its mother's head at his knee, more wolves pressing round his legs like children; a snowy pine clearing, soft grey morning, snow falling. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Wolf Father".
