Seated utterly still in an ancient high-backed chair, both hands holding a silver candle snuffer across her lap, face calm as marble, gold eyes level and unblinking on the viewer; a vaulted crypt at midnight, hundreds of candle stubs, most burned out, a few lighting her from below.

ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, fine dark brows, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. For this portrait she wears a centuries-old court gown of crimson brocade faded to dusty rose, a tall stiff collar of tarnished silver lace, old rings on every finger. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES is always on her brow. She has the stillness of something that no longer breathes: she does not blink and her shoulders never rise or fall. Whatever she wears is closed high at the throat, and no blood and no fangs show anywhere in the picture. Pose, light and setting: Seated utterly still in an ancient high-backed chair, both hands holding a silver candle snuffer across her lap, face calm as marble, gold eyes level and unblinking on the viewer; a vaulted crypt at midnight, hundreds of candle stubs, most burned out, a few lighting her from below. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "First Vampire".
