ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, fine dark brows, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. For this portrait she wears a centuries-old court gown of crimson brocade faded to dusty rose, a tall stiff collar of tarnished silver lace, old rings on every finger. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES is always on her brow. She has the stillness of something that no longer breathes: she does not blink and her shoulders never rise or fall. Whatever she wears is closed high at the throat, and no blood and no fangs show anywhere in the picture.
