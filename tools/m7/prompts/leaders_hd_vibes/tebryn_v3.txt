TEBRYN ARBANDI, the runecaster who returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; looks about fifty; gaunt and hollow-cheeked. Ashen skin with old pale scars, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard, long bony fingers with hooked, ragged nails. For this portrait he wears the regalia of the end of the world: a mantle of glossy black crow feathers over a long robe of deep violet silk embroidered with spiralling silver runes. A black cloth BLINDFOLD STITCHED WITH SILVER RUNES is always tied over his eyes; through it he sees the spirits of the dead. His eyes are never seen: the blindfold covers them completely in every portrait, and where he looks is simply where his blindfolded face is turned.
