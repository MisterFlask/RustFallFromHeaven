BEERI BAWL, heir of Graoin the Delver, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and strong. Ruddy weathered skin, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two braids, heavy brows, small fierce dark eyes. For this portrait he wears a miner's patched canvas smock in faded ochre, sleeves rolled, a thick leather tool harness hung with brass tools and a coil of rope. His dented brass MINER'S HELMET with its little lamp is always on his head. He is clenched defiance in every portrait: brows crushed down, jaw set, thick shoulders hunched forward like a bull's. Pose, light and setting: His left hand reaching up to turn up the wick of the lamp on his helmet, his right gripping a miner's pick over his shoulder, eyes fixed unblinking on the viewer, brows down; a black mine tunnel deep under the mountain at night, the helmet lamp the only light. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Mine Lamp".
