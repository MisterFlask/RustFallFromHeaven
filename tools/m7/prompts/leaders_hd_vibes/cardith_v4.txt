CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, a small straight nose, smooth unlined skin, short neat dark-brown hair. For this portrait he wears a coronation robe centuries out of fashion, crimson and gold brocade faded and stiff with age, a tall ancient gold crown worn smooth by time. His eyes are always cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils, the one thing in his soft boyish face that is not a child's. He holds himself with an old man's stillness: no fidgeting, no wasted movement, his small hands slow and precise. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Ageless Boy".
