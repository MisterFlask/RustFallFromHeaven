SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. For this portrait he wears a long white archivist's robe, its hems and sleeves embroidered with rows of small open eyes in gold thread. Great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded high behind him. He is an angel, terrible and unknowable: his eyes are pure white light with no pupils, his face is smooth and still as a mask with no human feeling in it, a second and a third pair of wings unfold behind the first with open eyes among the feathers, and any people near him reach only to his chest, kneeling or shielding their eyes. Pose, light and setting: Holding a huge ledger of every life in his realm open on one forearm and turning its pages with the other hand without looking down, his eyes of light on the tall windows around him, each a different land; pale morning. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Thousand Windows".
