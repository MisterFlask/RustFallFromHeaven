VIBE: "Endless Evening". Seated on the edge of her bed at dusk, rubbing her temple with one hand, a hairpin she has just pulled out dangling from the other, half her hair fallen down; eyes on the floor; grey-violet light in a bare stone bedchamber.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall. Pale skin gone dull with tiredness, long black hair hanging limp and loose with a few strands across her face, dark violet eyes with deep shadows under them. For this portrait she wears a heavy black velvet dressing robe over a high-necked crimson nightgown. Her pale white glowing JEWELS are always on her, set into whatever she wears (collar, girdle, rings). She is weary beyond anything mortal: alive since the first morning of the world and bored by all of it, and it shows in her whole body: shoulders dropped, weight leaning on what is beside her, eyelids half-closed, mouth slack, no tension anywhere. This weariness overrides any drama in the style: no heroic pose, no wind in her hair, no triumphant light, no rim light. Pose, light and setting: Seated on the edge of her bed at dusk, rubbing her temple with one hand, a hairpin she has just pulled out dangling from the other, half her hair fallen down; eyes on the floor; grey-violet light in a bare stone bedchamber. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Endless Evening". Colours: black, crimson, storm slate, gold.
