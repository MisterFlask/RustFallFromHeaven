ARTURUS THORNE, the old dwarf king of the deep halls, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a broad nose, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes, broad scarred hands. For this portrait he wears a king's heavy robe of oxblood-red wool trimmed with dark fur over a gold-chased breastplate, a heavy gold chain, rings on every thick finger. His squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points is always on his head. He is planted like the mountain he swears by: his weight set solid on the stone under him, never leaning or reaching. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Vault King".
