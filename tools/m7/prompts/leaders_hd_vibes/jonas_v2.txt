JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess Bhall, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green battle-scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, heavy brows over amber eyes. For this portrait he wears a warlord's black-iron scale hauberk with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black boar-hide mantle, and the Ember Banner's rust-orange hide sash across his chest. The broad dark BURN SCAR across the left side of his face and neck, from the sacred fire of Braduk he walked into at his goddess's command, is always plainly visible. No skulls and no blood anywhere in the picture.
