PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though centuries old; lean and wiry, with long restless fingers. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. For this portrait he wears the stage king's harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds, a tall pleated white ruff and a purple velvet cape trimmed in gold. His smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK, one black tear painted under its eye, is always with him, pushed up on his forehead or in his hand.
