VIBE: "Nobody Home". Her arms hanging limp at her sides, hands doing nothing at all, staring blankly straight into the camera; rain running down her face unnoticed; the deck of the Desecrated Vessel at grey dusk.

HANNAH THE IRIN, the mutineer pirate queen born of a cult dreamer, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; female; about thirty; wiry. Wind-burned olive skin with a grey, drowned pallor, long black hair always wet and hanging straight and heavy, pale grey eyes like worn sea glass with no light in them, faint ring-shaped marks on her neck and wrists like the grip of suckers. For this portrait she wears a drenched, ankle-length high-necked linen shift and a sodden grey shawl, like a dreamer lifted out of her recess. Her mother's NECKLACE OF HOLLOW BONE NEEDLES on a black cord, each needle tipped with a stub of whale-fat wax and a tiny wick, two or three still burning, wax dripped and hardened down her collarbone. She is hollow, and something else looks out through her: her face is completely relaxed and empty, no smile, no frown, brows and mouth slack; her eyes have no catchlight and are fixed level, on nothing or straight through the viewer; she does not blink. She is perfectly still while everything around her moves. This emptiness overrides any drama in the style: her face shows no feeling at all. Pose, light and setting: Her arms hanging limp at her sides, hands doing nothing at all, staring blankly straight into the camera; rain running down her face unnoticed; the deck of the Desecrated Vessel at grey dusk. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Nobody Home".
