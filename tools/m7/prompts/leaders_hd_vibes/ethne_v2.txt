VIBE: "Lame Parrot". Cradling Splendor on her cupped left hand against her chest and feeding him a grape from the fingertips of her right, eyes down on him, a soft smile, as if he were the world; under a tulip poplar by a marble fountain, misty garden at dawn, soft pearl light.

ETHNE THE WHITE, the young queen raised never to see suffering, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair, large clear blue eyes. For this portrait she wears a soft primrose-yellow silk morning gown with white lace at the cuffs and collar, her hair loose down her back, no jewels. SPLENDOR, her lame BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT, missing one claw and standing on one leg, is always with her, on her shoulder, on her hand or close by. However much she feels, and it shows openly in her face, her eyes stay clear and steady and she never looks away. Pose, light and setting: Cradling Splendor on her cupped left hand against her chest and feeding him a grape from the fingertips of her right, eyes down on him, a soft smile, as if he were the world; under a tulip poplar by a marble fountain, misty garden at dawn, soft pearl light. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Lame Parrot".
