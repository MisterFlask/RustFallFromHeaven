HANNAH THE IRIN, the mutineer pirate queen born of a cult dreamer, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; female; about thirty; wiry. Wind-burned olive skin with a grey, drowned pallor, long black hair always wet and hanging straight and heavy, pale grey eyes like worn sea glass with no light in them, faint ring-shaped marks on her neck and wrists like the grip of suckers. A long plain sea-coat of waterlogged grey-black sailcloth with no braid, buttons or sash, over a yellowed high-necked linen shift like a burial shirt, salt crust and dried kelp at the hems. Her mother's NECKLACE OF HOLLOW BONE NEEDLES on a black cord, each needle tipped with a stub of whale-fat wax and a tiny wick, two or three still burning, wax dripped and hardened down her collarbone. She is hollow, and something else looks out through her: her face is completely relaxed and empty, no smile, no frown, brows and mouth slack; her eyes have no catchlight and are fixed level, on nothing or straight through the viewer; she does not blink. She is perfectly still while everything around her moves. This emptiness overrides any drama in the style: her face shows no feeling at all. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Dreamer's Daughter". Colours: navy, sea green, storm yellow, bone white.
