FALAMAR, the charming pirate Sea-King, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late thirties; lean and graceful. Sun-bronzed skin, black hair in a short queue, a neat pointed black beard, lively dark eyes, a gold earring. For this portrait he wears his salt-stained royal-blue sea-coat with tarnished gold braid, a white lace jabot and sea boots still damp, plain among the nobles' silks. His broad BLUE TRICORN HAT WITH A LONG WHITE PLUME is on his head or in his hand. He is always at his ease: a loose stance, weight on one hip, one corner of his mouth turned up as though at a private joke. Pose, light and setting: Lounging against a gilded pillar, popping a sugared fruit from the royal banquet into his mouth with one hand, a brimming goblet in the other, eyes sliding sideways to the stiff nobles, grinning; a royal ballroom at night, blazing chandeliers, warm gold light. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Pirate Courtier".
