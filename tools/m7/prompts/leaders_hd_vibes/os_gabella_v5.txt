VIBE: "Endless Evening". Seated on the edge of her bed in full armour at dusk, too tired to take it off.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall. Pale skin gone dull with tiredness, long black hair hanging limp and loose with a few strands fallen across her face, dark violet eyes with deep shadows under them. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour, the armour scuffed and the jewels dimmed. She is weary beyond anything mortal: she has been alive since the first morning of the world, and it shows in her whole body: shoulders dropped, weight leaning on what is beside her, eyelids half-closed, mouth slack, no tension anywhere. This weariness overrides any drama in the style: no heroic pose, no wind in her hair, no triumphant light, no rim light. Pose, light and setting: she sits on the edge of a plain bed, one gauntlet unbuckled and dangling from her loose fingers, the other hand rubbing her temple; her half-closed eyes look down at the floor; her shoulders sag under the pauldrons. Flat grey-violet dusk from a tall window, no rim light, the colours faded. A bare stone bedchamber in her black citadel, a cold hearth, the crimson gown dusty at the hem. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Endless Evening". Colours: grey, faded crimson, black, dull violet.
