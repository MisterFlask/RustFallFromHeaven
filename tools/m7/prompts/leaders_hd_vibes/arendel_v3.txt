ARENDEL PHAEDRA, the Summer Queen of the Seelie court, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks about forty; tall and regal. Fair skin, long wavy copper-auburn hair, long pointed elf ears, soft grey-green eyes with faint fine lines at their corners. For this portrait she wears a gown of two halves, warm gold silk down one side and silver-blue silk down the other, joined at the front by a seam of small pearls. Her delicate GOLDEN CROWN OF INTERLACED LEAVES AND BRANCHES is always with her, on her brow or in her hands. Pose, light and setting: Seated at a rippled window of the winter palace, her right hand pouring wine from a silver jug into a second cup set for her sister, her left steadying it, a guarded smile, eyes on the viewer; half her face lit hearth gold, half winter-night blue from the glass. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Sister Seasons".
