SHEELBA, the orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean, a head taller than most men. Olive-green skin, a heavy brow, a broad jaw, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. For this portrait she wears both worlds at once: a fitted wine-red velvet human riding dress with lace cuffs, a shaggy black wolf-fur mantle over it, a bone-and-iron orc pauldron on one shoulder. At her throat, whatever else she wears, is the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. She is plainly an orc, not a human painted green: her tusks always show, even with her mouth closed.
