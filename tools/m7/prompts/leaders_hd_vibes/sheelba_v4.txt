SHEELBA, the orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean, a head taller than most men. Olive-green skin, a heavy brow, a broad jaw, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. For this portrait she wears both worlds at once: a fitted wine-red velvet human riding dress with lace cuffs, a shaggy black wolf-fur mantle over it, a bone-and-iron orc pauldron on one shoulder. At her throat, whatever else she wears, is the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. She is plainly an orc, not a human painted green: her tusks always show, even with her mouth closed. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Between Worlds". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
