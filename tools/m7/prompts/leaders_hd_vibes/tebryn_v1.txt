TEBRYN ARBANDI, the runecaster who returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; looks about fifty; gaunt and hollow-cheeked. Ashen skin with old pale scars, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard, long bony fingers with hooked, ragged nails. For this portrait he wears what he wore out of hell: the scaly hide of a pit beast thrown over his shoulders like a cloak, its spines along the ridge, over a coarse ash-brown shift. A black cloth BLINDFOLD STITCHED WITH SILVER RUNES is always tied over his eyes; through it he sees the spirits of the dead. His eyes are never seen: the blindfold covers them completely in every portrait, and where he looks is simply where his blindfolded face is turned.
