SANDALPHON, the immortal shade who traded part of his soul for eternity, leader of the Sidar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human, becoming a shade; male; ageless, looking about forty; tall and upright. Unnaturally pale ash-grey skin, a long, smooth, clean-shaven face with high cheekbones, short smoke-grey hair swept back, dull grey eyes in which light never glints. For this portrait he wears the ritual robe of the Grey: long, plain bone-white silk with wide sleeves, a narrow black stole stitched with silver runes hanging to the hem. He always wears a thin CIRCLET OF DULL PEWTER set with a single smoky grey stone that, like his eyes, gives back no light. He is a shade: wherever the light is weak his edges come apart, his shoulders, the ends of his sleeves and the hem of his clothes dissolving into soft grey mist that drifts off into the air, so it is hard to say where he stops and the air begins; his face always stays solid and clear. He feels almost nothing, and it shows: a smooth, relaxed face, level brows, a flat mouth, never a real smile, and no catchlight in his eyes.
