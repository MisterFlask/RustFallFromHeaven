ETHNE THE WHITE, the young queen raised never to see suffering, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair, large clear blue eyes. For this portrait she wears her father's state regalia cut down for her: a stiff white-and-silver brocade gown, a royal-blue velvet mantle with silver clasps, and a silver crown a little too big, sitting low on her brow. SPLENDOR, her lame BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT, missing one claw and standing on one leg, is always with her, on her shoulder, on her hand or close by. However much she feels, and it shows openly in her face, her eyes stay clear and steady and she never looks away. Pose, light and setting: Pressing her father's too-large signet ring into the red wax of a decree with her right hand, her left flat on the council table, chin up, eyes steady on a doubting councillor to one side; Splendor on her chair back; a marble council hall at midday, grey-bearded elders in shadow. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Young Crown".
