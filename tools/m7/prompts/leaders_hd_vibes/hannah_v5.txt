HANNAH THE IRIN, the mutineer pirate queen born of a cultist dreamer, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; female; about thirty; wiry. Wind-burned olive skin, salt-crusted black hair hanging wet and heavy, pale grey eyes like worn sea glass with no light in them. A weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons, a sea-blue sash, and a NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord. She is hollow, and something else looks out through her: her face is completely relaxed and empty, no smile, no frown, brows and mouth slack; her eyes have no catchlight, are slightly unfocused and look a little past the viewer at nothing; she does not blink. She is perfectly still while everything around her moves. This emptiness overrides any drama in the style: her face shows no feeling at all. Pose, light and setting: her hands tie a sailor's knot in a wet rope, quick and perfect, without her looking at them; her empty eyes look past you at nothing. Rain and wind lash the deck and whip the sails behind her, and not one strand of her heavy wet hair moves. Flat colourless light on her face; the storm behind her lit sickly green by lightning. The deck of her ship at night in a gale, a lantern swinging. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Sleepwalker". Colours: slate, navy, sickly green, bone white.
