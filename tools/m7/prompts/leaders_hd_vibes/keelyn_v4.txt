KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king Perpentach and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight, a child's build. A pale, freckled, round little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. For this portrait she wears the gold-and-black harlequin dress of her father's court with a high ruffled gold collar, and a small gold circlet she has put on herself. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, Puppy, a matching rose-pink bow tied on one of its horns, is always with her. She is a child and drawn as one: a child's round face and small hands, modest childish clothes, nothing grown-up or glamorous; sweet and a little eerie, her big amber eyes hardly ever blinking. Pose, light and setting: Seated on her father's throne, too big for her, her right hand holding one claw of the huge horned demon looming protectively behind her in shadow, her left hand stroking Puppy in her lap; chin up, eyes sliding sideways to the bowing ministers; the council hall at grey morning. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Unacknowledged Princess".
