DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; male; ageless, looking a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes. For this portrait he wears travel-worn road clothes: a charcoal riding coat and a cloak grey with dust, mud-splashed boots, plain and dark among the courtiers' bright silks. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN, made for a broader human head, sits tipped askew on his brow. His pale eyes are always tired and measuring, lids a little lowered and one eyebrow faintly raised: a man betrayed by every friend he has had, who expects you to be next. Pose, light and setting: Pulling the riding glove off his left hand finger by finger with his right as he walks in, eyes sliding sideways to the human courtiers whispering behind their hands, a dry half-smile; a bright white marble human court at morning, coloured light from stained glass on their silks. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Exile King".
