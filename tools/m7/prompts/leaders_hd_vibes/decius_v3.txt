DECIUS, the exiled soldier who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Sun- and wind-weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. For this portrait he wears travel-worn road clothes: a charcoal riding coat and a cloak grey with dust, mud-splashed boots, plain and dark among the courtiers' bright silks. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN sits tipped askew on his brow. His pale eyes are always tired and measuring, lids a little lowered and one eyebrow faintly raised: a man betrayed by every friend he has had, who expects you to be next. Pose, light and setting: Pulling the riding glove off his left hand finger by finger with his right as he walks in, eyes sliding sideways to the courtiers whispering behind their hands about the foreign soldier, a dry half-smile; a bright white marble human court at morning, coloured light from stained glass on their silks. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Exile King". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
