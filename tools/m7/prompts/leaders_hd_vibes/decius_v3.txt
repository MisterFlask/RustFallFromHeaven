DECIUS, the exiled soldier who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Sun- and wind-weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. For this portrait he wears travel-worn road clothes: a charcoal riding coat and a cloak grey with dust, mud-splashed boots, plain and dark among the courtiers' bright silks. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN sits tipped askew on his brow. His pale eyes are always tired and measuring, lids a little lowered and one eyebrow faintly raised: a man betrayed by every friend he has had, who expects you to be next. Pose, light and setting: Pulling the riding glove off his left hand finger by finger with his right as he walks in, eyes sliding sideways to the courtiers whispering behind their hands about the foreign soldier, a dry half-smile; a bright white marble human court at morning, coloured light from stained glass on their silks. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Exile King".
