CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty-five; tall and strong. Fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, a strong square jaw, dark chestnut hair, grey eyes, two fine lines of strain between the brows. For this portrait she wears a plain soldier's greatcoat of dark blue wool buttoned to the throat, a mail collar beneath, no rank but a small silver sword badge. Her long thick CHESTNUT BRAID always hangs forward over one shoulder. She holds herself like a soldier on parade whatever she feels: back straight, chin level. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Never Afraid".
