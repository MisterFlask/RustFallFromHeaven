KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king Perpentach and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight, a child's build. A pale, freckled, round little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. For this portrait she wears a sunny yellow tea-party dress with white puffed sleeves and a wide white sash, a paper party crown on her head. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, Puppy, a matching rose-pink bow tied on one of its horns, is always with her. She is a child and drawn as one: a child's round face and small hands, modest childish clothes, nothing grown-up or glamorous; sweet and a little eerie, her big amber eyes hardly ever blinking. Pose, light and setting: Her right hand pouring from a gold teapot into a tiny cup in a huge demon's claw, her left hand holding the lid on; beaming at her guest; a garden tea party at golden afternoon, horned guests three times her size in shade, in pink bows, Puppy on the table. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Demon Playmates".
