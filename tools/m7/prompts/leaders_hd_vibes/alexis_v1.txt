ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, fine dark brows, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. For this portrait she wears a scholar's long robe of dark ink-blue velvet over a high-necked black dress, narrow silver buttons to the throat, ink stains on one cuff. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES is always on her brow. She has the stillness of something that no longer breathes: she does not blink and her shoulders never rise or fall. Whatever she wears is closed high at the throat, and no blood and no fangs show anywhere in the picture. Pose, light and setting: Leaning toward the viewer, her right hand holding one candle up near your face, her left writing in an open notebook without looking, lips parted, unblinking gold eyes fixed on yours; plundered books in a dark library gallery, dusk dying crimson in a tall window. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Death's Student".
