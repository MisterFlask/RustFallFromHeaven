THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc, goblin and ogre hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old pale scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, a flattened nose, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. For this portrait he wears his full war gear: scorched black iron plate with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin over his shoulders, a heavy cleaver at his belt. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL is always on his head, worn as a helmet. No blood anywhere on him, his gear or his weapons. Pose, light and setting: Sneering, lip curled off one tusk, his right hand holding a tasselled rose-silk cushion out at arm's length by one tassel, his left ripping a lace curtain off its rail, eyes narrowed on the cushion; a looted gilded salon at smoky dusk, the soft city burning beyond tall windows. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Too Civilized". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
