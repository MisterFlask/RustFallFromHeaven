Sneering, lip curled off one tusk, his right hand holding a tasselled rose-silk cushion out at arm's length by one tassel, his left ripping a lace curtain off its rail, eyes narrowed on the cushion; a looted gilded salon at smoky dusk, the soft city burning beyond tall windows.

THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc, goblin and ogre hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old pale scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, a flattened nose, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. For this portrait he wears his full war gear: scorched black iron plate with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin over his shoulders, a heavy cleaver at his belt. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL is always on his head, worn as a helmet. No blood anywhere on him, his gear or his weapons. Pose, light and setting: Sneering, lip curled off one tusk, his right hand holding a tasselled rose-silk cushion out at arm's length by one tassel, his left ripping a lace curtain off its rail, eyes narrowed on the cushion; a looted gilded salon at smoky dusk, the soft city burning beyond tall windows. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Too Civilized".
