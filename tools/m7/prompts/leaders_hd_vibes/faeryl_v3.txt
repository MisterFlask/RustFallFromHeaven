FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; female; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair falling past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. For this portrait she wears a stiff formal court gown of black silk with a vast standing collar of silver spiderweb lace, fine silver threads like web strands trailing from her sleeves. Her tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS rises from her silver hair. In every portrait she hides a slim silver STILETTO with a small spider-shaped pommel where the viewer can see it and the person she is smiling at cannot; it lies flat and still, pointing at no one and never toward the viewer, with no blood anywhere. Whatever her mouth does, her violet eyes stay cold and still.
