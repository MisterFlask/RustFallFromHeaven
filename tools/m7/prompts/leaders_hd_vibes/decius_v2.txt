DECIUS, the exiled soldier who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Sun- and wind-weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. For this portrait he wears a desert campaign surcoat of sun-faded sand linen over plain mail, a loose white Malakim head-wrap around his neck, dust on his boots. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN sits tipped askew on his brow. His pale eyes are always tired and measuring, lids a little lowered and one eyebrow faintly raised: a man betrayed by every friend he has had, who expects you to be next.
