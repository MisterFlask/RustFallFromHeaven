SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. For this portrait he wears a human king's sober clothes: a dark blue doublet, a fur-trimmed mantle, a plain gold circlet. In the room he passes for a man, with a calm human face and grey eyes and no wings; only his shadow shows what he is. Pose, light and setting: Signing a decree with a quill like any king, his other hand pressing the parchment flat, while the shadow he throws on the wall has vast wings and many eyes; a lamplit study at night. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Hidden Angel". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
