SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. For this portrait he wears a human king's sober clothes: a dark blue doublet, a fur-trimmed mantle, a plain gold circlet. In the room he passes for a man, with a calm human face and grey eyes and no wings; only his shadow shows what he is. Pose, light and setting: Signing a decree with a quill like any king, his other hand pressing the parchment flat, while the shadow he throws on the wall has vast wings and many eyes; a lamplit study at night. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Hidden Angel".
