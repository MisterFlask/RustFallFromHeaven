VIBE: "Sun-Struck". Holding a gold-framed burning-glass up to the blazing noon sun in his right hand, its bright point kindling the altar fire, his left feeding dry palm fronds into the flame; laughing open-mouthed, face washed gold, eyes wide on the viewer; a white rooftop sun-altar at noon.

VARN GOSAM, the elven prince turned sun-priest, leader of the Malakim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an elf living among desert men; male; ageless, looks about forty; tall, lean and wiry. Sun-bronzed skin, a narrow face with high cheekbones, long straight black hair, long pointed elf ears, bright amber-gold eyes. For this portrait he wears light high-summer festival vestments: a sleeveless saffron-yellow linen robe, broad gold armbands, a pleated sunburst collar of gold-dyed linen spread across his shoulders. A GOLDEN SUN-DISC with straight pointed rays, the holy sign of Lugus, always hangs on a heavy chain at his chest. He never squints: even in the brightest light his amber-gold eyes stay wide open and his brows relaxed. Pose, light and setting: Holding a gold-framed burning-glass up to the blazing noon sun in his right hand, its bright point kindling the altar fire, his left feeding dry palm fronds into the flame; laughing open-mouthed, face washed gold, eyes wide on the viewer; a white rooftop sun-altar at noon. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Sun-Struck".
