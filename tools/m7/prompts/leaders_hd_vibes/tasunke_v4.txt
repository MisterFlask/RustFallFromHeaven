TASUNKE, the most famous raider of the horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late forties; broad-chested and rugged. Sun-darkened, weathered skin, deep lines at the corners of his eyes, a hooked nose, long black hair going grey at the temples, worn in two braids, a drooping black moustache, dark eyes. For this portrait he wears plundered finery over his armour: a looted noble's purple velvet coat too tight across his chest, three gold chains of different makers, a ring on every finger.
