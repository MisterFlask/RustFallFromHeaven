ETHNE THE WHITE, the young queen raised never to see suffering, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair, large clear blue eyes. For this portrait she wears a sober high-necked black mourning gown, a black lace veil pushed back off her face, a plain black cord at the waist. SPLENDOR, her lame BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT, missing one claw and standing on one leg, is always with her, on her shoulder, on her hand or close by. However much she feels, and it shows openly in her face, her eyes stay clear and steady and she never looks away. Pose, light and setting: Tying a white mourning ribbon onto a temple railing already hung with hundreds of them, both hands working the knot, tears running down a face she holds steady, eyes straight on the viewer; Splendor on her shoulder; temple steps in the poor quarter, overcast morning, flat grey drizzle. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Breaking Heart".
