CHARADON, the wolf-father who founded the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old but still hugely powerful; massive and broad, nearly a giant. Weather-beaten pale skin, old pale claw scars across one cheek and both forearms, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. For this portrait he wears his burial gear: ancient bronze scale armour gone green with age and rimed with white frost, grave goods of amber beads and bone amulets at his neck, a frozen hide shroud over one shoulder. His GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD is always on him, the snarling wolf's head on top of his own and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. His face never moves, whatever he is doing: no snarl, no smile, no anger, mouth a flat line in the beard, his yellow-grey eyes level and unblinking like a wolf watching the weakest of a herd. Pose, light and setting: Slowly closing and opening his left hand before his face, testing grey fingers not quite alive, his right hand braced on the stone lintel of the barrow he has just left; eyes on the hand; frost in his beard and brows; green aurora over a midnight barrow field. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Returned Dead".
