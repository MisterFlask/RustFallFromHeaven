CHARADON, the wolf-father who founded the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old but still hugely powerful; massive and broad, nearly a giant. Weather-beaten pale skin, old pale claw scars across one cheek and both forearms, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. For this portrait he wears a hunter's plain undyed hide coat and leggings, laced with sinew and bleached pale by snow, a collar of grey hare fur, like the clothes he wore as a lost boy. His GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD is always on him, the snarling wolf's head on top of his own and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. His face never moves, whatever he is doing: no snarl, no smile, no anger, mouth a flat line in the beard, his yellow-grey eyes level and unblinking like a wolf watching the weakest of a herd. Pose, light and setting: Notching a tally stick in his left hand with a flint knife in his right, the blade biting into the wood, one notch per survivor; eyes level and empty, straight on the viewer, as if counting you next; a bare hilltop above a hide-tent camp, bleached noon glare. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Blank Approval". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
