FLAUROS, Grand Prince of the vampires and younger brother of Alexis, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; male; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and sleek. A pale, strikingly handsome face, swept-back dark auburn hair, piercing gold-yellow eyes. For this portrait he wears a resplendent crimson silk coat embroidered in gold, a gold-lined crimson cape thrown back over one shoulder, a heavy gold chain of office. His long white FANGS show at the corners of his mouth. He is never in a hurry: heavy-lidded gold eyes, a slow private smile, a loose, lounging ease in every movement. No blood anywhere in the picture.
