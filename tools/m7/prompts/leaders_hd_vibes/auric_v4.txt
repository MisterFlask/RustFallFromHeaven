AURIC ULVIN, the farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair, ice-blue eyes too still and old for his young face, a farm boy's chapped knuckles. For this portrait he wears a lord's long robe of midnight-blue velvet edged in silver, and below its cuffs the frayed grey prison rags he still keeps knotted at both wrists. A jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE always rises from his brow. Cold follows him: his breath shows white, and frost creeps over whatever he touches. Pose, light and setting: His left fist clenched while his right hand winds a frayed strip of prison rag around that wrist and knots it like a vow, jaw set, eyes level and hard on the viewer; an empty prison cell, its iron door hanging open and frosted, grey evening light through the bars. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Prisoner's Rags".
