VIBE: "Sees Ghosts". Lowering his right hand to let the ghost of a white cat rub against his fingertips, his left hand gripping the bronze arm of his throne; brows drawn up in sorrow, eyes following the drifting dead through the haze of his bronze-and-black throne hall; pale-gold afternoon light on him alone.

CASSIEL, the archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: once an archangel, now wingless and mortal; male; ageless, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair with the first grey at the temples, deep grey eyes. For this portrait he wears his throne-room robe of plain deep royal-blue wool with a high black collar and bronze clasps, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols anywhere. Fine HAIRLINE CRACKS like those in an old marble statue run across his ivory skin at the temples, throat and backs of the hands, a faint cold white light showing in them. What is left of the angel in him is uncanny, though he has no wings and his eyes are human grey: his face is too symmetrical and too calm for a mortal, like a statue that has learned to breathe; he never blinks; and the air around him is wrong: dust motes, smoke and candle flames near him hang motionless, as if he were the still centre of the room. Pose, light and setting: Lowering his right hand to let the ghost of a white cat rub against his fingertips, his left hand gripping the bronze arm of his throne; brows drawn up in sorrow, eyes following the drifting dead through the haze of his bronze-and-black throne hall; pale-gold afternoon light on him alone. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Sees Ghosts".
