CASSIEL, the archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: once an archangel, now wingless and mortal; male; ageless, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair with the first grey at the temples, deep grey eyes. For this portrait he wears his throne-room robe of plain deep royal-blue wool with a high black collar and bronze clasps, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols anywhere. Fine HAIRLINE CRACKS like those in an old marble statue run across his ivory skin at the temples, throat and backs of the hands, a faint cold white light showing in them. What is left of the archangel in him shows, and it is uncanny: his face is perfectly symmetrical and still as carved marble, fine hairline cracks like those in old marble run from his temples into his hairline, a faint pale-gold light glows under his skin at the throat and wrists, his grey eyes never blink, and the air around him is wrong: dust motes, smoke and candle flames near him hang motionless, as if he were the still centre of the room.
