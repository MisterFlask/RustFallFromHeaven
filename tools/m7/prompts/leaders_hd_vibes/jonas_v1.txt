JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess Bhall, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green battle-scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, heavy brows over amber eyes. For this portrait he wears the vestments of Bhall's first priest: a long ember-red hide robe painted with black flame symbols, a collar of fire-blackened bone beads, bare iron bracers. The broad dark BURN SCAR across the left side of his face and neck, from the sacred fire of Braduk he walked into at his goddess's command, is always plainly visible. No skulls and no blood anywhere in the picture.
