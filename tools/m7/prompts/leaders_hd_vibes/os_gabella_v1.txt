VIBE: "Open Carriage". Slouched in the corner of her black carriage, elbow on the open window, chin propped on her hand, eyes half-closed; Galveholm's crowds scrambling out of the way outside, unnoticed; harsh dusty noon.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall. Pale skin gone dull with tiredness, long black hair hanging limp and loose with a few strands across her face, dark violet eyes with deep shadows under them. For this portrait she wears a dusty black silk travelling dress, a veil pushed back from her face, her gloves pulled off and dropped in her lap. Her pale white glowing JEWELS are always on her, set into whatever she wears (collar, girdle, rings). She is weary beyond anything mortal: alive since the first morning of the world and bored by all of it, and it shows in her whole body: shoulders dropped, weight leaning on what is beside her, eyelids half-closed, mouth slack, no tension anywhere. This weariness overrides any drama in the style: no heroic pose, no wind in her hair, no triumphant light, no rim light. Pose, light and setting: Slouched in the corner of her black carriage, elbow on the open window, chin propped on her hand, eyes half-closed; Galveholm's crowds scrambling out of the way outside, unnoticed; harsh dusty noon. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Open Carriage".
