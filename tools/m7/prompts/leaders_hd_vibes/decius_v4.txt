DECIUS, the exiled soldier who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Sun- and wind-weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. For this portrait he wears his worn fine steel plate with silver pauldrons and gold trim, buckled on even indoors at midnight, under a heavy deep-crimson cloak. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN sits tipped askew on his brow. His pale eyes are always tired and measuring, lids a little lowered and one eyebrow faintly raised: a man betrayed by every friend he has had, who expects you to be next.
