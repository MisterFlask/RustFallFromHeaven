CHARADON, the wolf-father who founded the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old but still hugely powerful; massive and broad, nearly a giant. Weather-beaten pale skin, old pale claw scars across one cheek and both forearms, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. For this portrait he wears a sleeveless hauberk of heavy blackened iron rings over oxblood leather, his huge scarred arms bare, thick iron torcs on his upper arms, a belt of wolf teeth. His GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD is always on him, the snarling wolf's head on top of his own and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. His face never moves, whatever he is doing: no snarl, no smile, no anger, mouth a flat line in the beard, his yellow-grey eyes level and unblinking like a wolf watching the weakest of a herd.
