MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. For this portrait she wears an undyed oatmeal wool work tunic with sleeves rolled to the elbows, a sheepskin vest and a leather apron, her braids tied up out of the way. A single large BLACK BEAR CLAW on a rawhide cord hangs at her throat, whatever she wears.
