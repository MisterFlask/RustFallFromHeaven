His left fingertips on the knot of his blindfold, tightening it, his right hand counting a string of bone beads, one for each of your dead; lips moving, blindfolded face turned straight at the viewer; the rim of the well of the dead at midnight, pale green ghosts streaming past.

TEBRYN ARBANDI, the runecaster who returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; looks about fifty; gaunt and hollow-cheeked. Ashen skin with old pale scars, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard, long bony fingers with hooked, ragged nails. For this portrait he wears his runecaster's robes of black and crimson, the hood thrown back, a high collar of dark iron, a belt hung with carved bone rune tablets. A black cloth BLINDFOLD STITCHED WITH SILVER RUNES is always tied over his eyes; through it he sees the spirits of the dead. His eyes are never seen: the blindfold covers them completely in every portrait, and where he looks is simply where his blindfolded face is turned. Pose, light and setting: His left fingertips on the knot of his blindfold, tightening it, his right hand counting a string of bone beads, one for each of your dead; lips moving, blindfolded face turned straight at the viewer; the rim of the well of the dead at midnight, pale green ghosts streaming past. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Blindfold Sight".
