RHOANNA, the Hippus Queen who united the warring horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her late twenties; tall and straight-backed, a born rider, one shoulder held a little stiff and low from an old wound. Sun-browned, wind-weathered skin, a strong jaw, a long thick chestnut braid, clear green eyes under straight dark brows. For this portrait she wears her family's whole heirloom armour of burnished bronze scale plates, too broad in the shoulders for her, over a burnt-orange quilted gambeson, a rust-brown cloak and a curved horseman's sword. Whatever else she wears, one broad BRONZE PAULDRON from her family's heirloom armour, a size too big and burnished bright, is strapped over her stiff shoulder.
