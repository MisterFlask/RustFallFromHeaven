VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-seeing sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, a long straight nose, fine lines at the eyes and mouth, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. For this portrait she wears a seer's loose robe of pale silver-grey silk, its long sleeves fallen back from her wrists, a hood of sheer grey gauze lying around her shoulders. On her brow, whatever else she wears, a slim silver circlet holds a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE. Her face gives almost nothing away: brows level, mouth closed and even, her head held very straight. Pose, light and setting: Her right hand writing with a silver stylus in a small book held open in her left, never looking down; the stone on her brow blazing violet, eyes wide and unfocused on a far point past the viewer; a tower roof at midnight under a sky thick with stars. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Seeing Stone". The character (not the background) is drawn as strongly chunky retro pixel art: very large square pixel blocks on the figure, only about 64 blocks across the whole picture, the face built from a few big clear blocks; a thick, solid, pure black outline three blocks wide around the entire silhouette, and bold black lines on every main shape inside it (eyes, nose, mouth, hands, folds and edges of the costume); flat colour areas with two or three shading steps, like a 16-bit sprite enlarged. The background stays soft, painterly and much less pixelated, so the figure stands out from it like a cut-out.
