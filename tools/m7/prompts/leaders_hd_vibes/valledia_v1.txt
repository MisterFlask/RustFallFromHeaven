VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-seeing sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, a long straight nose, fine lines at the eyes and mouth, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. For this portrait she wears a seer's loose robe of pale silver-grey silk, its long sleeves fallen back from her wrists, a hood of sheer grey gauze lying around her shoulders. On her brow, whatever else she wears, a slim silver circlet holds a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE. Her face gives almost nothing away: brows level, mouth closed and even, her head held very straight. Pose, light and setting: Her right hand writing with a silver stylus in a small book held open in her left, never looking down; the stone on her brow blazing violet, eyes wide and unfocused on a far point past the viewer; a tower roof at midnight under a sky thick with stars. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Seeing Stone".
