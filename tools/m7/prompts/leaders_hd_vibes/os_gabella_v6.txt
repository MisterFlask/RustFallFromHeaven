OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall. Pale skin gone dull with tiredness, long black hair hanging limp and loose with a few strands fallen across her face, dark violet eyes with deep shadows under them. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour, the armour scuffed and the jewels dimmed. She is weary beyond anything mortal: she has been alive since the first morning of the world, and it shows in her whole body: shoulders dropped, weight leaning on what is beside her, eyelids half-closed, mouth slack, no tension anywhere. This weariness overrides any drama in the style: no heroic pose, no wind in her hair, no triumphant light, no rim light. Pose, light and setting: she sits slumped on worn throne-room steps, slowly setting a gold crown down on the stone beside her with tired fingers; her head is tipped back against a pillar, her eyes half-open on the ceiling, her mouth slack. Low, colourless late-afternoon light, dust hanging in the air. An empty throne hall, the black throne behind her, rain on high windows. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Another Crown". Colours: dust grey, black, tarnished gold, faded crimson.
