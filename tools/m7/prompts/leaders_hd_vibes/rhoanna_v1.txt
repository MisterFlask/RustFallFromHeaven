RHOANNA, the Hippus Queen who united the warring horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her late twenties; tall and straight-backed, a born rider, one shoulder held a little stiff and low from an old wound. Sun-browned, wind-weathered skin, a strong jaw, a long thick chestnut braid, clear green eyes under straight dark brows. For this portrait she wears a chieftain's long wine-red felt coat embroidered at the hems with galloping gold horses, a cream silk shirt, a heavy belt of silver plaques, red cord wound through her braid. Whatever else she wears, one broad BRONZE PAULDRON from her family's heirloom armour, a size too big and burnished bright, is strapped over her stiff shoulder.
