ARTURUS THORNE, the old dwarf king of the deep halls, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a broad nose, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes, broad scarred hands. For this portrait he wears a stone-carver's heavy brown leather apron over a rolled-sleeve shirt of undyed grey linen, stone dust in the folds, his beard braids tucked into the apron strap. His squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points is always on his head. He is planted like the mountain he swears by: his weight set solid on the stone under him, never leaning or reaching.
