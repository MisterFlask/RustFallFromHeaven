CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty-five; tall and strong. Fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, a strong square jaw, dark chestnut hair, grey eyes, two fine lines of strain between the brows. For this portrait she wears a long mail hauberk under a crimson surcoat with a white cross, a steel gorget, a grey fur-lined field cloak thrown back from one shoulder. Her long thick CHESTNUT BRAID always hangs forward over one shoulder. She holds herself like a soldier on parade whatever she feels: back straight, chin level.
