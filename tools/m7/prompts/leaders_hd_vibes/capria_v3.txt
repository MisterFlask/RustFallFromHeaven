CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty-five; tall and strong. Fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, a strong square jaw, dark chestnut hair, grey eyes, two fine lines of strain between the brows. For this portrait she wears a long mail hauberk under a crimson surcoat with a white cross, a steel gorget, a grey fur-lined field cloak thrown back from one shoulder. Her long thick CHESTNUT BRAID always hangs forward over one shoulder. She holds herself like a soldier on parade whatever she feels: back straight, chin level. Pose, light and setting: Her right hand jabbing a rolled battle map toward a flank off frame, her left gripping the pommel of her sheathed sword, mouth wide on a barked order, eyes cut to the side; a windswept ridge at white noon, an army in drilled ranks below under crimson-and-white banners. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Iron Commander".
