VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-seeing sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, a long straight nose, fine lines at the eyes and mouth, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. For this portrait she wears the formal robes of the first archmage of the Amurites: deep crimson velvet with wide sleeves, borders embroidered with gold runes, a tall stiff collar. On her brow, whatever else she wears, a slim silver circlet holds a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE. Her face gives almost nothing away: brows level, mouth closed and even, her head held very straight. Pose, light and setting: Holding a sphere of violet light in each hand at equal height, a tiny burning city inside the left, the same city at peace inside the right; eyes lowered to the burning one; the domed hall of her mage school at noon, a shaft of sun from above. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Futures Weighed".
