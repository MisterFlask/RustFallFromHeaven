THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc, goblin and ogre hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old pale scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, a flattened nose, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. For this portrait he wears the same crude gear as the thousands behind him: a patched brown hide jerkin, rusty iron arm-rings, a ragged dull-red war cloth knotted at his waist. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL is always on his head, worn as a helmet. No blood anywhere on him, his gear or his weapons. Pose, light and setting: Chanting, mouth wide, his right fist pounding a hide war drum at his hip with a knotted stick, his left gripping the shoulder of a goblin beside him, eyes straight on the viewer; a misty valley at grey dawn, a thousand goblins and ogres massing in the mist behind him. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Nameless Horde".
