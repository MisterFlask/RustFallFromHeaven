AURIC ULVIN, the farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair, ice-blue eyes too still and old for his young face, a farm boy's chapped knuckles. For this portrait he wears a farm boy's patched brown homespun shirt and sheepskin jerkin, the left half already crusted white with ice and hung with icicles. A jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE always rises from his brow. Cold follows him: his breath shows white, and frost creeps over whatever he touches. Pose, light and setting: His right hand raised before his face, turning as he watches frost climb from the fingertips up his wrist, his left fist closed on ears of wheat freezing white, lips parted; one eye still blue, one gone white; the dead god's ruined ice temple under a green aurora at night. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Becoming God".
