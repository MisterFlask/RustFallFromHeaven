VIBE: "Tide Vessel". Lowering a lantern into the black water as if feeding the deep, without looking at it.

HANNAH THE IRIN, the mutineer pirate queen born of a cultist dreamer, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; female; about thirty; wiry. Wind-burned olive skin, salt-crusted black hair hanging wet and heavy, pale grey eyes like worn sea glass with no light in them. A weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons, a sea-blue sash, and a NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord. She is hollow, and something else looks out through her: her face is completely relaxed and empty, no smile, no frown, brows and mouth slack; her eyes have no catchlight, are slightly unfocused and look a little past the viewer at nothing; she does not blink. She is perfectly still while everything around her moves. This emptiness overrides any drama in the style: her face shows no feeling at all. Pose, light and setting: she stands at the end of a half-drowned pier, lowering a lantern on a rope toward the black water, her hand steady, her blank face turned toward the open sea and her eyes on nothing; the lantern's light falls on her face and does not reach her eyes. Grey pre-dawn fog, a sea gone unnaturally flat and still, small boats tilted on the mud. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Tide Vessel". Colours: grey-green, navy, bone white, lantern amber.
