AURIC ULVIN, the farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair, ice-blue eyes too still and old for his young face, a farm boy's chapped knuckles. For this portrait he wears armour of overlapping pale blue ice-like scales over grey mail, a long storm-grey cloak torn ragged by the wind, a silver gorget. A jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE always rises from his brow. Cold follows him: his breath shows white, and frost creeps over whatever he touches. Pose, light and setting: His arms spread wide, both fists clenched hard, dragging the blizzard down onto the land, chin raised, face calm and unsmiling, eyes lifted to the white sky as the storm answers him; a high ridge at midday, the world white to every horizon, flat glaring snow-light. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Endless Winter".
