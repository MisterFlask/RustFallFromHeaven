RHOANNA, the Hippus Queen who united the warring horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her late twenties; tall and straight-backed, a born rider, one shoulder held a little stiff and low from an old wound. Sun-browned, wind-weathered skin, a strong jaw, a long thick chestnut braid, clear green eyes under straight dark brows. For this portrait she wears a riding coat sewn from the felts of many tribes, each panel a different tribe's colour of red, ochre, blue or green, over a dark leather jerkin, horsehair tassels at the shoulders. Whatever else she wears, one broad BRONZE PAULDRON from her family's heirloom armour, a size too big and burnished bright, is strapped over her stiff shoulder. Pose, light and setting: Alone before a hundred horsetail standards, both hands knotting one more tribe's coloured ribbon onto the standard pole held in the crook of her arm, already thick with ribbons; a small closed-mouth smile, chin raised, eyes past the viewer on the horizon; open steppe at golden sunset, long shadows. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "United Tribes".
