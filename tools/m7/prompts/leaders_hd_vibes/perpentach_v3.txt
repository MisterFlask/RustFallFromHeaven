His right hand lowering the porcelain mask from his face, his left hand pulling a curtain aside; beneath, his face painted as another mask, white greasepaint, black diamonds over the eyes, a red smile wider than his own; eyes straight on the viewer; a candlelit masked ball at midnight.

PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though centuries old; lean and wiry, with long restless fingers. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. For this portrait he wears a masked-ball costume of black-and-silver brocade: a long fitted coat, a black domino cape, silver diamonds down the sleeves. His smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK, one black tear painted under its eye, is always with him, pushed up on his forehead or in his hand. Pose, light and setting: His right hand lowering the porcelain mask from his face, his left hand pulling a curtain aside; beneath, his face painted as another mask, white greasepaint, black diamonds over the eyes, a red smile wider than his own; eyes straight on the viewer; a candlelit masked ball at midnight. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Mask Under Mask".
