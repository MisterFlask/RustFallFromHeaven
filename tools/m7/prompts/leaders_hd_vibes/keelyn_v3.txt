KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king Perpentach and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight, a child's build. A pale, freckled, round little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. For this portrait she wears a plain black pinafore smock dusted all over with chalk, over a white blouse with sleeves pushed up, her hair bow knocked askew. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, Puppy, a matching rose-pink bow tied on one of its horns, is always with her. She is a child and drawn as one: a child's round face and small hands, modest childish clothes, nothing grown-up or glamorous; sweet and a little eerie, her big amber eyes hardly ever blinking. Pose, light and setting: Kneeling on the floor, her right hand chalking the last rune of a small summoning circle starting to glow, her left hand holding open a spellbook; a wide delighted grin, eyes on the circle; a dusty attic at night, lit violet from below, Puppy peering over her shoulder. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Chalk Circle".
