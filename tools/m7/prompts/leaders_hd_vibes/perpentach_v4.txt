PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though centuries old; lean and wiry, with long restless fingers. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. For this portrait he wears a mock general's sky-blue velvet coat with absurdly huge gold epaulettes, a sash pinned with tin medals, and a tall plumed helmet hung with little bells. His smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK, one black tear painted under its eye, is always with him, pushed up on his forehead or in his hand. Pose, light and setting: Both hands clapping together at his chest like a delighted child, a grin splitting his face, eyes following a cow sailing in a high arc from his catapult over the enemy walls; a siege camp on a hill at bright blue noon. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Flying Cows".
