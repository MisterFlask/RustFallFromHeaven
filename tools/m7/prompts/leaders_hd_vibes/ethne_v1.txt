ETHNE THE WHITE, the young queen raised never to see suffering, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair, large clear blue eyes. For this portrait she wears a plain dove-grey wool travelling cloak, hood down, over a simple white dress, her sturdy boots and hem muddied by the torn-up earth. SPLENDOR, her lame BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT, missing one claw and standing on one leg, is always with her, on her shoulder, on her hand or close by. However much she feels, and it shows openly in her face, her eyes stay clear and steady and she never looks away. Pose, light and setting: Pulling the last torn branches of the uprooted palace hedge aside with both hands, brows drawn up, eyes steady past the viewer's shoulder on the crowded, muddy poor quarter below; Splendor on her shoulder; raw stumps and torn earth; hazy late afternoon, a low sun through chimney smoke. Everything in the picture (the expression, where the eyes look, the hands, the light, the colours and the background) says "Eyes Open".
