THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. Black iron armour with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin, a cord of bone charms across his chest, a heavy cleaver at his belt, and a horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. Turned three-quarters, he leans forward out of the dark, head low, grinning wide with hungry, patient delight, the tiny warm windows of a city reflected in his eyes. A torch raised at his chest throws hot orange light up under his tusks; everything else is cold, moonless blue. Setting: a pine ridge at night; behind him, among the black trees, torch after torch is being lit as goblins and ogres gather; far below in the valley a walled human city glows small and warm under a starless sky. Colours: iron black, bone, torch orange, night blue.
