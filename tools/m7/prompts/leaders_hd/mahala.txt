MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. A black leather jerkin laced with rawhide, a charcoal hood half raised, and a heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw. Turned three-quarters, her eyes cut sharply sideways back toward the hall she is leaving, cheeks flushed, lips pressed in a tight, secret, triumphant smile; the bone hilt of a knife peeks from her sleeve at her chest. Light: deep blue dusk, first snowflakes drifting, a narrow slash of orange firelight from a door left ajar across her eyes. Setting: the rough carved timber wall of a great hall with wolf-skull gables at one side, a saddled horse steaming in the cold behind her; beyond, a Doviello camp of longhouses and hide tents among black pines, fires glowing, snowy mountains under a deep blue sky. Colours: black, charcoal, auburn, firelight orange, dusk blue.
