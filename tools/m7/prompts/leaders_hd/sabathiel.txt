VIBE: the angel who knew your failure before you walked in, and forgives it in the most crushing way possible: he is not angry, he simply expected too much of a mere human.

SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. Polished white-and-silver plate over a long white robe, bright gold at the collar, a crimson-and-white tabard, and great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded high behind him. His body is turned away toward a window and his head turned back over one shoulder to look down at the viewer: brows level and calm, eyelids half-lowered, mouth a small, sad, downturned line of patient disappointment, gentler and worse than anger. One gauntleted hand rests on the window sill. The light is his own cold silver glow and the windows. Setting: a high vaulted chamber of white stone at the top of a cathedral tower, tall arched windows on every side, each showing a different land far below, snowfields, green pastures, desert, the white walls and bell towers of the Bannor capital with its crimson banners, under a pale, wide sky. Colours: white, silver, gold, crimson.
