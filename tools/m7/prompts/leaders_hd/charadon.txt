VIBE: the lost boy who watched, expressionless from a hillside, as the wolves he had led home culled his own family, now an old man called back from the dead and still wearing that same blank, approving stare.

CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred. Weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. Black-dyed leather and dark iron scales with charcoal fur, a belt of wolf teeth, and a GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. Turned three-quarters, he looks down at you as a wolf watches a straggler: face utterly empty, no snarl, no anger, mouth a flat line, eyes level and unblinking. One heavy hand rests on the head of a grey wolf at his side. Light: flat, bleached, shadowless winter daylight drains almost every colour; snow glare whitens his face. Setting: a windswept hilltop with a leaning standing stone hung with skulls; far below, a tribal camp of hide tents and longhouses scattered in the snow, thin smoke rising, grey wolves loping between them; beyond, endless black pine forest under a white sky. Colours: charcoal, wolf grey, bone, snow white.
