VIBE: the clansman who pried open the forbidden stone door into the dreaded Age of Ice and found soft grass and a mild, starry night: three hundred years of stone ceiling lifting off his heart in a single breath.

KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; stocky and solid as a cask. A ruddy face, thick copper-red hair tied back, a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. A plain ochre-gold travelling coat over a brown leather jerkin, a merchant's purse and a brass lantern at his belt. Turned three-quarters, his head tipped far back to the open sky, hazel eyes wide and shining wet, mouth open in a breathless, disbelieving laugh, one hand raised palm-up as if to feel the mild night air. Cool silver moonlight falls full on his upturned face; a thin line of warm candle-gold leaks from the heavy carved stone door ajar in the hillside behind him. Setting: silver-green grass rolling down to a valley of moonlit forest and a silver river, distant mountains, and an enormous indigo sky thick with stars and the glowing band of the galaxy. Colours: ochre gold, copper, moon silver, indigo.
