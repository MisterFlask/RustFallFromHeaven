VIBE: the lonely little princess whose only playmates were summoned demons that vanished every year, until she learned to summon them herself: now she hugs her imp tight and dares anyone to make it leave.

KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight. A pale, freckled little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. A gold-and-black harlequin dress with a high ruffled gold collar. Both arms are wrapped tight around a small grinning red bat-winged IMP hugged against her cheek, a matching rose-pink bow tied on one of its horns. Turned three-quarters, her eyes fixed on you, round and unblinking, above a sweet smile with a stubborn, possessive edge: mine, and staying. A soft violet glow from a summoning circle on the floor lights both faces from beneath. Setting: a playroom of the carnival palace strung with gold party lanterns, a marionette and a toy theatre at one side; in the shadow behind her a huge horned demon waits patiently, a pink bow on its horn too; through a tall window, the striped tents and lantern towers of the Balseraph carnival city under a violet night. Colours: gold, black, rose pink, violet.
