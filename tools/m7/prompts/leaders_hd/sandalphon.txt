Unnaturally pale ash-grey skin, short smoke-grey hair swept back, dull grey eyes in which light never glints. A hard, cold white light strikes his face and that hand, suddenly solid, while the rest of him melts into the grey. Setting: a fog-choked lane of a city of tall grey towers and stone bridges, amber street lamps blurred into haloes, a black tunnel mouth at its end; the towers fade upward into mist under a colourless evening sky.
