VIBE: the Laughing Man has just told the best joke of his life and waits, delighted, for you to realise you are the punchline, while the crowd of stolen minds inside his head leans in to watch.

PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds under a purple-and-crimson velvet cape trimmed in gold, a tall pleated white ruff, and a smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Turned three-quarters, he leans toward you, head cocked, eyebrows high, lips pressed shut over a grin about to burst, eyes glittering as they search your face for the moment you understand; one long finger raised at his chest holds the pause. Light: a hard white spotlight from above, warm stage lanterns behind. Setting: the stage of his palace theatre, red velvet curtains at one side and a hanging marionette that wears his face; floating in the air behind him, faint pale ghost-faces of every age, the minds he has swallowed, leaning in with the same expectant grin; beyond the stage arch, a carnival city of striped tents, bunting and lantern towers at dusk. Colours: gold, crimson, purple, porcelain white.
