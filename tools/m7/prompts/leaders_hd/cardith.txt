CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. Royal robes of emerald green with gold trim and a high stiff collar, a jade seal on a gold chain. Turned three-quarters, he looks up at the viewer with a sweet, warm smile, head tilted slightly, while at chest height his small hand sets a carved jade chess knight on the board beside him without a glance. Light: clear, cool, innocent morning brightness. Setting: a marble loggia under green-tiled domes, the last pieces of a lost game on the board, a tall centaur guard in green and gold watching uneasily from between the columns; beyond the balustrade the immense Kuriotate capital spreads to the horizon, green domes and gold roofs without end, under a clean pale sky. Colours: emerald, jade, gold, white marble.
