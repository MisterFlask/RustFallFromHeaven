VIBE: the girl so afraid of dying that she became death's most patient student: leaning close by candlelight to watch your face for the moment the life leaves it, centuries on and still unable to see where it goes.

ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. A crimson velvet gown with a high standing collar of black lace, a long black velvet mantle trimmed in silver, a ruby brooch at her throat, and a slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES. Turned three-quarters, she leans toward the viewer, head tilted, studying your face with the absorbed, unblinking attention of a naturalist examining a moth: brows faintly drawn, lips parted, no cruelty in her, only cold curiosity. One pale hand lifts a single candle toward you; its amber light falls on her face from below. Setting: a gallery of her palace library, tall shelves of plundered books and a black iron candelabrum at one side; behind her a tall gothic window opens on the Calabim capital, black spires and flying buttresses under a dusk sky of deep crimson and violet, a pale moon rising. Colours: crimson, black, silver, candle amber.
