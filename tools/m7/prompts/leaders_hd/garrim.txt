GARRIM GYR, master enchanter and binder of spells, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; elderly; small, stooped and wiry. Ruddy, wrinkled skin, a big round nose, a long braided snow-white beard, bushy white eyebrows, sharp blue eyes behind small round brass spectacles. Ash-grey enchanter's robes embroidered in charcoal and silver, a leather apron of small tools, and beside his shoulder a brass STAFF HEAD of interlocking COGWHEELS. Turned three-quarters, he leans close over a glittering crystal held at chest height on a folded cloth, never bare-handed; eyes wide, full of its light, brows lifted in helpless fascination, while his mouth is pulled down tight with grief and dread, as if he hears it whispering. The crystal sparkles red, violet and deep blue, lighting his face from below; thin dark mist curls at its edges. Setting: his workshop, shelves of brass instruments and half-built clockwork at the sides; through a great round window behind him, the Luchuirp foundry halls glow orange inside a mountain, and huge stone-and-brass golems stand silent at their forges. Colours: ash grey, brass, charcoal, crystal red and violet.
