VIBE: the first woman, regarding you exactly as she regarded the god sent to claim her on the first morning of the world: one hand raised to halt you, calm as a lioness, faintly amused, the other hidden behind her back.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair stirring in the wind, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour. Half turned away, she looks back at you over her shoulder with the calm, dangerous patience of a lioness surveying her land and the small smile of one who knows how this ends; one gauntleted hand lifted at her chest, palm out: stop. Light: the strange bright light before rain, low gold sunlight slanting under a slate-dark storm sky, full on her face. Setting: a lone ancient tree at one side, its leaves turned skyward; behind her, wide untouched grassland bends in the wind to the edge of the world, a river shining, distant mountains, and on the horizon a single shaft of white divine light falling from the clouds. Colours: black, crimson, storm slate, gold.
