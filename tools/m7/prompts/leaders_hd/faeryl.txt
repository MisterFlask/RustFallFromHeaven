FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and silver spiderweb embroidery, a mantle of black fur, and a tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters toward someone across the table off to one side, she smiles with perfect courtly warmth, head graciously inclined, but her eyes are cold and still, and a tiny tightness at one corner of the smile betrays her pleasure in the thought behind it. One hand raises a slender glass of pale wine in a toast, knuckles white on the stem. Her other hand rests on the white tablecloth, and under its long fingers lies a slim silver STILETTO, mostly hidden by the black lace of her cuff: only its needle tip and a small spider-shaped pommel catch the candlelight. It lies flat and still, pointing at no one; no blood, no threat to the viewer, only the secret. Light: warm candlelight on her face from below, cold blue dusk rimming her hair and crown. Setting: a long dinner table with silver candelabra at one side; behind her a tall arched window of the winter palace frames early snow falling over a dark elven city of black trees and pale silver lights at dusk. Colours: deep purple, black, silver, ice blue.
