VIBE: the far-seeing queen alone before dawn, holding the painted seashell that proves the murder she ordered: no tears, no excuses, only the level acceptance that her hands are as bloody as the hireling's who did it, because she has foreseen what it will save.

VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. A high-collared deep purple robe with lilac lining and silver piping, a silver chain belt, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. Turned three-quarters, a small, delicately painted seashell lies in her open palm at her chest; she does not look at it. Her gaze goes past you into the far distance, face composed and unreadable, head inclined in the barest nod. Her face is split evenly in two: one half lit red-orange by a hanging bronze brazier, the other by the cold violet-grey of the hour before dawn. Setting: a tower chamber, the bronze brazier hanging at one side, star charts on the wall; through the tall arched window behind her, the Amurite capital's slender violet spires and rune-ringed towers still dark above a lake, the night only beginning to pale on the horizon. Colours: deep purple, lilac, silver, brazier orange.
