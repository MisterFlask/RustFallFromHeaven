ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows. Travel-dusted steel pauldrons over a khaki-gold surcoat, a broad belt with a gold buckle, and a squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Turned three-quarters, head bowed a little, gruff brows knotted, his deep-set brown eyes lift to meet yours, ashamed and hopeful, mouth pressed tight in the beard as if words have failed him. One broad, scarred hand rests on a tall stone pillar beside him, carved with weathered bands of tiny figures, its travelling tarp folded back. He stands in the cool blue shadow of a colossal gate's outer arch; behind him the gate opens on a dwarven city carved into the mountain, terraces, brass lamps and great stone halls glowing in warm late sunlight, merchants and laden ponies streaming in past him, snowy peaks above. Colours: gold, bronze, iron grey, warm stone.
