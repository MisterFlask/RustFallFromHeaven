VIBE: the archangel turned weary mortal, the only one in his throne room who can see the murdered girl's ghost, leaning toward her with her own father's grief in his eyes, pleading and commanding at once that she open her eyes.

CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, deep grey eyes. A plain deep royal-blue robe with a high black collar and bronze clasps, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols. Turned three-quarters, he leans forward from his throne toward someone just in front of the viewer, brows drawn up in the middle with a father's sorrow, lips parted as he speaks, one hand lifted at chest height, palm open, urging her to wake. Light: a clear, soft pale-gold daylight holds only him. Setting: the bronze-and-black throne hall dissolves into drifting grey haze, faint pale figures of other lost souls just visible in it; through its open colonnade, the free Grigori city, bronze domes, adventurers' guild towers and a busy harbour in soft afternoon light. Colours: royal blue, bronze, ivory, pale gold.
