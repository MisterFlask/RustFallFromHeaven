TEBRYN ARBANDI, a runecaster returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about fifty; gaunt, hollow-cheeked. Ashen, scarred skin, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard. Black and crimson robes, hood thrown back, a high collar of dark iron, a belt hung with carved bone tablets, and a black cloth BLINDFOLD stitched with silver thread, tied over his eyes to see the dead. Turned three-quarters, one clawed hand at his chest drags at the air; mouth open in a strained snarl, cords standing in his neck, sweat on his brow: a man holding a door shut against what lies beyond. Pale green-white ghosts stream past him into his hand, lighting him in a cold flicker. Setting: the black stone rim of a vast well on a blasted plain, broken obelisks at the sides; rising from it, too late, a pale figure with dark wings; far behind, a Sheaim planar gate, a ring of black stone filled with green fire, under a starless purple-black sky. Colours: black, crimson, ghost green, iron.
