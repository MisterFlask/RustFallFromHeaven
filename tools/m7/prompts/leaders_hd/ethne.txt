VIBE: the princess raised never to see suffering, now a queen who has had her garden hedges torn out so that she must face her people's misery every morning, and does, steadily, with a heart that still breaks each time.

ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes. A simple white gown with a high collar and silver embroidery, a pale grey shawl. Turned three-quarters, she looks out past the viewer's shoulder at something below her, brows drawn up in open compassion, yet her eyes clear and steady, her chin raised, her small mouth set firm: she will not look away. Both hands at her chest cradle a small BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT standing on one leg, its other claw tucked up, lame. Light: soft, cool, overcast morning, pearl-grey and white, almost shadowless. Setting: a palace garden of white marble fountains and colonnades whose hedges have been ripped out, raw stumps and torn earth opening onto the crowded, muddy rooftops and smoking chimneys of the poor quarter below; beyond it, the white temple domes of the city under a soft grey sky. Colours: white, silver, pearl grey, a touch of parrot blue and yellow.
