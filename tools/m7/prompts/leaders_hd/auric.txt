AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A white wolf-fur cloak over a pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, frayed prisoner's rags still at his neck and wrists, and a jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE rising from his brow. Turned three-quarters, he looks back at you over his shoulder, utterly calm, with the faintest closed smile of someone keeping a secret you will never be told, his eyes too still and old for his young face. One hand hangs open at his side, frost spreading from his fingertips into the air. Setting: a broken stone archway on a glacier pours out a blinding cold white light that rims his hair and leaves his face in soft blue shadow; ghostly men in ancient robes kneel in the snow around it; far behind, the frozen mountain where the god died, a ruined temple of ice on its peak, under a pale green aurora. Colours: ice blue, white, silver, pale green.
