SHEELBA, an orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean. Olive-green skin, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. A huge burnt-orange orc warrior's fur cloak around her shoulders over iron-studded hide armour, and at her throat the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. One fist clutches the lace against her chest. Turned three-quarters, chin lifted, jaw set, small tusks bared in a half-snarl, her red-rimmed eyes glare straight into yours, hurt and burning at once. Cold blue-green moonlight falls on her from one side; the ember glow of a war camp rims the other. Setting: a jungle hilltop, giant ferns and vines at the edges, orc war banners on spears behind her; far off down the hill glow the small warm windows and festival lanterns of a human town with a church spire, celebrating spring. Colours: burnt orange, olive green, lace white, moon blue-green.
