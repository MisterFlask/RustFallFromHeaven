FLAUROS, Grand Prince of the vampires and younger brother of Alexis, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; man; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and sleek. A pale, strikingly handsome face, swept-back dark auburn hair, piercing gold-yellow eyes, long white FANGS just showing in his smile. A high-collared black velvet coat embroidered in dark red, and a loosely tied crimson silk cravat with a ruby pin. Turned three-quarters, he leans lazily on a stone balustrade, eyelids heavy, a slow private smile of anticipation, eyes sliding sideways to the sinking sun as if timing it; one hand dangles a goblet of dark red wine over the drop. The last low blood-orange sunlight rakes across his face from the side, half of it already in shadow. Setting: a high balcony of a gothic palace, black stone gargoyles at the corners; far below, a crowd with raised fists fills a plaza, and the long black shadows of the palace spires creep across the cobbles toward them like teeth; beyond, the black-spired Calabim city under a blood-orange sky. Colours: black, crimson, blood orange, gold.
