DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a human soldier who rose to rule other nations; man; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. Worn fine steel plate with silver pauldrons and gold trim under a heavy deep-crimson cloak, and a plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, slouched sideways in a tall dark throne, head tilted back, one eyebrow faintly raised, a dry, unimpressed half-smile, eyes level and tired on the viewer, measuring. One hand holds a wine goblet loosely at chest height, forgotten. Setting: a captured human throne hall at late-summer dusk, dusty amber sunbeams slanting across faded banners of the kings before him; through its tall windows, the city he took, its walls still scarred from the siege, roofs turning rust in the last light. Colours: crimson, steel, gold, rust amber.
