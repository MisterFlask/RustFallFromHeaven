VIBE: pain as prayer: the rebel angel who defied the gods, kneeling alone in falling ash with eyes shut and jaw locked, begging the very gods he abandoned for the strength to finish the war they told him to stop.

BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining. Dented silver-grey plate over a torn white tabard with a gold winged-sun emblem, and battered grey-white WINGS with ragged feathers half spread behind him. Kneeling, turned three-quarters, head bowed forward like a bull's, eyes squeezed shut, brows knotted, lips moving in a fierce silent prayer; both huge fists clasped at his chest around the hilt of a small ceremonial dagger, held upright like a holy relic. Light: flat, ashen twilight, everything grey and white except a dull red glow on one side of his face. Setting: a scorched hill of the Mercurian war camp, grey tents and torn white banners in the falling ash; far behind, the shining white towers of the Mercurian gate city on one horizon and hellfire burning red under a smoke-black sky on the other. Colours: silver, white, ash grey, hell red.
