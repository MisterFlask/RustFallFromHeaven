VIBE: the dwarf who survives out of pure spite: mud-caked in a mad jester-king's cage, a gold coin crushed in his fist, glaring at the laughing court and refusing to die because his death would please them.

BEERI BAWL, survivor and heir of the clan's founders, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and strong. Ruddy weathered skin streaked with drying mud, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two muddy braids, small fierce dark eyes. A torn ash-grey quilted work jerkin, a leather tool harness with brass buckles, and a dented, mud-smeared brass MINER'S HELMET, its little lamp dark. Turned three-quarters, head lowered like a bull about to charge, he glares out at you, brows crushed down, nostrils flared, teeth bared in a snarl of pure defiance; one mud-caked fist held up at his chest, knuckles white around a small gold coin. Light: harsh, flickering torchlight. Setting: thick iron cage bars at both edges of the picture; beyond them a garish carnival throne room, gilded arches hung with masks and pennant bunting, nobles in grinning masks and gaudy saffron, rose and teal costumes laughing and applauding, soft and blurred; through the arches, the striped tents and lantern towers of a carnival city at dusk. Colours: ash grey, copper, brass, saffron and rose.
