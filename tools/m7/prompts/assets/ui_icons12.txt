No outline, or only where two shapes must separate. Palette for the whole set: warm gold, ivory, and one muted accent colour per icon (deep red, teal, sage green, violet, sky blue or terracotta), chosen to read on a deep navy panel. Every icon must still be recognisable at 24 pixels. Arrange them in a 4 x 4 grid: four rows of four icons, each icon centred in its own equal square cell, every icon the same size and filling about 65% of its cell, with wide empty space between neighbours so no two icons touch. No frames, no buttons, plates, badges or circles behind the icons, no shadows, no text, no letters or numbers unless an icon below asks for them, no labels, no grid lines.
