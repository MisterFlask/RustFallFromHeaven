Palette for the whole set: warm gold, ivory, and one muted accent colour per icon (deep red, teal, sage green, violet, sky blue or terracotta), chosen to read on a deep navy panel. Every icon must still be recognisable at 24 pixels. Arrange them in a 4 x 4 grid: four rows of four icons, each icon centred in its own equal square cell, every icon the same size and filling about 65% of its cell, with wide empty space between neighbours so no two icons touch.
