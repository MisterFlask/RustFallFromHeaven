VIBE: at the council of the good nations, the queen who has never told a lie offers a friend her warmth, is answered coldly, and for the first time senses a deceiver at the table.

ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes. A formal high-collared white gown heavy with silver embroidery, a pale grey shawl, and the BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT on the high back of her chair at her shoulder, on one leg, its lame claw tucked up. Turned three-quarters in her council seat, her eyes follow someone walking away along the table to one side, polite smile fading, brows faintly drawn, head tilted, lips parted on an unfinished sentence. One hand is still half raised from the table in a greeting no one took. Light: warm late-afternoon sun through tall lattice windows, honey gold, long shadows across the table. Setting: carved pale wood and sandstone of a round council hall at the edges; in the middle distance, a round table of maps, small delegates, and a raven-haired woman in flowing robes taking her seat between a white-robed elf and a grim soldier; far behind, through the windows, the golden fields and towers of Golden Leane under a hazy amber sky. Colours: honey gold, white, sandstone, soft violet.
