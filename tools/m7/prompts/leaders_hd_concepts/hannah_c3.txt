HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled. Wind-burned olive skin, a wild mane of salt-tangled black hair, stormy sea-grey eyes. The weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons thrown open in the heat over a loose white shirt, a sea-blue sash with a cutlass, and the NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord pale against her sun-dark throat. Pose: turned three-quarters, one forearm leaning on the ship's rail, head cocked, she looks straight at you with steady sea-grey eyes and a slow, lazy half smile; her other hand is held out palm up across the gap between the hulls, fingers curling once: pay. Light: harsh white noon sun overhead, short hard shadows, glitter off the water under her chin. Setting: the black rail and coiled ropes of her ship at one side, a gap of bright turquoise water between two hulls below; in the middle distance, her other pirate ships closing in under full sail; far behind, a Lanun island harbour town of white houses climbing a green hill to a tall lighthouse, under a deep blue cloudless sky. Colours: navy, turquoise, white, sun gold.
