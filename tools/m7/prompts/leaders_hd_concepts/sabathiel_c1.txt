VIBE: at the end of the canyon in hell, wings spread to hold open the gateway of light for his people, the angel looks back at the rearguard still fighting and cannot bear that someone must stay behind to keep it open.

SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair lifting in a hot wind, luminous pale-grey eyes. Scorched white-and-silver plate over a long white robe singed at the hem, bright gold at the collar, a torn crimson-and-white tabard streaked with ash, and his great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS spread wide and blazing with light. His body is turned three-quarters toward the gateway, his head and eyes turned back over one shoulder toward the battle behind him: brows drawn together, mouth open as if to call a name, the calm angelic face cracking with grief. One gauntleted hand stretches back, open, toward the fighting. Light: blinding white-gold from the gateway and his own wings on one side, the red glare of hell on the other. Setting: black volcanic canyon walls at the edges; in the middle distance, ragged families hurrying into the light and a thin line of soldiers holding back shadowy demons and hounds; far behind, a burning plain of ash under a low sky of red cloud and falling cinders. Colours: white, gold, hellfire red, ash black.
