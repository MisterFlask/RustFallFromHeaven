HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled. Light: hard white daylight breaking through a gap in the clouds behind her, a cold rim on her wet hair, green light glowing through the wave crests.
