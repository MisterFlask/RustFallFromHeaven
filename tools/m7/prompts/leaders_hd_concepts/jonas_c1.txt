VIBE: the morning after the endless winter broke over Braduk, the warlord who blasphemed his way to a goddess watches the first sun in living memory melt the snow, and dares, for one moment, to be glad.

JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, amber eyes, and a broad dark BURN SCAR over the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. Dented iron armour with rust-orange trim, a deerskin cloak charred at the edges, a belt of trophies. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tipped back, amber eyes squinting up into the low sun, his mouth loosened into a wary, astonished grin; one big hand raised open beside his face, catching falling meltwater. Light: low gold morning sun on his upturned face, cold blue shadows in the snow. Setting: a snow-laden pine dripping at one side, slush and black earth showing through; in the middle distance, small dark orcs stepping out of hide huts, staring at the sky; far behind, the holy city of Braduk the Burning, half of it a glowing fire pit sending up a column of smoke, under grey cloud breaking open into pale blue. Colours: snow white, rust orange, pale gold, iron.
