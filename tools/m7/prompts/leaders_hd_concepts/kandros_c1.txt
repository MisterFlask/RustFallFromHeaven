KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; stocky and solid as a cask. A ruddy face, hazel eyes, thick copper-red hair tied back, a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. A dusty stone-grey work smock over a brown leather jerkin, a merchant's purse at his belt. Pose: turned three-quarters toward a carved pillar, the fingertips of one hand resting on fresh runes, reading them by touch, a chisel in his fist; his hazel eyes are not on the stone but lifted past it toward the dark ceiling, resolved, jaw set. Light: a cluster of small candles at the foot of the pillar, warm gold from below. Setting: rows of tall stone pillars carved top to bottom with runes, fading into darkness at the sides; in the middle distance, a stone altar with a hammer laid on it and a small old blind Hand Seer reading a pillar with both palms; far behind, a tall shaft cut up through the rock to a small circle of grey winter sky with snow drifting down. Colours: candle gold, granite grey, ochre, deep umber.
