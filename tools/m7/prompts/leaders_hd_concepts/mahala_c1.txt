VIBE: at her first-snow festival the pack howls for the fighters in the bonfire ring, and their chieftain watches with cool eyes, quietly picking out who is strong enough to lead and who will only slow them down.

MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. A black leather jerkin laced with rawhide, the charcoal hood down, and the heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw, snow settling on the fur. Pose: turned three-quarters, her sharp amber eyes sliding sideways toward the contest at one side, cool and measuring, one corner of her mouth faintly lifted, not quite a smile; one hand holds a curved horn cup at her chest, raised but not yet drunk. Light: a huge bonfire throwing orange light on her face from one side, falling snow lit gold around her, black night beyond. Setting: carved timber posts topped with wolf skulls at one side, snow trampled into a ring; in the middle distance, two wrestlers grappling, small and silhouetted against the bonfire, a ring of shouting Doviello with raised fists; far behind, longhouses and hide tents under black pines, the snowy mountains faint under a night sky full of falling snow. Colours: bonfire orange, black, snow white, auburn.
