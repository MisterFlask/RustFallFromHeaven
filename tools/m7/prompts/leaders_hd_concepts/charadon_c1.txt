VIBE: called back from the dead by his shamans, the old wolf takes his first breath in a frozen barrow and, before anything else, studies his own hand for weakness.

CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred. Weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. Black-dyed leather stiff with ice, dark iron scales, a belt of wolf teeth, and the GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its frosted pelt over his shoulders. Turned three-quarters, sitting up from a stone bier, he holds one heavy hand before his face and flexes it, frost cracking off the knuckles, eyes fixed on it, narrowed and cold, testing it like a spear: no wonder, no gratitude. Light: green and violet aurora light pouring through the broken barrow roof, a dull red glow of coals from below. Setting: rough stones and bone charms of a burial barrow at the edges; in the middle distance, hunched shamans in antler headdresses kneeling in shadow round a drum and a ring of coals; through the barrow's mouth, white tundra running to far black mountains under a night sky of rippling aurora. Colours: frost white, aurora green, bone, charcoal.
