VIBE: at dusk on the rim of the sacred fire pit of Braduk, the first priest of Bhall lifts his spear to the goddess who owns his life and his glory, and feels her heat answer him.

JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, amber eyes, and a broad dark BURN SCAR over the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. Dented iron armour with rust-orange trim, the deerskin cloak charred at the edges, a belt of trophies. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the pit, head bowed, his amber eyes raised into the flames, wide with fear and awe; one massive arm holds his spear upright before him, point to the sky, in salute. Light: blazing orange fire from below, hard on his scar, the dusk behind him cold violet. Setting: the scorched black stone rim of the pit at one side, charred spear shafts stuck in its cracks; in the middle distance, a sea of flame and a ring of small kneeling orcs in silhouette; far behind, the broken towers of holy Braduk half swallowed by the fire, under a violet dusk sky heavy with smoke. Colours: fire orange, ember gold, scorched black, dusk violet.
