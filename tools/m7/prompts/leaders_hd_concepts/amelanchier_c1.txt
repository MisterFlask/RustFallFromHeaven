AMELANCHIER, elven ranger-prince, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; male; ageless, looks about thirty; tall and lean. Fair skin, long pale-blond hair tied back, long pointed elf ears, narrow hard green eyes. A leaf-patterned green-and-gold tunic and a leather baldric under a dark-green hooded cloak, a quiver of grey-fletched arrows at his hip, and the gold-tipped top of a tall PALE WOODEN LONGBOW over his shoulder. Turned three-quarters, he looks straight at the viewer, chin level, eyes cold and unblinking, mouth a flat line: no anger in him, only certainty. One hand is raised before his chest, palm flat toward you, fingers together. Light: last violet dusk behind the trees, a cool green glow from the forest on his face. Setting: at the edges black trunks of ancient trees standing close as a wall; in the middle distance a gnarled thorn tree beside the end of a muddy human road, hung with battered iron helmets and a broken sun-painted shield, elven rangers half hidden in the boughs above; far behind, over the treetops, the golden lights of an elven tree-city in a colossal oak, under a violet sky with the first stars. Colours: deep green, violet, iron grey, pale gold.
