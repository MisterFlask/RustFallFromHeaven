VIBE: someone has dared to enter the freezing black tent where he sits possessed, and Auric opens his eyes on the intruder, the dark bursting into blue fire full of armies and dragons, sweat turning to frost on his skin.

AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A pale sky-blue robe edged in silver hanging loose, a white wolf-fur cloak pooled around him, frayed prisoner's rags at his neck and wrists, and his jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE. Seated, turned three-quarters and leaning forward, he looks straight at the viewer with wide, glowing eyes, lips parted on words that will not come, his young face hollow and possessed, beads of sweat frozen white on his brow. One hand grips his knee, frost creeping from his fingers across the robe. Light: blue-white fire pouring from his eyes and the air around him, everything beyond it pitch black. Setting: at the edges the heavy black curtains of the tent's entrance; in the middle distance the tent walls alive with flickering shadows of marching armies, ruined cities and a rearing dragon; far behind, through a slit in the tent, sunlit snow and the grey wall of the frozen mountain. Colours: black, blue fire, frost white, deep violet.
