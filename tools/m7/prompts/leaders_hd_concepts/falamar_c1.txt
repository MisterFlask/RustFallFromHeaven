FALAMAR, the charming pirate Sea-King, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late thirties; lean and graceful. Sun-bronzed skin, black hair in a short queue, a neat pointed black beard, lively dark eyes, a gold earring. His royal-blue captain's coat with gold braid floating open, the white shirt and lace jabot stirring, a sea-blue sash, and the broad BLUE TRICORN HAT WITH A LONG WHITE PLUME, clutched to his chest in one hand, the plume streaming upward. Turned three-quarters, weightless in the water, he looks up at a luminous woman made of water and tide, small in the light high above him; his usual smirk is fading into a sheepish, guilty grimace, brows lifted, head tilted back, silver bubbles rising from his lips. Light: soft shafts of turquoise and white light from far above on his upturned face, deep blue darkness below. Setting: drifting kelp and a broken ship's rail sinking at the edges; in the middle distance, the wreck of his ship settling on white sand among barrels; far below and behind, the glowing domes and spires of an underwater city of the sea god's people. Colours: turquoise, deep blue, pearl white, royal blue.
