CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. Royal robes of emerald green with gold trim and a high stiff collar, a jade seal on a gold chain. Seated on a throne far too big for him, turned three-quarters, he looks straight at the viewer, his soft face perfectly calm, lips parted on a word, the slit pupils narrowed to needles. One small hand is raised, palm down, slowly lowering. Light: a cold narrow beam from a high window across his eyes, the room in deep green shadow. Setting: at the edges the throne's green marble arms; in the middle distance a kneeling nobleman, head bowed, small and in shadow, and a tall centaur guard in green and gold half turned away, hand tight on his spear; far behind, through an arched window, the green domes and gold roofs of the Kuriotate capital under a low storm sky. Colours: dark emerald, gold, jade, storm grey.
