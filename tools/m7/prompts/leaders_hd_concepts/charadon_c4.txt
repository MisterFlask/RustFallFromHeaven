VIBE: the instant Mahala calls him a grey hair, past his prime, a mistake to bring back, and the resurrected wolf goes still while the rage climbs into his face.

CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred. Weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes, a dark red flush climbing his neck into his face. Black-dyed leather, dark iron scales, a belt of wolf teeth, and the GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its pelt over his shoulders. Turned three-quarters in a heavy chair, he stares after someone leaving through a doorway off to one side, eyes wide and fixed, nostrils flared, lip drawn back from clenched teeth in a snarl not yet loosed. One hand grips the chair's carved arm so hard the wood splits. Light: cold grey daylight from the door, dull red from a banked hearth. Setting: soot-black longhouse posts hung with antlers and shields at the edges; in the middle distance, a table of hide maps, and in the bright doorway the small silhouette of a braided woman walking out; far behind her, hide tents under black pines and a low snow sky. Colours: soot black, ember red, iron grey, dull purple.
