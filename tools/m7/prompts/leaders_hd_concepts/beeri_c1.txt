VIBE: home in the mountain at last, Beeri works through the night on a mighty clockwork golem, grinning at the thought of it mashing a certain laughing harlequin underfoot.

BEERI BAWL, survivor and heir of the clan's founders, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and strong. Ruddy weathered skin, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two braids, small fierce dark eyes. A scorched leather apron over an ash-grey quilted work jerkin with rolled sleeves, a leather tool harness with brass buckles, soot on his cheeks, and his dented brass MINER'S HELMET, its little lamp burning bright. Turned three-quarters, he looks straight at the viewer over his shoulder with a slow, grim, satisfied grin, one eye narrowed. One thick hand rests a big brass wrench on his shoulder. Light: hot orange forge glow from one side, the yellow beam of his helmet lamp. Setting: at the edges racks of gears, springs and iron plates; in the middle distance the towering half-built brass-and-iron body of a clockwork golem in scaffolding, its huge foot raised over a painted harlequin mask nailed to a block, small dwarf apprentices on the ladders; far behind, through a great arch, the cavern workshops of the Luchuirp glowing with furnaces and moving golem silhouettes, under a rock ceiling lost in smoke. Colours: brass, forge orange, soot black, copper.
