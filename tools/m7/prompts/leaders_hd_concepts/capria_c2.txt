VIBE: on the wall of the last town before the corrupted lands she watches hellfire burn along the border all night, the demons of her childhood come for her at last, and decides that if the Bannor do not stand, no one will.

CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty-five; tall and strong. Fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, dark chestnut hair in a long thick CHESTNUT BRAID over one shoulder, grey eyes. Soot-dulled steel plate over a white surcoat with a red cross, a mail coif pushed back. Turned three-quarters, she looks out over the wall toward the burning horizon, eyes hard and level, jaw clenched, an old fear mastered into stillness. Both hands rest stacked on the pommel of a long two-handed sword, its point grounded on the stone before her. Light: red-orange glow of distant fires on one side of her face, cold blue night on the other, faint gold blessing-runes along the parapet. Setting: at the edges heavy grey merlons of a small fortress wall; in the middle distance withered black trees and horned shapes moving along a line of fire in the dark, small; far behind, the burning horizon of the infernal lands under a starless smoke-red sky, and on the hills to one side the tiny watchfires of Bannor forts. Colours: ember orange, steel, crimson, night blue.
