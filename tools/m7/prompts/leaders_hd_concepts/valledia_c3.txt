VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely under a thin silver-grey veil, cool grey eyes. A plain high-collared mourning robe of dark grey-purple with lilac lining, a silver chain belt, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. Turned three-quarters, head slightly bowed, she looks straight at you from under level brows, eyes steady and wet, face composed. One hand is pressed flat over her heart. Light: pale white daylight pouring from high abbey windows, cool and clean on her face. Setting: white stone columns and rows of tall candles at the edges; in the middle distance, white-robed monks small in a cloister, one old abbot listening in shadow; far behind, through an open arch, the horizon dark with columns of black smoke rising from burning border cities, under a pale wintry sky. Colours: dove grey, deep purple, abbey white, smoke black.
