VIBE: from the highest tower of his drowned city of the dead, Hyborem sits enthroned above the still red waters, utterly at rest, a king waiting for the whole world to sink down and do him reverence.

HYBOREM, Lord of the Infernals, the demon who broke out of hell to conquer the mortal world, leader of the Infernals in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: demon; male; ageless; towering and hulking, with massive shoulders. Deep crimson skin, burning yellow eyes under a heavy brow, two huge curved black HORNS sweeping up from his brow. Black obsidian spiked armour with gold trim, a tattered dark red cape spilling over the arm of a throne of black stone. Pose: seated, turned three-quarters, one forearm along the throne's arm, the clawed hand hanging loose over its end; his head tilts slightly down and his burning eyes look down and away over the city below, heavy-lidded and patient. Light: none from the sky; a lurid red glow rises from beneath the glassy water up his armour, chin and horns. Setting: the parapet of a tall black tower carved with stone ivy and flowers at one side; in the middle distance, domes, spires and kingly halls standing in perfectly still dark water, their turrets lit red from below; far behind, an open temple yawning level with the waves, its idols' diamond eyes glinting, under a black starless sky with a faint red haze. Colours: blood red, obsidian black, gold, verdigris.
