VIBE: alone on his palace roof after the ghost girl asks whether the dead are doomed to the grey forever, the weary archangel looks up toward the closed doors of the true heaven and smiles, because he believes they will open one day.

CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, deep grey eyes. A plain deep royal-blue robe with a high black collar and bronze clasps, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols, a dark grey cloak. Turned three-quarters, seated on a low parapet, he tips his head back and looks up at the stars past the viewer's shoulder, a small patient smile easing the weariness in his face. One hand rests palm up on his knee, as if waiting to be taken. Light: cold silver starlight on his face, a faint warm glow from the city below along his jaw. Setting: black-and-copper parapets of the palace roof at the edges; at the parapet's far end, small and faint, a translucent girl with a white cat in her lap; below, the lamplit roofs and meeting halls of Midgar; far behind, the tall copper-capped palace towers against a deep night sky thick with stars. Colours: midnight blue, copper, ivory, starlight silver.
