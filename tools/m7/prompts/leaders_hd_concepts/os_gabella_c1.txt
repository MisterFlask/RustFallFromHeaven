VIBE: from the open window of her black carriage at the gates of Galveholm, the Queen of Storms considers you without the least interest or fear, then says two words: get in.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair falling loose over one shoulder, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour. Pose: seated at the carriage window, turned three-quarters, one gauntleted forearm resting along the sill; her dark violet eyes look straight at you, level and dispassionate, the face perfectly still but for the faintest smirk; the fingers of that hand lift idly toward the carriage door. Light: the oppressive white glare of a hot noon, harsh on her face, the carriage black inside behind her. Setting: the lacquered black window frame with iron fittings at one side; in the middle distance, black horses with burning hooves and wolfish teeth stamping in the dust, ragged figures shrinking back from the road; far behind, the dark gates and spiked walls of Galveholm, a column of black smoke rising beside them, under a hazy sulphur-yellow sky. Colours: black, crimson, sulphur yellow, bone white.
