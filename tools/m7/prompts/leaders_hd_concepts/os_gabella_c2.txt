OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair stirring in the hot updraft, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour, the only light on the stair. Pose: turned three-quarters, she holds a clear crystal up between gauntleted finger and thumb at eye level, her dark violet eyes fixed on it, intent, a small unguarded hope at the corners of her mouth. Light: the cold white glow of her jewels on her face, and a black-violet flicker rising from flames below. Setting: rough cavern walls and worn stone steps at the sides, a great vault door standing open; in the middle distance, a stone arch carved with spirals, and beyond it the pit of black flames with taut silver chains running down into it; far behind, through a crack in the cavern roof, the dark towers of Galveholm far above under a storm sky lit by violet lightning. Colours: black, jewel white, violet, deep crimson.
