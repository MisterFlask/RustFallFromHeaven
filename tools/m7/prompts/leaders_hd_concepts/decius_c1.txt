DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; man; ageless, looks a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes. A dark padded arming shirt, one sleeve cut away over a bandaged upper arm, a heavy deep-crimson cloak thrown over his shoulders, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters on the edge of a camp bed, he looks straight at the viewer as at the bound killer, with calm, tired interest, the scarred eyebrow lifted, a thin dry smile, his numbed arm hanging at his side. His good hand beckons with two fingers: come closer. On a folding table beside him lies the assassin's slim stiletto. Light: a guard's torch at one side throws warm orange light across his face; outside, cold blue snow light. Setting: carved tent poles, hanging rugs and tumbled furs of a campaign tent at the edges; in the middle distance, desert soldiers in turbans and mail crowding the tent door; through it, a snowy camp and the jagged peaks of Tempus Mor under falling snow at night. Colours: crimson, torch orange, snow blue, steel.
