DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a human soldier who rose to rule other nations; man; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. A padded arming shirt, one sleeve cut away over a bandaged arm, a deep-crimson cloak over his shoulders, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters on a camp bed, he looks straight at the viewer as at the bound killer, with calm, tired interest, the scarred eyebrow lifted, a thin dry smile. His good hand beckons with two fingers: come closer. On a folding table beside him lies the assassin's stiletto. Light: a guard's torch throws warm orange across his face; outside, cold blue snow light. Setting: tent poles, hanging rugs and tumbled furs at the edges; in the middle distance, desert soldiers in turbans and mail crowding the doorway; through it, a snowy camp and the jagged peaks of Tempus Mor under falling night snow. Colours: crimson, torch orange, snow blue, steel.
