VIBE: from a ridge at grey dawn she watches a raid ride off against a walled town it cannot take, the necessary cull of the headstrong that she will never admit to, and lets nothing show on her face.

MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. A black leather jerkin laced with rawhide, the charcoal hood raised against the wind, and the heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw, frost on the fur. Pose: turned three-quarters, standing very still, her sharp amber eyes following the riders down in the valley, the face composed and unreadable; only her gloved hand, gripping the bear-claw clasp tight, gives her away. Light: cold flat grey dawn, a thin seam of pale pink on the horizon, no warmth on her face. Setting: frozen grass and a wind-bent pine at one side; in the middle distance, a line of young Doviello raiders on horseback trotting down the slope, small and dark, a few waving spears; far behind, a stone-walled town of another people with towers and watchfires on a hill across a frozen river, under a heavy slate sky. Colours: slate grey, pine green, dawn pink, black.
