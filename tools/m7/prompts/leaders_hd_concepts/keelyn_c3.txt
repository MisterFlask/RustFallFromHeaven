VIBE: on the harbour wall at sunset, a griffon feather in her hair and her dress patched from adventures, the princess points out to sea and announces that next she wants one of the giant wiggly beasts.

KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight. A pale, freckled little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. Her gold-and-black harlequin dress patched at the hem, its gold collar crumpled, a striped griffon feather tucked in beside the bow. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, a matching rose-pink bow on one of its horns, sits on her shoulder pointing the same way. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the sea, her large amber eyes wide and fixed far out on the water, mouth open in eager delight; one arm stretched out, pointing at the horizon. Light: low coral-orange sunset on her face, the sea cool teal. Setting: the stone harbour wall with an iron mooring ring and a coiled net at one side; in the middle distance, painted ships with white and purple sails, and a tall wheeled cage being built on the quay; far behind, the lantern towers of Jubilee along the shore, and far out a dark curling tentacle breaking the surface, under a coral and violet sunset sky. Colours: coral, teal, gold, rose pink.
