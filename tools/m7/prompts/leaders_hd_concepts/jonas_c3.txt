VIBE: in the captured hall of a Bannor fortress, the cunning warlord offers you his terms with one fist on the map: kneel to the Clan of Embers now, or burn when the goddess asks for you.

JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, amber eyes, and a broad dark BURN SCAR over the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. Dented iron armour with rust-orange trim, the deerskin cloak charred at the edges, a belt of trophies hung with a dented Bannor helm. Pose: turned three-quarters, leaning over a table, his amber eyes straight on yours, calculating, one corner of his mouth lifted over a tusk; his heavy fist rests on an unrolled hide map, two knuckles down on a city. Light: grey afternoon light from a broken window, smoky orange firelight from the other side. Setting: cracked white-and-red Bannor shields stacked against a pillar at one side; in the middle distance, a doorway onto a courtyard where his warriors stand small in smoke; far behind, the white walls of a Bannor citadel on a far hill, burning in places, under rolling brown smoke. Colours: iron grey, rust orange, smoke brown, Bannor white and red.
