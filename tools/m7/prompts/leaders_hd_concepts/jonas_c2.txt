VIBE: midnight in his war tent, rain drumming on the hides, and the girl's scream the goddess left with him rises in his skull again, so the warlord who fears nothing presses a hand to his ear and waits for it to end.

JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, amber eyes, and a broad dark BURN SCAR over the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. Dented iron armour unbuckled and slipping off one shoulder, the deerskin cloak charred at the edges round his back. Pose: seated, turned three-quarters, hunched forward, his eyes squeezed shut, teeth gritted between his tusks; one huge hand pressed flat over his ear, the scarred side of his face toward the light. Beside him the goddess's small undying flame burns in an iron cage on a post. Light: the flame's harsh white-gold light on his scar, everything else in rain-blue darkness. Setting: three crossed spears and a hide shield leaning at one side; in the middle distance, through the open tent flap, dark tents and a small sentry in the rain; far behind, a jagged black jungle ridge lit for an instant by lightning under a black rain sky. Colours: rain blue, white-gold, hide brown, grey-green.
