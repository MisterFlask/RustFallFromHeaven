VIBE: learning that the young queen has built a gate to call down warriors of heaven, the ancient monk strikes his staff on the infirmary floor and asks his goddess why he has lived so long to see this.

EINION LOGOS, the ancient head of the monastic order, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; very old; lean and upright, a former warrior. Deeply lined tan skin, a long white beard, close-cropped white hair, steady grey eyes. A plain white and ash-grey wool habit with a rope belt, and the small PAINTED SEASHELL on a cord at his chest. Turned three-quarters, he tips his head back and looks upward past the vaulted ceiling, eyes wet and wide with anguish, brows drawn up, mouth open on a prayer. One gnarled hand grips a tall plain wooden staff whose foot has just struck the stone floor. Light: warm amber light from hanging oil lamps on his face, deep blue night in the windows. Setting: vaulted stone arches of a temple infirmary at the edges; in the middle distance, rows of cots with bandaged pilgrims resting, white-robed healers, and, small and in shadow, a slight young woman in white with her head bowed; far behind, through a tall arched window, the torchlit scaffolding of a huge new gate of gold and white stone glowing on a hill under a starless night sky. Colours: amber, white, deep night blue, gold.
