VIBE: in a clearing where two deer lie dead and stained purple by the corruption, the archmage who has never failed sinks down into the ash and understands, all at once, what the only spell strong enough to save the forest will cost.

THESSA, archmage of the elven city of Evermore, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks in her twenties; slender. Pale skin gone grey-white, long black hair tangled from travel, long pointed elf ears, dark green eyes, a single GOLD FEATHER in her hair. A mud-stained dark green travelling cloak over a plain grey-green robe, the hood fallen back. Turned three-quarters, sitting low on the ashen ground, she stares into the middle distance with wide, unfocused eyes, her face drained, one hand pressed hard over her mouth. Light: flat dull grey overcast light, a faint sickly violet sheen on everything near her. Setting: leafless twisted saplings and swollen violet growths at the edges; in the middle distance, two dark violet shapes of fallen deer half-hidden in withered grass; far behind, the deep forest still green and untouched along the horizon, a silver river winding through it, under a low grey sky. Colours: ash grey, corrupted violet, dull green, gold.
