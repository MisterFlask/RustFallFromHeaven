Porcelain-pale skin, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. A severe gown of dark crimson velvet buttoned to the throat, black lace cuffs, a long black velvet mantle trimmed in silver, and her slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES. Seated upright on a tall black throne, turned three-quarters, chin raised, her eyes slide sideways and down toward someone lounging out of the picture, one brow lifted, upper lip curled in a fastidious sneer. One pale hand rests on the throne's arm, a single finger lifted in impatience. Light: grey dawn through tall windows behind half-drawn curtains, cold on her face, a few guttering candles warm at her side. Setting: at one edge the empty twin throne beside hers; in the middle distance a hall of black marble columns and an ambassador's green-and-gold cloak left lying on a bench; far behind, through the tall windows, the spires and flying buttresses of the Calabim capital under a pale grey sky with a thin line of dawn.
