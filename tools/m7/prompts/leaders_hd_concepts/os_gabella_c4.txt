VIBE: hunting alone in the oldest forest of the world, as she did in the first days of creation when she was its greatest predator, the immortal queen catches the scent of her prey and is, for once, simply happy.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair stirring in the wind, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown kilted up for running, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour, faint in the daylight. Pose: turned three-quarters, crouched forward, her head turned sharply aside, dark violet eyes wide and bright, fixed on something in the undergrowth at one side, a fierce, delighted half smile; one gauntleted hand holds a long plain hunting spear upright beside her, point to the sky. Light: green-gold spring sunlight filtering through the leaves in a net of bright spots across her face and armour. Setting: a massive mossy oak trunk and ferns at one side; in the middle distance, a sunlit glade with a white stag half hidden among birches; far behind, a vast hollow tree with an arch of black roots, the shadowed refuge a goddess once gave her, under bright spring clouds seen through the canopy. Colours: forest green, sun gold, black, crimson.
