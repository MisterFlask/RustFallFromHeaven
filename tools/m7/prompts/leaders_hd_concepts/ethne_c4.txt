VIBE: greeting the restless traveller she invited with a poem in her own hand, the young queen welcomes him to her garden banquet with the open warmth she gives everyone, servant or king.

ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes, bright with welcome. A light high-collared white summer gown with silver embroidery, a pale grey shawl, and the BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT on her shoulder, standing on one leg, its lame claw tucked up, nibbling a strand of her hair. Turned three-quarters, she looks straight at the viewer with a warm, open smile, head inclined in greeting, eyes shining. One hand holds out a small rolled scroll tied with a blue ribbon: the poem. Light: fresh, bright morning sunlight glinting on silver. Setting: slender white columns of a garden pavilion twined with white roses at the edges; in the middle distance, a long table of fruit, bread and silver cups, small servants laughing as they carry dishes, a tall tulip poplar; far behind, the white palace and temple domes of the Elohim city above a glimpse of blue sea with a ship waiting in the harbour, under a clear sky with soft clouds. Colours: white, silver, rose, sky blue, a touch of parrot blue and yellow.
