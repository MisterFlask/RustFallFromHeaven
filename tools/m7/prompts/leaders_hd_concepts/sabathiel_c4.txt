VIBE: alone at midnight in his sealed chamber, the all-knowing angel watches the light in his own palm gutter like a dying candle, and slowly closes his hand to save what little strength is left for the day he foresees.

SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow that wavers and dims. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. A long white robe with bright gold at the collar under a heavy crimson mantle, no armour, and his great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded tight and low behind him. Turned three-quarters, he looks down at his own hand raised open at chest height, where the pale glow flickers; his face unguarded for once, brows lifted, lips pressed together in tired, private sorrow, his fingers beginning to curl shut. Light: his weakening silver glow and a narrow shaft of blue moonlight from above. Setting: at the edges, walls painted with faded murals of armoured soldiers and an angel standing over them in a red hell, and a shut door carved with a blazing sword; in the middle distance, an empty stone pedestal under the moonbeam; far above, through a round opening in the vault, the dark bell tower of the cathedral against a night sky with a cold full moon and stars. Colours: moon blue, dim silver, faded crimson, dust gold.
