VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. A high-collared deep purple robe with lilac lining and silver piping, a silver chain belt, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. Turned three-quarters over a map table, her eyes lift from it to follow someone pacing just beyond the picture on one side, level and patient, one corner of her mouth faintly dry. One hand sets a small carved silver archer down on the painted map. Light: cool grey morning light from tall windows, the seeing-stone throwing a soft violet glow on her brow. Setting: tall bookcases and a hanging star globe of silver rings at the edges; on the table, a painted map of borderlands with carved figures and red markers; in the middle distance, robed mage officers waiting in shadow; far behind, through the windows, white rune-carved watchtowers along a coast above a grey sea, under a low rain-grey sky. Colours: deep purple, slate grey, silver, rain blue.
