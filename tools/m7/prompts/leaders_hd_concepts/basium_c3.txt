VIBE: on a high cliff at first light he watches the demon-held land below the way a shepherd watches the forest that hides the wolves, patient for the day the armies of men finally come to it.

BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining, hard pale-grey eyes. A long grey cloak over dented silver-grey plate and a white tabard with a gold winged-sun emblem, and his battered grey-white WINGS with ragged feathers folded close. Turned three-quarters at the cliff's edge, he looks far out to one side over the land, eyes narrowed and unblinking, face still with an ageless patience. One bare hand rests on a tall war hammer planted upright beside him. Light: cold blue before dawn, a thin gold line of sunrise touching his scars. Setting: at the edges wind-bent grass and pale rock; in the middle distance a valley of withered black trees and smoking vents; far behind, across a blighted plain, the twisted black towers and red fumes of the Fane of Lessers, the tiny white sails of arriving fleets on the sea beyond, under a sky turning from deep blue to gold. Colours: slate blue, dawn gold, silver, white.
