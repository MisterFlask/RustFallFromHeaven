VIBE: on a high cliff at first light he watches the demon-held land below the way a shepherd watches the forest that hides the wolves, patient for the day the armies of men finally come to it.

BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining, hard pale-grey eyes. A long grey wool cloak over dented silver-grey plate, a white tabard with a gold winged-sun emblem beneath, and his battered grey-white WINGS with ragged feathers folded close against the wind. Turned three-quarters at the cliff's edge, he looks far out to one side over the land below, eyes narrowed and unblinking, face utterly still: the patience of something that does not age. One bare hand rests on the head of a tall war hammer planted upright beside him. Light: cold blue before dawn, a thin line of gold sunrise touching the scars on his cheek. Setting: at the edges wind-bent grey grass and pale rock; in the middle distance a valley of withered black trees and smoking vents where the corruption begins; far behind, across a blighted plain, the twisted black towers and red fumes of the Fane of Lessers, and on the sea beyond, the tiny white sails of arriving fleets, under a vast clear sky turning from deep blue to gold. Colours: slate blue, dawn gold, silver, white.
