VIBE: the first breath of the mortal world: Hyborem steps out of the torn wall of creation onto frosty farmland at dawn and tastes the air with the slow, delighted hunger of one who has waited an eternity for something new.

HYBOREM, Lord of the Infernals, the demon who broke out of hell to conquer the mortal world, leader of the Infernals in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: demon; male; ageless; towering and hulking, with massive shoulders. Deep crimson skin, burning yellow eyes under a heavy brow, two huge curved black HORNS sweeping up from his brow. Black obsidian spiked armour with gold trim, a tattered dark red cape trailing embers. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the horizon, chin lifted, his burning eyes narrowed on the distant towers of a mortal town, a slow closed-mouth smile; one huge clawed hand raised at his chest, fingers flexing open to feel the cold air. Light: pale gold dawn sun on his face from the front, hellfire from behind rimming his horns in orange. Setting: a frost-white hawthorn hedge at one side, the grass blackening and curling where he stands; in the middle distance, small horned shapes in shadow following him, and the white walls of a human town across a misty river; far behind, the rift he stepped from hanging in the sky like torn cloth, the jagged black spires of hell glowing red inside it, under a pale morning sky with rose clouds. Colours: crimson, frost white, dawn gold, hellfire orange.
