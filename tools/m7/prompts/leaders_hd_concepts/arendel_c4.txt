VIBE: alone in her warded chambers after the coronations, the Queen of Evermore lifts her fingers to the clasp of her amulet with a small private smile the true Arendel, screaming in a silver box beneath the great red oak, never wore.

ARENDEL PHAEDRA, queen of the Seelie court, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks about forty; tall and regal. Fair skin, long wavy copper-auburn hair, long pointed elf ears, soft grey-green eyes. A night robe of deep emerald velvet with gold leaf embroidery, a pendant amulet of green glass on a fine gold chain at her throat, and her delicate GOLDEN CROWN of interlaced LEAVES and branches. Turned three-quarters toward a tall mirror at one side, her eyes slide from it to the viewer as if she has caught you watching; head tilted, a slow, knowing, faintly cruel closed-lip smile, the warmth gone from her eyes. Two fingers of one hand rest on the clasp of the amulet, about to lift it away. Light: a single candle warm on her face, cold blue moonlight from a window behind. Setting: at the edges the carved wooden frame of the mirror and a curtained bed of living branches; in the middle distance a door whose frame glows with small violet ward-runes; far behind, through an arched window, the vast red oak of Evermore black against a moonlit sky, a faint glow at its roots. Colours: emerald, gold, moon blue, violet.
