BEERI BAWL, survivor and heir of the clan's founders, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and strong. Ruddy weathered skin, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two braids, small fierce dark eyes. A fur-collared coat over an ash-grey quilted jerkin, a leather tool harness, a pick over one shoulder, and his dented brass MINER'S HELMET, its little lamp still lit. Turned three-quarters at the lip of the tunnel, he looks up and away at the sky, eyes screwed up against the light, mouth half open, gruffness and wonder fighting on his face. One hand is raised flat above his brows, shading his eyes. Light: warm gold sunrise full on his face, cool blue shadow of the cave behind him. Setting: at the edges a mine mouth with old timber props and dripping icicles; in the middle distance dwarves and tall mud golems carrying tools onto a hillside of melting snow, small; far behind, on distant hills, the overgrown ruins of a great city of the old dwarven empire, toppled golem statues among its towers, under a wide sky of pale gold and blue. Colours: dawn gold, moss green, slate, copper.
