DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; man; ageless, looks a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes. Battered steel plate with silver pauldrons and gold trim, a torn deep-crimson cloak, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, arms folded across his breastplate, chin up, he looks up past the viewer at a high balcony, jaw set, eyes hard, a flat half-smile of contempt: a man with nothing left to lose. Light: violet dusk turning to night, the sickly yellow-green glow of street lamps from below, a red rim of last light on his white hair. Setting: crumbling mud-brick tenements and broken shutters of a slum alley at the edges, overturned carts and dropped spears on the cobbles; in the middle distance, pale servants waiting in dark doorways, and on a carved balcony above the street the small, elegant figure of a vampire lord in black looking down; far behind, the black spires of the palace of Prespur under a violet sky with a thin red moon. Colours: black, crimson, steel, sickly green.
