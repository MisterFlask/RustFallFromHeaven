DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a human soldier who rose to rule other nations; man; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. Battered steel plate with silver pauldrons, a torn deep-crimson cloak, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, arms folded across his breastplate, chin up, he looks up past the viewer at a high balcony, eyes hard, a flat half-smile of contempt: a man with nothing left to lose.
