VIBE: kneeling on the frozen lake in the dragon's cavern, the would-be god of winter leans close to whisper into a dragon's skull, then breathes frost into its bones until they begin to grow flesh again.

AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A white wolf-fur cloak over a pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, frayed prisoner's rags at his neck and wrists, and his jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE. Turned three-quarters, he leans close to the huge ivory dragon skull beside him, eyes lowered to it, an intimate half-smile, white frost-breath flowing from his lips into the dark eye socket. One hand rests flat on the skull's brow, the way a man calms a horse. Light: pale blue glow of ice mana in the cavern walls, brighter white where frost settles on bone. Setting: at the edges walls of glowing blue ice crystal and the curve of giant ribs half sunk in the frozen lake; in the middle distance two cloaked generals in shadow on the ice, small; far behind, a vast shaft rising to a ring of pale sky and the ruined summit of the frozen mountain, snow drifting down. Colours: pale blue, bone ivory, frost white, deep teal.
