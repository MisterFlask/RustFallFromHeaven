FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared deep purple travelling gown with silver spiderweb embroidery, a black fur mantle beaded with rain, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters at a small table, she looks at an envoy off to one side with sweet, wide-eyed sincerity, lips parted as if promising forever. One hand signs with a long black quill. Her other hand rests on the parchment, wearing a silver spider ring whose one long leg is a curved blade folded under her fingers, glinting on the viewer's side, hidden from the envoy, pointing at no one.
