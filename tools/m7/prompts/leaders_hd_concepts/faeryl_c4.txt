FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared travelling gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and silver spiderweb embroidery, a black fur mantle beaded with rain, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters at a small table, she looks across it at an envoy off to one side with sweet, wide-eyed sincerity, head tilted, lips parted as if promising forever. One hand signs the treaty with a long black quill. Her other hand lies relaxed on the parchment's edge, wearing a silver spider ring whose one long leg is a small curved blade folded back under her fingers; its tiny steel hook catches the light on the viewer's side, hidden from the envoy, pointing at no one. Light: soft gold lantern light on her face under the canopy, cold blue-grey rain light around. Setting: dark carved posts of an open treaty pavilion, rain dripping from its eaves, at the edges; in the middle distance, a small hooded envoy and his guards in shadow, and black-cloaked archers half hidden among the wet trees; far behind, a dark elven forest of tall black trees and pale silver lights in the mist under a low rain sky.
