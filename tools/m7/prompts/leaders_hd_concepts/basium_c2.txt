VIBE: at the brink of the bleeding wound in the world called the Throne of Hell, his angels tell him it is finished, and Basium answers "not yet" before marching his whole nation down into hell.

BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining, hard pale-grey eyes. Silver-grey plate over a white tabard with a gold winged-sun emblem, and his battered grey-white WINGS with ragged feathers folded tight. Turned three-quarters at the brink, he looks down and ahead into the pit with grim, unblinking resolve, mouth hard, the scars catching the red light. One fist holds a heavy black iron war club upright before his chest, tiny anguished demon faces glowing red in its surface. Light: deep red glow from the pit below, a white-gold rim from the angels' swords. Setting: at the edges cracked black rock oozing dark ichor; in the middle distance two tall Seraphim with flaming swords at the pit's lip and ranks of soldiers and angels waiting, small; far below, inside the vast pit, a mountain slope falling away to dark swamps and the red walls of an infernal city, under a smoke-black sky. Colours: hell red, silver, white gold, black.
