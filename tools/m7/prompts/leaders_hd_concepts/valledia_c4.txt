VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still. Pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes. Formal academic robes of deep purple with a lilac-lined hood and silver piping, a heavy silver chain of office, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. Seated in a high-backed examiner's chair and turned three-quarters, she looks straight at you, one brow raised a fraction, mouth unreadable. One elbow rests on the carved chair arm, the fingertips of that hand steepled under her chin. Light: many small floating mage-lights of white and lilac overhead, cool and even, one bright point reflected in her eyes. Setting: the dark carved wood of an examiners' bench and stacks of scrolls at the edges; in the middle distance, tiered benches of robed students watching in the gloom, and a rune circle on the floor still smoking from a spell; far behind, tall windows of a great domed academy hall opening onto slender rune-ringed towers under a golden late-afternoon sky. Colours: deep purple, lilac, silver, late gold.
