FLAUROS, Grand Prince of the vampires and younger brother of Alexis, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; man; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and sleek. A pale, strikingly handsome face, swept-back dark auburn hair, piercing gold-yellow eyes, long white FANGS just showing in his smile. A high-collared black velvet coat embroidered in dark red, a loosely tied crimson silk cravat with a ruby pin. Turned three-quarters by a reading table, he looks straight at the viewer with smooth courtesy, head inclined like a perfect host, gold eyes glinting with private amusement. One hand holds out a crystal glass of thick dark red wine toward the viewer. Light: warm gold oil lamps on his face and the glass, cool grey rain light from tall windows behind. Setting: towering shelves crammed with leather books and scrolls at the edges, a rolling ladder; in the middle distance, pale servants and a thin grey-haired archivist in shadow carrying stacks of books; far behind, through rain-streaked windows, the black castle of a vampire brood on a crag above a rain-soaked river valley under a heavy grey sky. Colours: lamp gold, black, wine red, rain grey.
