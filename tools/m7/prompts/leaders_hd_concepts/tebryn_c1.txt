VIBE: on the island of the sealed well, the runecaster tests his new barrier on an apple tree and cups its first dark fruit, which beats slowly in his palm like a heart, with the quiet satisfaction of a gardener: his mission has begun.

TEBRYN ARBANDI, a runecaster returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about fifty; gaunt, hollow-cheeked. Ashen, scarred skin, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard. Black and crimson robes, hood thrown back, a high collar of dark iron, a belt hung with carved bone tablets, and a black cloth BLINDFOLD stitched with silver thread, tied over his eyes. Turned three-quarters, his blindfolded face is bent down toward his hand, head tilted as if listening, a thin calm smile. One hand is cupped at chest height around a small dark red-brown apple. Light: soft spring morning sun, green light filtering through leaves, a faint green glow from a rune on the trunk. Setting: at one side the warped black trunk and twisted branches of the apple tree, a rune glowing on its bark; in the middle distance, the closed stone face of a vast well ringed with carved runes, small green apples fallen in the grass; far behind, the lush jungle of a quiet island where bright birds scatter, and a calm turquoise sea under a clear morning sky. Colours: lush green, black, dark red, turquoise.
