VIBE: in the hour before dawn after the funeral she insisted on for her enemy, the Summer Queen rests her hand on the silver coffin of the sister she warred against and at last lets herself grieve.

ARENDEL PHAEDRA, queen of the Seelie court, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks about forty; tall and regal. Fair skin, long wavy copper-auburn hair, long pointed elf ears, soft grey-green eyes. A high-collared mourning gown of deep grey-green silk, a girdle of gold leaves at her waist, a long veil of sheer silver gauze falling from her delicate GOLDEN CROWN of interlaced LEAVES and branches. Turned three-quarters, head bowed, her eyes are lowered to the coffin before her, tears on her cheeks, mouth trembling but closed: grief with no audience. One hand rests flat on the closed lid of a silver and mithril coffin at the lower edge, a single white flower beneath her fingers. Light: grey-blue first light through open arches, and the warm glow of a hundred guttering candles at her side. Setting: at the edges living tree-pillars of the Temple of Leaves, wound with white flowers; in the middle distance heaps of flowers and small lanterns, and mourners standing far back in shadow; far behind, through the arches, the sleeping streets of Evermore and a great red oak against a sky fading from stars to pale grey. Colours: silver, candle gold, grey-green, dawn blue.
