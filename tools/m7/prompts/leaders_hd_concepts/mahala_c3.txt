MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. A black leather jerkin laced with rawhide, sleeves pushed up, the charcoal hood down, and the heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw. Pose: turned three-quarters, leaning over a camp table, she has lifted her head to look straight at you, sharp amber eyes narrowed and measuring, lips pressed thin in thought; one finger rests on the map, holding her place. Light: warm amber light from a small tallow lamp on the table, from below, cold blue night at the open tent flap. Setting: hide tent walls hung with antlers and a wolf pelt at one side, the map's broken blue wax seal on the table; in the middle distance, through the open flap, a sentry by a small fire; far behind, the snowy Doviello mountains under a clear deep blue night sky and a pale green ribbon of northern lights. Colours: lamp amber, hide brown, ink black, ice blue.
