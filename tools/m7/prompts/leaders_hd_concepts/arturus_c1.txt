VIBE: alone by candlelight in the Rune Hall, the old king carves the last fifty years of his banished friend's clan history into the stone pillar he means to give back, one patient rune at a time, as penance.

ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes. A leather work apron over a khaki-gold tunic with rolled sleeves, stone dust in his beard, and his squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Turned three-quarters, kneeling at a tall stone pillar, his eyes are fixed on the rune under his chisel, brows knotted, lips pressed tight in the beard. His scarred hands hold a small iron chisel to the stone and a hammer raised just above it. Light: a cluster of candles beside him, warm amber on his face, deep blue darkness all around. Setting: at the edges rows of stone pillars carved with bands of tiny runes, fading into the dark; in the middle distance an old blind runewarden reading a far pillar with his fingertips, small; far behind, the hall opens on the vast cavern of the Underhome, the tiny lamps of a dwarven city glimmering under a ceiling of stone. Colours: candle amber, slate blue, gold, warm stone.
