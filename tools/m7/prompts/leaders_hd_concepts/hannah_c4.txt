VIBE: on a dead-calm moonlit night, when the sea god no longer answers anyone, something vast rises beside her ship, and the Kraken of Fields reaches down to it as to an old friend.

HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled. Wind-burned olive skin, a wild mane of salt-tangled black hair, stormy sea-grey eyes. The weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons, collar turned up against the night chill, a sea-blue sash with a cutlass, and the NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord glinting in the moonlight. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the dark water, leaning out over the stern rail, her eyes lowered to the sea, a soft, private smile on her lips; one hand reaches down, fingertips just touching the curled tip of an enormous dark tentacle rising out of the water to meet them. Light: cold silver moonlight from above, a faint blue-green glow welling up from the deep beneath the hull. Setting: the carved stern rail and an unlit stern lantern at one side; in the middle distance, black water still as glass, the curve of a vast pale shape just under the surface; far behind, a rocky coast with a dark ruined sea temple, under a huge full moon and thin silver clouds. Colours: midnight blue, moon silver, deep sea green, bone white.
