VIBE: on a moonlit terrace the Boy-King rests his small hand on the snout of his dragon, and the great beast's eye, gold-green and slit like his own, slowly opens beside him.

CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. A night-blue silk robe over emerald robes with a high stiff collar, a jade seal on a gold chain. Turned three-quarters, he looks straight at the viewer with a small, calm, secret smile, head tilted, as if the likeness were a private joke between you. One small hand rests flat on the scaled green-black snout of an enormous dragon whose head lies in shadow beside him, only one huge gold-green slit-pupiled eye catching the moonlight. Light: cool silver moonlight on his face, an ember-orange glow from the dragon's nostrils below. Setting: at the edges a white marble balustrade and dark cypress; in the middle distance palace rooftops and a tiny guard staring up in alarm; far behind, the Kuriotate capital spreading to the horizon in countless lamps, green domes silvered by the moon, under a deep blue sky with a full moon. Colours: moon silver, emerald, ember orange, night blue.
