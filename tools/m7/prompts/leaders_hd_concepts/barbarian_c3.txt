VIBE: from the stake wall of a goblin fort in the morning mist, he watches the drilled ranks of a holy crusade march up the valley to destroy him, and licks a tusk with pure, eager joy.

THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. Black iron armour with spiked pauldrons beaded with mist, a shaggy black bearskin, a cord of bone charms across his chest, and his horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. Turned three-quarters, leaning on the sharpened logs, he looks off to the side and down at the army below, eyes narrowed with delight, tongue touching one tusk, a slow, eager grin. One hand rests a heavy cleaver flat on his shoulder, the blade pointing up and away. Light: cold pale morning, a weak sun behind white mist. Setting: at the edges the sharpened stake wall of the fort, hung with scorpion-shell totems; in the middle distance goblin archers with blackened arrowheads crouched along the walkway, and below, hazy in the mist, the white-and-crimson ranks of a crusader army under tall cross-marked banners; far behind, a jagged pine-covered ridge under a thinning grey-white sky. Colours: mist white, rust, bone, moss green, crimson.
