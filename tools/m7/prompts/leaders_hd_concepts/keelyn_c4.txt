VIBE: awake at midnight in her enormous bed with her imp asleep in her lap, the child who lost a playmate every year wonders, very quietly, whether one day she will be unsummoned too.

KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight. A pale, freckled little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. A quilted gold-and-black harlequin dressing gown buttoned up to a soft ruffled gold collar. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, a matching rose-pink bow on one of its horns, lies curled asleep in her lap, still grinning. Pose: sitting up in bed, turned three-quarters, head tilted down, her large amber eyes lowered to the imp, a small worried frown; one hand strokes its head between the horns. Light: cool silver moonlight from a tall window, a tiny violet glow from floating wisp nightlights. Setting: four-poster bedposts with big pink ribbons, coal-black curtains and grinning carved skulls at the sides; in the middle distance, a white bunny with red eyes and small fangs asleep on the quilt, and toy puppets slumped on a shelf; far behind, through the window, the lantern towers of Jubilee dimmed for the night, under a deep blue sky with a crescent moon. Colours: midnight blue, rose pink, black, moon silver.
