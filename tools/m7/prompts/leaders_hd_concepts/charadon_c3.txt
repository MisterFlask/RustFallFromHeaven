VIBE: standing in the first thaw of the new age, the wolf who thrived in the ice watches the snow turn to mud and crushes the first spring flower, certain that a soft world will make soft men.

CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred. Weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. Mud-spattered black-dyed leather and dark iron scales, a belt of wolf teeth, and the GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its pelt over his shoulders. Turned three-quarters, nose lifted into the warm wind, he looks south past the viewer, eyes narrowed in contempt at the smoke of distant farms, lip curled over one tooth. At his chest, without looking, one heavy fist crushes a small pale-blue spring flower, petals falling between his fingers. Light: thin, watery spring sunshine from one side, steam rising off wet ground. Setting: melting snowbanks and a dripping ice ledge at the edges; in the middle distance, a river breaking its ice, a war band and grey wolves picking through the mud; far behind, the last northern glacier glittering on the horizon, under a washed blue sky with fast clouds. Colours: mud brown, pale spring green, wolf grey, washed blue.
