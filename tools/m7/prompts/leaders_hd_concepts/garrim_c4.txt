GARRIM GYR, master enchanter and binder of spells, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; elderly; small, stooped and wiry. Ruddy, wrinkled skin, a big round nose, a long braided snow-white beard, bushy white eyebrows, sharp blue eyes behind small round brass spectacles. Ash-grey enchanter's robes under a miner's leather coat, a brass lantern hooked to his belt, and his staff in the crook of his arm, its brass STAFF HEAD of interlocking COGWHEELS by his shoulder. Pose: turned three-quarters, one arm stretched out over the drop, the crystal knotted in a thick grey cloth in his fist, red and violet light leaking through its folds; his eyes fixed on the bundle, brows knotted, jaw clenched, the face caught between letting go and holding on. Light: the crystal's red-violet glow from his hand, the lantern's warm yellow, black below. Setting: timber pit props and a rusted rope winch at the sides, a chain strung across the shaft's edge; in the middle distance, two small miners in shadow by an ore cart; far behind, through the gallery mouth, the night snow peaks of the Luchuirp mountains under a cold green aurora. Colours: charcoal, crystal red and violet, lantern yellow, aurora green.
