VIBE: at grey dawn after the carnival, among slack tents and trampled ribbons, the Laughing Man looks at you as if he has never seen you before, and asks in a quiet, sane voice whether enough have died yet.

PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A rain-darkened harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds, the purple-and-crimson velvet cape trimmed in gold hanging wet from his shoulders, the tall pleated white ruff wilted, and the smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tilted a little, his gold-flecked eyes look straight at you, puzzled and unguarded, brows drawn together, the mouth soft, no grin at all; one hand holds a wilted paper flower at his chest. Light: pale shadowless grey dawn, cold blue in the puddles. Setting: a slack striped tent wall and a toppled wooden stage at one side, puddles of floating confetti; in the middle distance, an empty carnival square, a burnt-out lantern pole, an empty cage with its door hanging open; far behind, the lantern towers of Jubilee dark and unlit, under a low dove-grey sky with a pale band of light at the horizon. Colours: dove grey, faded gold, wet crimson, pale blue.
