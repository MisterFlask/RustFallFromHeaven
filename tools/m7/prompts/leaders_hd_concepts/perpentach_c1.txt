VIBE: alone in the sunken Tower of Eyes, the prisoner finds his own head far too crowded, and presses his hands to his ears while his mother, his master and the minds he stole all argue over him at once.

PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A torn harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds grey with damp, the tall pleated white ruff limp and stained, no cape, and the smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Pose: turned three-quarters, crouched against the wall, both hands pressed hard over his ears, his gold-flecked eyes wide, darting up toward the voices, mouth open in a silent cry. Light: one thin shaft of grey-green daylight from a high slit window, cold on his face, the rest in swampy dark. Setting: wet moss-slick stone walls and an iron door with a grille at one side, black water pooling on the floor; in the middle distance, pale ghost figures faint as breath leaning in around him, a woman reaching out her arms, an old bearded mage; far behind, through the slit, a drowned swamp where motionless golem guards stand knee-deep, under a low sick-green sky. Colours: swamp green, wet grey, faded gold, porcelain white.
