KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; stocky and solid as a cask. A ruddy face, hazel eyes, thick copper-red hair tied back, a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. A plain ochre-gold travelling coat muddy at the hem over a brown leather jerkin, a merchant's purse, an unlit brass lantern and a hand axe at his belt. Pose: turned three-quarters, one broad hand pressed flat on the top stone of a fresh cairn of grey rocks; his hazel eyes, red-rimmed and wet, have turned sideways and up toward the stone door in the hillside behind him, narrowed, his mouth set hard in the beard. Light: cold rose-grey dawn light from one side, mist on the grass. Setting: wet grass and a twisted thorn tree at one side, a mithril battle axe lying in the grass; in the middle distance, the carved stone door of the Underhome in the hillside, standing a crack open; far behind, a misty forest valley and distant mountains, under a pale dawn sky with the last stars fading. Colours: dawn rose, mist grey, moss green, copper.
