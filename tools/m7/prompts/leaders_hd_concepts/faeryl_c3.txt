VIBE: her palace taken, the winter queen waits alone in her frozen garden to welcome the conquering captain with a gracious bow and a bouquet, and she is not even really there.

FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and silver spiderweb embroidery, a long black fur mantle whose hem dissolves into drifting frost mist, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters toward soldiers approaching off to one side, she inclines her head in a perfect gracious welcome, a serene smile on her lips, half-lidded eyes resting on their captain, amused. In the crook of one arm she cradles a bouquet of black winter roses with frosted petals; deep among the long stems, on the side turned to the viewer, lies the slim silver hilt and bare blade of a dagger, pointing down into the stems at no one. Her edges are faintly translucent: through one shoulder the hedges show. Light: pale grey-pink dawn, the orange glow of the invaders' torches from one side. Setting: frosted hedges and a frozen fountain with a silver spider statue at the edges; in the middle distance, small soldiers with torches coming through an iron garden gate; far behind, the captured black-and-silver Unseelie palace with smoke rising from one tower under a cold dawn sky. Colours: frost white, black, rose grey, deep purple.
