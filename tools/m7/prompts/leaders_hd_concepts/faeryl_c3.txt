FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared deep purple velvet gown with silver spiderweb embroidery, a long black fur mantle whose hem dissolves into drifting frost mist, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters toward soldiers approaching off to one side, she inclines her head in gracious welcome, serene smile, half-lidded eyes resting on their captain, amused. In the crook of one arm she cradles a bouquet of frosted black roses; deep among the stems, on the viewer's side, lies the silver hilt and bare blade of a slim dagger, pointing down at no one. Her edges are faintly see-through, like mist. Light: pale grey-pink dawn, orange torchlight from one side. Setting: frosted hedges and a frozen fountain with a silver spider statue at the edges; in the middle distance, small soldiers with torches coming through an iron garden gate; far behind, the captured black-and-silver palace, smoke rising from one tower, under a cold dawn sky. Colours: frost white, black, rose grey, deep purple.
