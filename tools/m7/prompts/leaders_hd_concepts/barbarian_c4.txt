THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. Black iron armour under a looted crimson velvet mantle trimmed with white ermine, a cord of bone charms over it, and his horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. Turned three-quarters, sprawled in a carved oak chair too small for him, he looks straight at the viewer, one heavy brow raised, with a wide, mocking, tusked smile. One huge hand lifts a jewelled gold chalice toward you in a toast, little finger daintily raised. Light: warm hearth glow on his face, red firelight through tall windows behind. Setting: at the edges torn tapestries and a toppled silver candelabrum; in the middle distance a long table where goblins squabble over silver plates, small and in shadow; far behind, through the tall windows, the roofs of a captured human town lit by fire and a temple spire against a smoky red sunset. Colours: crimson, gold, iron black, fire orange.
