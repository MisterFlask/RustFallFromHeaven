CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, deep grey eyes. A plain deep royal-blue robe with a high black collar and bronze clasps, sleeves rolled up, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols. Turned three-quarters beside a sickbed out of frame, he looks down at the feverish child he will not heal, brows drawn together, mouth pressed thin, head bowed by the choice. One hand is raised at chest height, closing slowly into a fist as a faint pale-gold glow dies between his fingers. Light: flat grey-green rain light from tall windows, the last gold of the glow on his knuckles. Setting: bronze-hinged shutters and whitewashed pillars of a long infirmary at the edges; in the middle distance, rows of low cots and small herbalists in brown with baskets of herbs and steaming copper kettles; through the windows, the rain-dark bronze domes of the Grigori city under a heavy grey sky. Colours: sage green, rain grey, royal blue, ivory.
