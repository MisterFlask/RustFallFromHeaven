VIBE: on the highest terrace of Galveholm, the woman who has been a prisoner of creation since its first morning lets the Worldbreak go, and as the sky cracks open her face shows something no one has seen on it before: relief.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair streaming in a rising wind, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown, pale white glowing JEWELS set into the black armour blazing bright. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tipped back, her dark violet eyes lifted to the breaking sky, lips parted on a long breath out, the face calm and almost tender; one gauntleted hand raised open above her shoulder, fingers spread, as if releasing a bird. Light: jagged white-violet fire tearing across the clouds above, lighting her face from overhead, red glow from the city below. Setting: a black stone balustrade carved with spirals at one side; in the middle distance, the rooftops and spires of the Sheaim city, small figures on the walls looking up; far behind, a great black planar gate on a hill glowing violet, under a night storm sky splitting open in long burning cracks of white and violet. Colours: black, crimson, white-violet, storm slate.
