A pale, strikingly handsome face, swept-back dark auburn hair, piercing gold-yellow eyes, long white FANGS just showing in his smile. Turned three-quarters toward small kneeling hunters off to one side, he smiles down at them with kind, almost tender warmth, but his gold eyes have slid aside to the pulse in one young hunter's bare throat. Setting: snow-laden dead pines and a hide windbreak at the edges; in the middle distance, ragged hide tents half buried in drifts, thin people in furs, a slain elk on a sledge; far behind, a vast wall of glacier ice glowing faintly blue through the blizzard under a starless sky. Colours: ice blue, snow white, black, ember orange.
