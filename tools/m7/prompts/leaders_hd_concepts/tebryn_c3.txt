VIBE: watching through an enchanted mirror as his spy in the Clown Prince's palace is caught in the middle of her report, the runecaster feels nothing at all, and lifts one hand to let the image go dark.

TEBRYN ARBANDI, a runecaster returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about fifty; gaunt, hollow-cheeked. Ashen, scarred skin, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard. Black and crimson robes, hood thrown back, a high collar of dark iron, a belt hung with carved bone tablets, and a black cloth BLINDFOLD stitched with silver thread, tied over his eyes. Turned three-quarters toward a tall mirror at one side, his blindfolded face angled to the glass, expressionless, lips a thin closed line. One hand is raised flat before the glass, fingers spread, to wipe the picture away. Light: cold silver-blue glow from the mirror on his face, the room in deep red shadow from one black candle. Setting: at the edge, the carved obsidian frame of the mirror, its glass showing a tiny woman seized by guards in jester colours; in the middle distance, shelves stacked with bone tablets and rune-scratched slates; far behind, through a narrow window, a dead city on ashen hills, its windows lit green, under a starless night with a sickly green aurora. Colours: mirror silver, deep red, black, sickly green.
