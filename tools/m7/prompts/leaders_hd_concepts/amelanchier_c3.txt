VIBE: at dawn in the great plaza of Evermore the ranger is anointed king and given the elven army, and he takes the oath with the grave, unsmiling face of a man who knows exactly what defending them will cost.

AMELANCHIER, elven ranger-prince, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; male; ageless, looks about thirty; tall and lean. Fair skin, long pale-blond hair tied back, long pointed elf ears, narrow hard green eyes. Ceremonial dress: a long leaf-embroidered mantle of deep green over a silver-scaled leather cuirass and a white-and-gold tunic, a new slender circlet of silver oak leaves on his brow, and the gold-tipped top of a tall PALE WOODEN LONGBOW over his shoulder. Turned three-quarters, he looks up and to one side toward a palace balcony above him, eyes steady and solemn, jaw set, no smile at all. His right hand is pressed flat over his heart in the oath. Light: the first golden sun of dawn striking his face from the side, cool blue shadow over the plaza behind. Setting: at the edges the carved living-wood pillars of the palace stair, wound with white blossom; in the middle distance a great crowd of elves filling a stone plaza, small and hazy, petals drifting in the air; far behind, the vast red oak of Evermore towering over the city, its crown glowing gold and white against a clear pale-gold sky. Colours: gold, white, deep green, dawn rose.
