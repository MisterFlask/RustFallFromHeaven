VIBE: after a long siege of the first dwarven city on the surface, the horsemen are simply gone one morning, and the old king looks out at the empty plain with the dry, weary satisfaction of a dwarf who never expected men to last.

ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes. Soot-stained steel pauldrons over a khaki-gold surcoat, a powder horn on a strap across his chest, and his squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Turned three-quarters on the battlement, he looks straight at the viewer, one bushy brow raised, mouth crooked in a dry, tired half-smile in the beard, as if to say: such are the ways of men. One broad hand rests on the muzzle of a long dwarven musket propped upright against the wall beside him. Light: cool early sun, pale gold through drifting smoke. Setting: at the edges massive square-cut stone merlons; in the middle distance dwarven musketeers lowering their guns along the wall, and below an empty plain of trampled grass with abandoned lances; far behind, the new dwarven city of stone halls and brass-domed towers climbing the hillside against the mountain, under a clearing morning sky streaked with smoke. Colours: bronze, stone grey, pale gold, smoke white.
