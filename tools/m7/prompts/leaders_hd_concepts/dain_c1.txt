VIBE: told by his queen that the war he dreads will come whatever he does, the archmage cuts brazier smoke into the faces of the demon king and the old peacemaker, searches them for a way out, and finds none.

DAIN THE CASWALLAWN, an absent-minded archmage and veteran siege-mage, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late thirties; lanky. Fair skin, untidy dark-brown hair, a scruffy beard, bright blue eyes with dark rings under them. Rumpled violet robes with gold trim, a worn grey scarf, scorch marks on one sleeve, and the tall, crooked PURPLE WIZARD HAT WITH SMALL GOLD STARS. Turned three-quarters, very still, all the absent charm gone, he studies the smoke at his fingertips, brows drawn, jaw tight, head bent forward. One hand is raised, two fingers parting a plume of smoke into two drifting faces, a horned demon lord and an old bearded monk. Light: hot orange glow from a hanging bronze brazier below, cold white storm light from a window. Setting: carved shelves of crystal orbs and star charts in the queen's chamber at the edges; in the middle distance, small and in shadow on a high seat, a still woman in deep purple, a violet stone glowing on her brow; through the window, the slender violet spires and turning rune rings of the Amurite capital under a dark midday storm sky. Colours: violet, smoke grey, ember orange, storm white.
