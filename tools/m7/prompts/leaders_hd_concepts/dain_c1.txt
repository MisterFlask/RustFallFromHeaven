DAIN THE CASWALLAWN, an absent-minded archmage and veteran siege-mage, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late thirties; lanky. Fair skin, untidy dark-brown hair, a scruffy beard, bright blue eyes with dark rings under them. Rumpled violet robes with gold trim, a worn grey scarf, scorch marks on one sleeve, and the tall, crooked PURPLE WIZARD HAT WITH SMALL GOLD STARS. Turned three-quarters, he stands very still, all the absent charm gone; his eyes are fixed on the smoke at his fingertips, brows drawn, jaw tight, head bent forward to study it. One hand is raised, two fingers parting a plume of smoke into two drifting faces, a horned demon lord and an old bearded monk. Light: hot orange glow from a hanging bronze brazier below the smoke, cold white storm light from a window behind. Setting: dark carved shelves of crystal orbs and star charts in the queen's audience chamber at the edges, the brazier hanging on chains; in the middle distance, small and in shadow on a high seat, a still woman in a deep purple robe with a faint violet stone glowing on her brow, watching him; through the window, the slender violet spires and turning rune rings of the Amurite capital under a dark midday storm sky. Colours: violet, smoke grey, ember orange, storm white.
