PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds under a purple-and-crimson velvet cape trimmed in gold, a tall pleated white ruff, and a smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the field below, head tilted down, gold-flecked eyes glittering, intent on the dancers, lips parted in a rapt smile; one long hand raised before him, fingers spread and curled as if holding invisible marionette strings. Light: warm orange lantern light from below, and behind him a huge red moon rimming his hair and cape. Setting: a grassy hilltop with a lantern on a crooked pole at one side; in the middle distance, small masked dancers in motley whirling across the night field toward a line of torches and shields, ribbons and bright blades flashing; far behind, the carnival city of Jubilee with its lantern towers on the coast, under a deep blue night sky. Colours: purple, lantern orange, moon red, gold.
