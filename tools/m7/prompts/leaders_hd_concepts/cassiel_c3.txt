VIBE: as the conqueror's army breaks into his burning capital, the archangel waits alone in the centre of his throne room and says, without fear: leave my children be, I am the one you want.

CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, deep grey eyes, soot on one cheekbone. The plain royal-blue robe with high black collar and bronze clasps, torn at one shoulder, no weapon. Turned three-quarters, standing straight, he looks directly at the viewer, the conqueror at his door, face calm and grave, eyes full of sorrow but empty of fear. His hands hang open at his sides, palms turned forward: he offers himself. Light: orange glow of the burning city on one side of his face, cold white morning light on the other. Setting: tall black-and-copper pillars of the throne hall at the edges, an empty bronze throne behind him; in the middle distance, through smoke, loyal craftsmen gripping hammers and armoured shapes in shadow at the doors; through high windows, the city of Midgar burning under a sky of black smoke and ash. Colours: copper, flame orange, ash grey, royal blue.
