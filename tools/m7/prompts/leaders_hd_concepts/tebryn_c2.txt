VIBE: before his planar gate, demons stirring in its fire, the archmage of the end of the world turns his blindfolded face to you and offers you its secrets as freely as he offers them to anyone, because the more who reach for power, the sooner it all ends.

TEBRYN ARBANDI, a runecaster returned from centuries of torment in hell to bring about the end of the world, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about fifty; gaunt, hollow-cheeked. Ashen, scarred skin, cropped iron-grey hair, a short grey beard. Black and crimson robes, hood thrown back, a high collar of dark iron, a belt hung with carved bone tablets, and a black cloth BLINDFOLD stitched with silver thread, tied over his eyes. Turned three-quarters, his blindfolded face turned straight toward you, head tilted forward eagerly, a wide fervent smile. One hand is held out to you, palm up, a small rune of green fire burning above it. Light: the gate's hot green fire behind him rimming his silhouette, the rune lighting his face from below. Setting: black basalt pillars carved with runes at the edges; in the middle distance, a huge ring of black stone filled with swirling green fire where horned and winged shapes move, robed acolytes of many races kneeling small before it; far behind, a city of black spires on a scorched plain, under a sky of churning purple storm cloud and green lightning. Colours: hellish green, black, crimson, storm purple.
