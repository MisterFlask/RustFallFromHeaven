VIBE: alone by the sacred fire of Braduk the Burning while orc children play in its glow, the warlord turns an old holy sun-medallion in his fingers and stares into the flames with a rage he has no words for, for the men his people were before their goddess fell.

THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. Black iron armour with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin, a cord of bone charms across his chest, and his horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. Seated on a fallen stone, turned three-quarters, he looks down into the fire at one side, brow furrowed, tusked mouth shut and grim, eyes far away. One huge hand holds a small tarnished bronze sun-disc up to the firelight, thumb rubbing its worn face. Light: deep orange-red firelight from below and to one side, the rest smoky darkness. Setting: at the edges fallen columns and cracked stone of a ruined human temple; in the middle distance orc children playing around a great pit of fire and a priestess in red rags, all small; far behind, the burning ruins of a once-holy city half collapsed into a glowing chasm, under a black sky of drifting embers. Colours: fire red, ember orange, bronze, smoke black.
