VIBE: after days lost in black despair inside his tent, the god-king walks out into the morning and with a few words lifts his whole army to its feet, blazing with the inhuman passion that is the other half of him.

AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A white wolf-fur cloak thrown back over a pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, frayed prisoner's rags still knotted at his wrists, and his jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE. Turned three-quarters, he looks out and down over the army below him, eyes blazing, mouth open mid-shout, chin high, a cold fire in his face not quite human. One fist is raised high beside his head. Light: hard, low winter sunrise from the side, gold on his face, long blue shadows on the snow. Setting: at the edges the stiff, frosted flaps of a white felt tent; in the middle distance pale Illian warriors in white and Doviello barbarians in brown furs raising spears and cheering, small; far behind, the Illian capital on a frozen plain, a pillar of white light rising from its heart and an ivory dragon circling it, under a clear, bright ice-blue sky. Colours: gold, ice blue, white, fur brown.
