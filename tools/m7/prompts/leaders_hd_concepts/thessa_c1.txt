VIBE: questioning a tired stranger on the night after the Beltane theft, the practical archmage turns his gift, a golden feather, in the candlelight and cannot make herself give it back, though she has tested it and there is no magic in it at all.

THESSA, archmage of the elven city of Evermore, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks in her twenties; slender. Pale skin, long black hair, long pointed elf ears, dark green eyes. A high-collared archmage's robe of deep green with silver leaf embroidery under a dark grey mantle, and the single GOLD FEATHER, not yet in her hair, held up between two fingers before her face. Turned three-quarters, she gazes at the feather, lips slightly parted, brows faintly drawn together, head tilted, caught between suspicion and a pull she cannot explain. Light: warm amber from a single candle on the table, a glint of gold from the feather on her cheek, cold blue night at the window. Setting: at the edges, the curved living-wood walls of a small office grown inside a tree, a table with a pouch of incense and a white festival mask; in the middle distance, a pale, tired human man seated in shadow; far behind, through a round window, the tree towers of Evermore with the last festival lanterns going out one by one, under a deep blue night sky. Colours: deep green, candle amber, gold, night blue.
