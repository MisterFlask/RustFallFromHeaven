CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred. Weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. Black-dyed leather and dark iron scales, a charcoal fur mantle, a belt of wolf teeth, and the GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its pelt over his shoulders. Turned three-quarters, leaning over the feast table, he looks straight at the viewer with a wide, hungry grin of yellow teeth, head cocked like a wolf's, the eyes not grinning at all. One fist raises an iron-bound drinking horn to the viewer, ale slopping over its rim. Light: hot amber firelight from a hearth pit below, a thin cold blue rim from the smoke hole. Setting: yellowed dragon ribs arching overhead at the edges; in the middle distance, a long table of roast meat, shouting warriors in furs, wolves fighting over bones; through a great doorway, the marble towers of the Bone Palace under a dark sky with a red moon. Colours: amber, blood red, bone yellow, charcoal.
