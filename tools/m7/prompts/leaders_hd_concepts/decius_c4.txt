DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; man; ageless, looks a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes, frost in his eyebrows. Frost-rimed steel plate with silver pauldrons, a heavy deep-crimson cloak with snow on its fur collar, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, he holds a long black sword upright before his face, its blade furred with white frost, and studies it with narrowed eyes, head tilted, lips pressed thin, not afraid, calculating. Light: harsh white-blue light from the pillar on one side, flat grey arctic daylight on the other. Setting: frosted black walls of a captured northern city at the edges, siege ladders against them; in the middle distance, small commanders arguing round a map table in the snow; far behind, a vast pillar of pale light rising from a ring of grim fortress buildings into a low white sky. Colours: ice white, steel, crimson, pale blue.
