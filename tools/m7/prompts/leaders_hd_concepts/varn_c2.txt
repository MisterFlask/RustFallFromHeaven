VIBE: in the little desert village whose hunters saved him from freezing, the refugee elf has raised the sun-disc over the first shrine he built to Lugus and turns to welcome the first villagers who come, astonished that anyone would follow him.

VARN GOSAM, elven prince turned sun-priest, leader of the Malakim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an elf living among desert men; male; ageless, looks about forty; tall, lean and wiry. Sun-bronzed skin, long straight black hair under a loose cream headscarf, long pointed elf ears, bright amber-gold eyes. Simple undyed cream robes with gold embroidery only at the hem, an ochre sash, and a staff topped with a radiant GOLDEN SUN-DISC with straight pointed rays, planted beside him. Turned three-quarters, he looks straight at you with an open, almost shy smile of welcome, eyes bright, head tilted. One hand is held out toward you, open, palm up, inviting you in. Light: warm red-gold evening sun low from one side, deep violet shadows on the other. Setting: the mud-brick wall of a small shrine with a sun symbol daubed in ochre, and a palm-frond awning at the edges; in the middle distance, a few robed villagers and children hanging back curiously among palm trees and goat pens; far behind, a ring of low dunes and a single rocky butte glowing red, under a sunset sky of rose and violet. Colours: cream, ochre, rose red, violet.
