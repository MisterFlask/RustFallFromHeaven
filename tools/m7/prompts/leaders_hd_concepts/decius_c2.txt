DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a human soldier who rose to rule other nations; man; about fifty, weathered; tall and lean. Weathered fair skin, short soldier-cropped iron-grey hair, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale grey eyes. Worn steel plate under a sun-faded sand-coloured desert mantle, the deep-crimson cloak rolled across one shoulder, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, face turned from the light, he looks down and aside at the sand, brows pulled together, mouth set hard, though his shoulders have not turned away. One hand rests heavily on the pommel of his sheathed sword. Light: white-gold dawn light reflected up from the pool under his jaw, the sky pale and cool. Setting: fused desert glass cracked into plates at the edges; in the middle distance, beside a crystal-clear pool glowing softly, a small robed elf waiting with a camel; far behind, Malakim tents on the dunes and a domed white desert city under a pale gold and blue dawn sky. Colours: white gold, sand, crimson, pale sky blue.
