DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; man; ageless, looks a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes. Worn steel plate under a sun-faded sand-coloured desert mantle with its hood thrown back, the deep-crimson cloak rolled across one shoulder, and the plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Turned three-quarters, he has turned his face from the light and looks down and aside at the sand, brows pulled together, mouth set hard, though his shoulders have not yet turned away. One hand rests heavily on the pommel of the sheathed sword at his hip. Light: white-gold dawn light reflected up from the glowing pool, bright under his jaw, the sky pale and cool. Setting: a vast field of fused desert glass cracked into plates at the edges, sand blowing across it; in the middle distance, beside a crystal-clear pool glowing softly, a small robed elf waiting with a camel; far behind, the tents of a Malakim camp on the dunes and a domed white desert city under a dawn sky of pale gold and blue. Colours: white gold, sand, crimson, pale sky blue.
