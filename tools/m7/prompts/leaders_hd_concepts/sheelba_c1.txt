SHEELBA, an orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean. Olive-green skin, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid half unravelled, threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. A huge borrowed burnt-orange orc warrior's fur cloak clutched around her shoulders over the torn remains of a grey wool human dress, and at her throat the torn white LACE COLLAR of that dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. Turned three-quarters, she stands frozen, eyes wide and fixed on the great fire ahead, lips parted, head pulled back. One hand grips the edge of the fur cloak at her chest. Light: hot orange-red glow from the fire on her face, cool blue jungle dusk behind her. Setting: carved wooden gateposts hung with antlers and animal skulls at the edges; in the middle distance, orc warriors sharpening spears and harnessing wolves around clan standards; far behind, a vast burning pit at the heart of a city of black stone and hide huts, the ember banner flying above it all, under a smoky violet dusk sky. Colours: burnt orange, flame red, olive green, dusk violet.
