VIBE: on the summit at the height of his ascension, a god raging in the blizzard around him, Auric watches the last two heroes stagger toward him through the snow, smiles, and raises one hand to slow time itself.

AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A torn pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, a white wolf-fur cloak whipping, frayed prisoner's rags at his neck and wrists, and his jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE, grown tall. Turned three-quarters, untouched by the storm, he looks straight at the viewer with a calm, faintly amused smile, head tilted, eyes too bright. One hand is raised, palm out, the snowflakes around it hanging frozen in the air. Light: hard white storm-light through a break in the clouds, a silver rim on his hair. Setting: at the edges jagged black rock and ice of the peak; in the middle distance below him a man and a woman staggering up the slope, a sword between them, nearly lost in snow; far behind, a vast vortex of blizzard cloud around the peak in which the faint giant face of the god of winter takes shape, under a white and black storm sky. Colours: storm grey, white, ice blue, black.
