AMELANCHIER, elven ranger-prince, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; male; ageless, looks about thirty; tall and lean. Fair skin smudged with soot, long pale-blond hair tied back, long pointed elf ears, narrow hard green eyes. A soot-stained leaf-patterned green-and-gold tunic and a leather baldric under a dark-green cloak pulled back, and the gold-tipped top of a tall PALE WOODEN LONGBOW over his shoulder. Turned three-quarters on a broad bough, head tilted, his eyes are half closed and turned down toward the forest floor far below, listening, utterly still, brows faintly drawn, lips parted. One hand lies flat on the massive grey bark of the trunk beside him, fingers spread. Light: hazy noon, the sun a dull red disk through drifting smoke, soft red-brown light on his face. Setting: at the edges giant fir boughs and needle clusters; in the middle distance an ancient white-haired elf druid with clouded blind eyes on the next bough, and rangers with bows among the branches, all small; far below and behind, a half-ring of wildfire glowing through the smoke, and beyond it the hazy shape of an elven city among giant trees under a smoke-brown sky. Colours: smoke grey, bark brown, fir green, ember red.
