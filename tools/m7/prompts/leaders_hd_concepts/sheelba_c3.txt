SHEELBA, an orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean. Olive-green skin, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. A leather smith's apron over iron-studded hide armour, her burnt-orange fur cloak thrown back, and at her throat the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. Turned three-quarters, she holds the spearhead up at eye level in a pair of iron tongs, edge still glowing, studying it with narrowed eyes and a fierce tusked grin. Light: forge orange from below on one side, cold blue-grey dawn from the open side. Setting: the rough clay wall of a furnace and a steaming water trough at the edges; in the middle distance, orc smiths hammering at anvils and racks of new spears; far behind, through the open side of the forge, a new orc town of straight log streets and a timber palisade with watchtowers built like a Bannor fort beside a jungle river, under a pale pink dawn sky. Colours: forge orange, iron grey, soot black, dawn pink, olive green.
