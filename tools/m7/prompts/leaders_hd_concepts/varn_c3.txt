Setting: the woven hangings of a large desert council tent at the edges; in the middle distance, a curtained inner doorway where a woman's silhouette waits alone in the lamplight; far behind, through the open tent flap, the white domes of a desert town asleep under a vast starry sky and a thin crescent moon.
