VIBE: having just told the gentle Elohim queen the grim original of her favourite fairy tale at her own banquet, the archmage regrets it at once and wonders whether making the pastries dance would save the evening.

DAIN THE CASWALLAWN, an absent-minded archmage and veteran siege-mage, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late thirties; lanky. Fair skin, untidy dark-brown hair, a scruffy beard, bright blue eyes. Violet robes with gold trim, brushed for once, a worn grey scarf, faint scorch marks on one sleeve, and the tall, crooked PURPLE WIZARD HAT WITH SMALL GOLD STARS. Turned three-quarters in a carved chair at the end of a long table, he looks straight at the viewer with a sheepish, hopeful half-smile, eyebrows raised, shoulders hunched a little, like a boy caught out. One hand hovers over a silver plate where a small cream pastry has risen an inch into the air and is slowly turning above a few violet sparks. Light: warm golden candlelight from silver candelabra, a soft white glow from a theatre stage behind. Setting: carved white-marble columns at the edges and a long white banquet table of buttered vegetables, cream sauces and wine glasses; in the middle distance, small noble guests gone quiet, and a stage with painted scenery of a tree and two white birds; far behind, through tall windows, the white temple domes of the Elohim capital under a dusky violet evening sky. Colours: violet, cream, white, candle gold.
