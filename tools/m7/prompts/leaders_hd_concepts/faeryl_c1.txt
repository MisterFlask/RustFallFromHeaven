FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and silver spiderweb embroidery, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters toward her cousin seated off to one side, she smiles with sisterly concern, head tilted, but her eyes rest past her on the window glass, measuring it. One hand is raised as if smoothing her hair, fingertips on the spider-shaped head of a long silver hairpin; on the viewer's side its shaft shows as a needle-thin stiletto tucked flat in the hair behind her ear, hidden from her cousin by her hand, pointing at no one. Light: pale amber afternoon light through rippled glass, wavering on her face. Setting: the carved black-wood frames of a tall ancient window at the edges; in the middle distance, through the warped glass, the twisted forest blossoming out of season under early snow; far behind, black-and-silver palace towers among the trees under a pale autumn sky. Colours: deep purple, amber, black, silver.
