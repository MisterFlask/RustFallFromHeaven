FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, straight silver-white hair past long pointed ears, narrow violet eyes. A high-collared gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and silver spiderweb embroidery, a short black fur cape, and the tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Turned three-quarters toward someone seated at the window off to one side, she smiles with soft, sisterly concern, head tilted in sympathy, but her violet eyes rest past her cousin on the glass, measuring it. One hand is raised to her hair as if smoothing it, fingertips on the spider-shaped head of a long silver hairpin; on the viewer's side its shaft shows as a needle-thin stiletto tucked flat along the coil of hair behind her ear, hidden from her cousin by her raised hand, pointing at no one. Light: pale amber autumn afternoon light through rippled old glass, wavering on her face, cold violet shadow on her far side. Setting: a tall ancient window whose glass has run and rippled, carved black-wood frames at the edges; in the middle distance, through the warped glass, the forest twisted into strange shapes, blossoming out of season under early snow; far behind, the black-and-silver towers of the Unseelie palace among the trees under a pale autumn sky. Colours: deep purple, amber, black, silver.
