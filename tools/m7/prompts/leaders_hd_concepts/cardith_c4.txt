VIBE: long after midnight, alone in the vast chancery, the boy who has looked ten for longer than anyone remembers presses his seal onto one more decree that keeps his rival clans in their places, and for a moment looks very old and very tired.

CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. Emerald robes with gold trim, the high stiff collar unfastened, sleeves pushed back. Seated at a huge desk, turned three-quarters, he looks out of a window to one side at the city, eyes half-lidded and dulled, an ancient weariness on his child's face. One small hand presses the jade seal on its gold chain into green wax on a parchment. Light: a single oil lamp warm on his face, dark blue night elsewhere. Setting: at the edges towering shelves of scrolls and ledgers, a cushion on his tall chair; in the middle distance a great wall map of the confederation dotted with gold settlement pins, and a secretary asleep in shadow; far behind, through the tall window, the Kuriotate capital asleep, green domes dark and a few lamps burning, under a starry sky with the first grey of dawn. Colours: lamp amber, emerald, night blue, gold.
