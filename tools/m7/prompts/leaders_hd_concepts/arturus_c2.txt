ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes. Steel pauldrons over a khaki-gold surcoat thrown on in haste, a great axe slung at his back, and his squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Turned three-quarters, both arms flung up and wide in a grand priestly gesture, mouth open on a booming threat, brows raised in mock wrath; but his eyes cut sideways to meet the viewer's with a sly glint: he knows what is about to happen. Light: dim red embers of a cook fire below him, the first white-gold flash bursting up out of them onto his face. Setting: at the edges a toppled tent and a cart wheel, the cart's tarp folded back from a tall carved stone pillar; in the middle distance masked riders with lances circling the camp, horses beginning to rear; far behind, black hills and the faint snowy peaks of the dwarven mountains under a starlit night sky. Colours: night blue, flash white, gold, ember red.
