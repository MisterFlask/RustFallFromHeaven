PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds under a purple-and-crimson velvet cape trimmed in gold, a tall pleated white ruff, and a smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tipped back, gold-flecked eyes wide and following something high across the sky, mouth open in a shout of pure delighted laughter; his long hands clapped together in front of his chest. Light: bright morning sun on his upturned face. Setting: a wooden siege platform hung with gold bells at one side, the arm of a huge garishly painted catapult swinging up beside him; in the middle distance, a brown-and-white cow tumbling through the blue air, unhurt and astonished, and soldiers in motley staring up; far behind, the grey walls of an enemy city on a hill, and nearer, the striped tents of the Balseraph camp, under a high blue sky with small round clouds. Colours: sky blue, gold, crimson, cream.
