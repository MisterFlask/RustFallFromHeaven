VIBE: alone in the half-flooded cult chamber where the dreamer who bore her once lay pierced with needles, the pirate queen turns one hollow bone needle in the candlelight and tries to feel anything at all for the mother and the twin sister she never knew.

HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled. Wind-burned olive skin, a wild mane of salt-tangled black hair, stormy sea-grey eyes. The weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons hanging open, a sea-blue sash with a cutlass, and the NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on its black cord. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tilted down, one needle lifted free of the others between her finger and thumb at eye level, her eyes on it, the face still and closed, only a small hard crease between her brows. Light: cold blue-green sea light rippling up from the flooded floor, tiny wick flames burning in old needles stuck in the wall niches, warm on her cheek. Setting: shallow empty stone recesses lined along the walls at the sides, wax drips on their ledges; in the middle distance, an altar of the Octopus Overlords wrapped in carved tentacles, half in the water; far behind, through a cave mouth, a grey sea and the drowned towers of a cult city under a heavy overcast dusk. Colours: sea green, wax yellow, bone white, slate grey.
