EINION LOGOS, the ancient head of the monastic order, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; very old; lean and upright, a former warrior. Deeply lined tan skin, a long white beard, close-cropped white hair, steady grey eyes. A plain white and ash-grey wool habit with a rope belt, and the small PAINTED SEASHELL on a cord at his chest. Turned three-quarters at a treaty table, he looks straight at the viewer, the demon across the table, stern and unmoved; but a shadow of unease has crept into his eyes, head tilted a fraction as he studies a smile he cannot read. One hand presses flat on the signed parchment, holding it down. Light: sulphur-red glow from cracks in the ground on one side, cold grey ash light from above. Setting: torn flaps of a black treaty tent at the edges, an ink pot on the table; in the middle distance, small deep-blue horned demons waiting in a sulphur haze; far behind, on a green hill beyond the scorched plain, the white walls and bell tower of an Elohim abbey under drifting ash with one patch of blue. Colours: sulphur yellow, ash grey, white, ember red.
