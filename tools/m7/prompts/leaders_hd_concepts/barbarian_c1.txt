THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. His armour stripped to a studded leather harness over scarred shoulders, a shaggy black bearskin knotted at his waist, a cord of bone charms across his chest, and his horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet, one horn freshly chipped. Turned three-quarters, chest heaving, head lowered, he looks straight at the viewer from under the skull with a wide, fierce, challenging grin, dust on his face. One fist holds up the dented iron war-mask of the beaten chief like a trophy. Light: harsh white noon sun from above, dust glowing in the air. Setting: at the edges a ring of sharpened timber stakes hung with rags and bones; in the middle distance goblins and ogres crowding the stakes, some kneeling, and far back a huge ogre sitting dazed; far behind, a sprawling camp of hide tents and smoke under a pale, burnt-white sky. Colours: dust ochre, bone, iron black, sun white.
