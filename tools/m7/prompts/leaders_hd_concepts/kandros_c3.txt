KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; stocky and solid as a cask. A ruddy face, hazel eyes, thick copper-red hair tied back, a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. A fine ochre-gold coat trimmed with brown fur over a brown leather jerkin, a heavy merchant's purse and brass lantern at his belt. Pose: seated, turned three-quarters, a cut blue gem held up between finger and thumb at eye level, a brass jeweller's loupe pushed up on his forehead; his hazel eyes have left the gem to look straight at you, twinkling, a broad shrewd grin parting the beard. Light: warm afternoon sun through the window, the gem throwing a point of blue onto his cheek. Setting: a brass scale, stacked coins and a tankard on the inn table at one side; in the middle distance, traders and a fiddler small and blurred in the common room; far behind, through the window, a new Khazad city of stone halls and gold-roofed vaults climbing a mountainside, under a bright blue sky with tall clouds. Colours: ochre gold, copper, sky blue, warm amber.
