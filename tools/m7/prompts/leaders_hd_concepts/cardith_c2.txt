VIBE: at dusk the Boy-King himself comes down to the gate of his capital to welcome a ragged band of hunted creatures who have fled persecution in other lands, and his welcome is entirely sincere.

CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. A plain dark green travelling cloak over royal robes of emerald with gold trim and a high stiff collar, a jade seal on a gold chain. Turned three-quarters, he looks down and to one side at the arrivals before him, head inclined, face open and warm, the reptilian eyes softened in the lamplight. One small hand is held out, palm up, in welcome. Light: warm amber lamplight from the gate on his face, violet-blue dusk behind. Setting: at the edges the open bronze-bound doors of a city gate under a green-tiled arch; in the middle distance a weary band of rare folk, a serpent-tailed lamia, a limping centaur, horned figures with bundles, all small and in soft shadow; far behind, the immense Kuriotate capital on its hills, green domes and gold roofs glowing with countless lamps, under a violet sky with the first stars. Colours: emerald, lamp amber, violet, gold.
