ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes. A high-collared white nightgown with silver embroidery under a pale grey shawl, and the BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT perched on her shoulder on one leg, its lame claw tucked up, feathers fluffed in alarm. Turned three-quarters at a battlement, she looks back over her shoulder toward a voice in the dark, eyes wide and frightened, but her chin lifted and her small mouth set in refusal. One hand is pressed flat to her chest, holding the shawl closed: she will not reach out. Light: cold silver moonlight, warm orange lanterns below, a faint unnatural red glow at the edges. Setting: white stone battlements at the edges, a gate below with its iron portcullis half raised; in the middle distance, small men running down the street and the hunched shadow of a werewolf at its end; far behind, the white temple domes of the Elohim capital under an autumn night sky, a vague horned shape seated in the clouds. Colours: moon silver, night blue, lantern orange, white.
