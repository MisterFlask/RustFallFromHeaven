SHEELBA, an orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; tall, strong and lean. Olive-green skin with streaks of ember-orange war paint across the cheekbones, small neat lower tusks, a thick black braid threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp hazel eyes. A huge burnt-orange orc warrior's fur cloak over iron-studded hide armour, a necklace of boar tusks, and at her throat the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress, its ripped edge hanging loose. Turned three-quarters, head tipped back a little in laughter, tusks bared in a wide grin, her eyes look straight into yours. One hand raises a curved horn cup high in a toast; a hunting spear leans upright against her shoulder. Light: roaring gold-orange bonfire light on her face, deep night blue behind. Setting: jungle palms and hanging vines lit orange at the edges; in the middle distance, orcs dancing round a great bonfire, sparks streaming upward; far behind, a chain of bonfires burning on hilltop after hilltop across the dark land, under a starry sky with a huge orange moon rising. Colours: bonfire gold, burnt orange, night blue, olive green.
