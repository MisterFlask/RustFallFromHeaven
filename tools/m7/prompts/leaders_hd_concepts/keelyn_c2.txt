VIBE: the day her father went off to his festivals, the little princess walked into his ministers' secret meeting with her biggest friend behind her and told them, very politely, that she would be in charge for a while.

KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight. A pale, freckled little face, raven-black hair tied back with a rose-pink bow, large amber eyes. A gold-and-black harlequin dress with a high ruffled gold collar under a gold chain of office far too big for her. Her small grinning red bat-winged IMP, a matching rose-pink bow on one of its horns, clings to her shoulder. Pose: turned three-quarters toward the council table at one side, chin up, large amber eyes looking sideways down at the ministers, a small satisfied smile; one small hand rests on her hip. Light: cold grey morning light through tall windows, a warm orange flicker from the demon behind. Setting: carved panelled walls at one side; in the middle distance, a long table of scattered papers, the ministers small and shrinking in shadow at its far end; filling the doorway behind her, half in shadow, an enormous horned Balor wreathed in dancing flame, a rose-pink bow on one horn; far behind, through the windows, Jubilee's palace towers under a white overcast sky. Colours: black, gold, ember orange, rose pink.
