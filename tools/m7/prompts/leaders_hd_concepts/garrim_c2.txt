GARRIM GYR, master enchanter and binder of spells, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; elderly; small, stooped and wiry. Ruddy, wrinkled skin, a big round nose, a long braided snow-white beard, bushy white eyebrows, sharp blue eyes behind small round brass spectacles. Ash-grey enchanter's robes soaked dark at the hem under an oiled leather cape, a leather apron of small tools, and his staff planted in the mud beside him, its brass STAFF HEAD of interlocking COGWHEELS by his shoulder, rain beading on the cogs. Pose: turned three-quarters, chin tucked, brows drawn down, he glares straight at you over his wet spectacles, lips pursed in offended pride; one hand pinches the rim, half lifting them off his nose to wipe them. Light: flat grey afternoon rain light, one pale break of sun behind him rimming the cape. Setting: wet heather and grey boulders at the sides; in the middle distance, three huge stone-and-brass golems stuck motionless in a churned field, rain streaming off their shoulders; far behind, the dark mountain gate of the Luchuirp halls flanked by carved golem guardians, under low rolling rain clouds. Colours: ash grey, wet slate, brass, heather purple.
