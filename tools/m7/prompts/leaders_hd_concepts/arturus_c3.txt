ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad. Weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids bound with gold rings, bushy grey brows, deep-set brown eyes. Steel pauldrons over a khaki-gold surcoat, a short brown cloak stirring in the wind, and his squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Turned three-quarters, chin up, he looks out over the plains below with fierce, steady eyes, mouth open on a deep, carrying word, the fear he will not show only a hard set to his jaw. One arm sweeps out toward the plains, broad hand open. Light: clear high sunlight, warm on his face. Setting: at the edges the rough mouth of a mountain tunnel and grey boulders; in the middle distance dwarves crouched against the rocks and gripping the ground, small, faces turned from the sky; far below and behind, green hills, blue lakes and golden valleys to the horizon, the snowy peak of the dwarves' mountain on one side, under an immense blue sky with towering white clouds. Colours: sky blue, gold, steel, meadow green.
