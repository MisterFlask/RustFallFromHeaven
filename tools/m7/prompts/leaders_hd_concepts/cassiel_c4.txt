VIBE: a refugee priestess asks him whether the one true god her people preach really exists, and the archangel who once stood in heaven answers with a small, kind smile and nothing more.

CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean. IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, deep grey eyes. A light summer robe of deep royal blue open at the high black collar, bronze clasps, a bronze-buckled belt, no holy symbols. Turned three-quarters on a stone bench, he leans back a little and looks straight at the viewer, one pale eyebrow lifted, a small knowing smile, head tilted: he has heard the question a thousand times and will not answer it. One hand is raised, the index finger resting against his lips. Light: warm late-afternoon sun through vine leaves, dappling gold over his marble-white face. Setting: bronze trellises heavy with grapevines in a walled refugee garden at the edges; in the middle distance, small robed refugees reading scrolls around a fountain, people debating on a meeting hall's steps; far behind, the free city's busy harbour full of foreign ships under a clear sky with high summer clouds. Colours: ivory, bronze, leaf green, sky blue.
