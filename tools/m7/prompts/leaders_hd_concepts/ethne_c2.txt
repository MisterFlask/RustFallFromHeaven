ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes, wet with tears. A high-collared white gown with silver embroidery, a pale grey shawl, and the BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT standing on her raised wrist on one leg, its lame claw tucked up, wings half spread at the light. Turned three-quarters, she looks up past the viewer into the blaze of the opening gate, lips pressed together, tears on her cheeks, chin raised: she has chosen this and will carry it. Her other hand is pressed over her heart. Light: brilliant gold-white light from the gate across her face, cool blue dawn shadow behind. Setting: white marble steps and tall pillars of the gate plaza at the edges; in the middle distance, an immense arch of gold and white stone on a hill, filled with light, rows of small winged armoured figures marching out, monks kneeling to watch; far behind, the white temple domes of the Elohim city under breaking dawn clouds pierced by rays. Colours: gold, white, dawn blue, a touch of parrot blue and yellow.
