VIBE: in the Age of Ice the starving tribe she has adopted huddles around the fire, eating the meat she hunted for them, while she asks, so gently, for only a few drops of blood in return.

ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. A long hooded mantle of white wolf fur over a dark crimson wool gown, snow caught in her braids, a ruby brooch pinning the fur at her throat, and her slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES. Turned three-quarters, she looks down and aside at the huddled people by the fire with a tender, motherly smile, her eyes cold and calculating. One bare pale hand, untouched by the cold, holds out a small empty silver cup toward them. Light: warm orange firelight from below on one side of her face, blue blizzard light on the other. Setting: at the edges the snow-heavy hide walls and bone poles of a shelter; in the middle distance gaunt figures in ragged furs, small and in shadow, around a fire where a deer haunch roasts; far behind, a lone black stone tower on a frozen ridge, the first seat of the vampires, under a grey-white sky of driven snow. Colours: white, crimson, fire orange, blizzard blue.
