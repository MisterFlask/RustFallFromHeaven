CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty-five; tall and strong. Fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, dark chestnut hair in a long thick CHESTNUT BRAID over one shoulder, grey eyes. Dented silver plate over a white surcoat with a red cross, a battered round shield lowered on one arm. Turned three-quarters, she looks off to one side at a figure in the rubble, eyes wide and shining with sudden tears, lips parted on a name, the iron in her face breaking into disbelieving joy. One bare hand rises, open, half reaching toward him. Light: one shaft of grey-gold daylight through a broken dome onto her face, the rest grey smoke. Setting: at the edges broken black columns and catapult-smashed masonry; in the middle distance a grey-bearded warrior with a battered shield standing in the rubble, small, and robed mages closing a wavering red portal; far behind, the shattered black towers of the infernal city, and on a hill beyond them the white tents of the Bannor army, under a grey sky breaking open to blue. Colours: smoke grey, silver, crimson, pale gold.
