Turned three-quarters, risen upright beside a carved stone bed, he looks straight at you, eyes wide open and luminous, brows level, lips parted on the words, accusation and certainty in his stillness. One hand is raised at chest height, forefinger lifted, stopping you before you can speak. Light: the room in near darkness, his own cold silver glow the only light, brightest on his face and hand. Setting: at the edges, stone columns built into the walls of a high cubic vaulted chamber; behind him the long carved stone bed, and a closed door outlined by a thin line of candlelight; on the far wall, the first ghostly image of a window forming out of bare stone, showing snowy mountain peaks under a grey dawn sky. Colours: silver, slate black, white, faint gold.
