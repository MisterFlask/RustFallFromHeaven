MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean and hard as a huntress. Wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes. A black leather jerkin laced with rawhide, the charcoal hood half raised, and the heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw, rime on its edges. Pose: turned three-quarters, head tipped back, her sharp amber eyes raised to the statue above her, wary and calculating, breath steaming; one gloved hand rests flat on a pillar of ice, testing its cold. Light: cold blue-white daylight glowing through the ice walls, a pale glint reflected in her eyes. Setting: tall pillars of clear blue ice carved with frost patterns at the sides; in the middle distance, small pale robed Illian priests crossing a frozen floor, and a ring of white candles before an altar; far behind, a gigantic statue of a horned winter god cut from glacier ice, and through a high opening, white tundra under a pale blue-white winter sky. Colours: glacier blue, white, black, auburn.
