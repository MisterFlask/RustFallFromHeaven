BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining, hard pale-grey eyes. Dented, scorched silver-grey plate, a torn white tabard with a gold winged-sun emblem streaked with soot, and his battered grey-white WINGS with ragged feathers drooping half open. Turned three-quarters, head bowed, he looks down at the feather in his hand, his stone face almost still, only a tight jaw and a wet glint in his eyes betraying grief. One huge scarred hand holds up a single long white feather, luminous at its edges, between thumb and finger. Light: soft grey-violet dusk, with a faint warm gold glow from the feather itself. Setting: at the edges broken spears and scorched earth; in the middle distance winged Valkyries in silver walking slowly and kneeling far away across the field, small, hazy and luminous; far behind, the tall white towers of the Mercurian capital on a hill, a column of pale light rising from it, under a twilight sky of violet and grey with a single bright star. Colours: violet, grey, silver, pale gold.
