HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled, with wind-burned olive skin, a wild mane of salt-tangled black hair with a few loose braids, stormy sea-grey eyes and a fierce, volatile glare like a squall about to break. She wears a weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons over a dark leather jerkin, a sea-blue sash knotted at one shoulder, and a NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord, a relic of the cult-born dreamer mother she never knew. Background: the storm-dark deck of a pirate ship under black sails, grey-green waves rearing beyond the rail.
