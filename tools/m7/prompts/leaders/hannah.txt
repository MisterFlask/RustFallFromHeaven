VIBE: the prophesied tempest in human form: the sea has drawn back from the harbour, the whole world holds its breath before the wave, and she alone is calm, smiling the hungry smile of the storm about to fall.

HANNAH THE IRIN, the feared mutineer pirate queen, leader of the Lanun in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty; wiry and hard-muscled, with wind-burned olive skin, a wild mane of salt-tangled black hair and stormy sea-grey eyes. She wears a weathered navy-blue sea-coat with tarnished silver buttons, a sea-blue sash, and a NECKLACE OF THIN BONE NEEDLES on a black cord, relic of the cultist dreamer who bore her. Head tilted slightly down, eyes wide, unblinking and fixed on you, she smiles a quiet, hungry, almost serene smile; the wind lifts her hair like weed in a current. The light is the sickly yellow-green of the hour before a hurricane, and lightning behind her rims her in cold white. Beyond her the sea has drained from the harbour, ships stranded on wet black sand, and a green-black wall of water rears on the horizon.
