DECIUS, the exile who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dark elf of Erebus ruling human nations; man; ageless, looks a weathered fifty; tall and lean. Dusky blue-grey skin, short soldier-cropped white hair, long pointed ears, a narrow hawkish face lined by campaigns, an old pale scar through one eyebrow, pale ice-grey eyes. He slouches sideways in a tall dark throne, head tilted back, one eyebrow faintly raised, a dry, unimpressed half-smile, eyes level and tired on the viewer, measuring. One hand holds a wine goblet loosely at chest height, forgotten. Worn fine steel plate with silver pauldrons and gold trim under a heavy deep-crimson cloak. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN tipped carelessly on his brow. Background: a captured human throne hall at late-summer dusk, dusty amber sunbeams across faded banners, the light turning to rust.
