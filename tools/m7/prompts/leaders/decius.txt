DECIUS, a soldier-noble exiled from the Bannor who rose to be king, leader of the Bannor, Malakim or Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; mid-forties; tall, broad and battle-hardened. Weathered olive skin, short dark hair greying at the temples, a close-trimmed black beard, an old scar through one eyebrow, hooded dark eyes with a wary, calculating, world-weary look. Worn but fine steel plate armour with silver pauldrons and gold trim, a heavy deep-crimson cloak clasped at one shoulder. A plain, dented STEEL CROWN resting on his brow. Background: a windswept ridge at dusk above a distant walled city, the campfires of an army glowing on the plain below.
