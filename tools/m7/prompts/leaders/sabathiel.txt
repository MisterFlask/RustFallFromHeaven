SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair, luminous pale-grey eyes. His body is turned away toward a window and his head has turned back over one shoulder to look down at the viewer: brows level and calm, eyelids half-lowered, the mouth a small, sad, downturned line of patient disappointment, gentler and worse than anger. Polished white-and-silver plate over a long white robe, bright gold at the collar. Great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded high behind him. The only light is his own cold silver glow and the windows: a dark vaulted chamber whose tall arched windows each show a different land far below, snowfields, green pastures, desert, a sprawling city, flickering past too fast to hold.
