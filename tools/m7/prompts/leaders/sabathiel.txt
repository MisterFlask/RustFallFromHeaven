SABATHIEL, the hidden angel-king, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: an angel; male; ageless; huge and broad-shouldered, his skin giving off a faint pale glow. A smooth, flawless pale face, long straight silver-white hair falling past the shoulders, luminous pale-grey eyes that seem to see straight through everything, a calm, cold, all-knowing expression of stern disappointment. Polished white-and-silver plate armour over a long white robe, trimmed with bright gold at the collar and shoulders. Great WHITE FEATHERED WINGS folded high behind the shoulders. Background: a dim vaulted stone hall whose tall arched windows look down from a dizzying height onto a sprawling walled garrison city under a pale sky.
