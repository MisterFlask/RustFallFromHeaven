GARRIM GYR, master enchanter and binder of spells, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; elderly; small, stooped and wiry. Ruddy, wrinkled skin, a big round nose, a long braided snow-white beard, bushy white eyebrows, sharp blue eyes behind small round brass spectacles. He leans close over a glittering crystal held at chest height on a folded cloth, never bare-handed; eyes wide, full of its light, brows lifted in helpless fascination, while his mouth is pulled down tight with grief and dread, as if he hears it whispering. The crystal sparkles red, violet and deep blue, lighting his face from below; thin dark mist curls at its edges. Ash-grey enchanter's robes embroidered in charcoal and silver. A brass STAFF HEAD of interlocking COGWHEELS by his shoulder. Background: his dark stone study, shelves of brass instruments sunk in shadow.
