GARRIM GYR, master enchanter and binder of spells, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; elderly; small, stooped and wiry. Ruddy, wrinkled skin, a big round nose, a long braided snow-white beard, bushy white eyebrows over sharp, curious blue eyes behind small round brass spectacles, a stern, wary, scholarly expression. Ash-grey enchanter's robes embroidered in charcoal and silver, with a brass-buckled leather collar. A brass STAFF HEAD of interlocking COGWHEELS cradling a pale blue crystal beside his shoulder. Background: a lamplit stone study cut into a mountain, its shelves crowded with crystals, brass instruments and old books.
