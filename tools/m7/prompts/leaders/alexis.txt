VIBE: the girl so afraid of dying that she became death's most patient student: leaning close by candlelight to watch your face for the moment the life leaves it, centuries on and still unable to see where it goes.

ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, glossy black hair swept up in a coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes. A crimson velvet gown buttoned up to the chin, with a high standing collar of black lace. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES. She leans in very close, head tilted slightly, studying your face with the absorbed, unblinking attention of a naturalist examining a moth: brows faintly drawn, lips parted, no cruelty in her at all, only cold curiosity. One hand holds a single candle up near your face to see better; its small amber light falls on her from below and the side, and everything else sinks into black. Behind her, barely visible, dark shelves of plundered books climb into shadow.
