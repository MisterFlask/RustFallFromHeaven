ALEXIS, queen and first of the vampires, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and poised. Porcelain-pale skin, glossy black hair swept up into an elegant coil of braids, dull gold-yellow eyes and the cool, faintly sneering gaze of one who studies death with clinical curiosity. A crimson velvet gown with a high standing collar of black lace and a black velvet ribbon at her throat. A slender silver CROWN SET WITH BLOOD-RED RUBIES. Background: a candle-lit gothic throne hall of dark stone hung with tall crimson curtains.
