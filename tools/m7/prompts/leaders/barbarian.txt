THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, two jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow and a snarling scowl of pure fury. Black iron armour with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin over his shoulders and a cord of bone-white charms across his chest. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. Background: a rocky hilltop war camp of crude hide tents and bonfires under a dark stormy sky.
