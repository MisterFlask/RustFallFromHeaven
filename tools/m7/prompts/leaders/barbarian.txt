THE ORC WARLORD, a nameless chieftain of the raiding orc and goblin hordes, leader of the Barbarians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; battle-hardened middle age; huge, thick-necked and broad. Grey-green skin crossed by old scars and crude black war paint, jutting yellowed lower tusks, small red-rimmed amber eyes under a heavy brow. Black iron armour with spiked pauldrons, a shaggy black bearskin and a cord of bone charms across his chest. A horned bone-white BEAST SKULL worn as a helmet. He leans forward out of the dark, head low, grinning wide with hungry, patient delight, the tiny warm windows of your city reflected in his eyes. A torch raised at his chest throws hot orange light up under his tusks; everything else is cold, moonless blue. Behind him, among black pines on a ridge, torch after torch is being lit as goblins and ogres gather.
