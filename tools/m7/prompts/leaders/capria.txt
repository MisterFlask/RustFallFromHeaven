CAPRIA, the battle commander who led her people out of hell as a girl, leader of the Bannor in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about thirty; tall, strong and athletic, a hardened soldier. Sun-browned fair skin, a thin pale scar across one cheekbone, dark chestnut hair pulled back tightly, steady grey eyes, a fierce, protective, unflinching expression. Heavy riveted dark-brown leather armour with a silver steel gorget at the throat, under a white wool tabard edged in silver. A long thick CHESTNUT BRAID falling over one shoulder. Background: a wide green grassland at dawn, the white walls and cathedral spire of a fortified garrison town on a distant hill.
