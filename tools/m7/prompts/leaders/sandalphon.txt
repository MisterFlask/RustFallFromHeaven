SANDALPHON, an immortal shade who traded part of his soul for eternity, leader of the Sidar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human, becoming a shade; male; ageless, looking forty; tall and upright, with unnaturally pale ash-grey skin, short smoke-grey hair swept back and dull grey eyes in which light never glints. He wears a high-collared ash-grey robe over charcoal layers; his SHOULDERS AND COLLAR DISSOLVE INTO SOFT GREY MIST. Calm, lips parted mid-word, eyes turned mildly aside toward an unseen pupil, he does not look as his pale hand, raised at chest height, grips a gloved wrist thrust in from the fog, its dagger stopped short of his throat. A hard, cold white light strikes his face and that hand, suddenly solid, while the rest of him melts into the grey. Behind, a fog-choked lane ends at a black tunnel mouth, amber lamps blurred into haloes.
