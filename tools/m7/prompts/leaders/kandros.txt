VIBE: the clansman who pried open the forbidden stone door into the dreaded Age of Ice and found soft grass and a mild, starry night: three hundred years of stone ceiling lifting off his heart in a single breath.

KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; stocky and solid as a cask, with a ruddy face, thick copper-red hair tied back and a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. He wears a plain ochre-gold travelling coat over a brown leather jerkin. His head is tipped far back to the open sky, hazel eyes wide and shining wet, mouth open in a breathless, disbelieving laugh, one hand raised palm-up as if to feel the mild night air. Cool silver moonlight falls full on his upturned face; a thin line of warm candle-gold leaks from the heavy stone door ajar in the hillside behind him. Around him, silver-green grass rolls down under an enormous indigo sky thick with stars.
