KANDROS FIR, the merchant-prince who led his clan out of the Underhome to trade with the surface world, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and solid as a cask, with a sun-tanned ruddy face, thick copper-red hair tied back, quick appraising hazel eyes, a confident trader's half-smile and a stubborn set to his jaw. He wears a rich ochre-gold travelling coat trimmed with brown fur over a dark brown leather jerkin, heavy gold chains and rings, and a FORKED COPPER-RED BEARD braided into two plaits bound with gold coin rings. Background: the great stone gate of a dwarven hold opening onto green, sunlit hills.
