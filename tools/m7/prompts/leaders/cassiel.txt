CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean, with smooth IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features, long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples, and deep grey eyes full of great sadness and patient kindness, tired from the burden of mortality.
