VIBE: the archangel turned weary mortal, the only one in his throne room who can see the murdered girl's ghost, leaning toward her with her own father's grief in his eyes, pleading and commanding at once that she open her eyes.

CASSIEL, an archangel who renounced the gods and became a mortal man, leader of the Grigori in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: now wholly human and wingless; male; ageless but weary, looking about fifty; tall and lean, with IVORY-WHITE SKIN like polished marble, finely carved noble features and long straight pale-gold hair greying at the temples. He wears a plain deep royal-blue robe with a high black collar and bronze clasps, and no holy symbols. He leans forward from his throne, deep grey eyes locked on yours, brows drawn up in the middle with a father's sorrow, lips parted as he speaks, one hand lifted at chest height, palm open toward you, urging you to wake. A clear, soft pale-gold daylight holds only him; the bronze-and-black throne room beyond dissolves into drifting grey haze, in which faint pale figures of other lost souls can just be made out.
