SHEELBA, an orc raised as a human daughter who ran back to her people, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; female; about twenty; strong and lean. Smooth olive-green skin, small neat lower tusks, a thick braid of black hair threaded with ember-orange beads, sharp intelligent hazel eyes and the defiant, watchful look of someone who knows both worlds. A mantle of burnt-orange fox fur and brown leather over her shoulders, and beneath it the torn white LACE COLLAR of a human girl's dress she never threw away. Background: an orc war camp of hide tents and glowing fire pits at the edge of a dark green jungle at dusk.
