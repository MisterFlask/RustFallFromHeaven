ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large watery blue eyes, a kind, compassionate, quietly resolute expression. A simple but fine white gown with silver embroidery at the collar and a pale grey shawl over her shoulders. A small BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT perched on her shoulder, standing on one leg. Background: palace gardens of marble fountains and a tall tulip tree, the hedges cut away to reveal the humble rooftops of a town beyond.
