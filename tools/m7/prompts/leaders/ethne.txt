ETHNE THE WHITE, gentle young queen, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; woman; about twenty; slight and graceful. Fair, pale skin, long straight white-blonde hair held back by a thin silver band, large blue eyes. She looks out past the viewer's shoulder at something below her, brows drawn up in open compassion, yet her eyes are clear and steady, her chin raised, her small mouth set firm: she will not look away. Both hands at her chest cradle a small BLUE-AND-YELLOW PARROT standing on one leg, its other claw tucked up, lame. A simple white gown with a high collar, silver embroidery and a pale grey shawl. Light: soft, cool, overcast morning, pearl-grey and white, almost shadowless. Background: a palace garden of marble fountains whose hedges have been ripped out, raw stumps opening onto the crowded, muddy rooftops of the poor quarter beyond.
