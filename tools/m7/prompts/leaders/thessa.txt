THESSA, archmage of the elven city of Evermore, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks in her twenties; slender. Pale skin, long black hair, long pointed elf ears, dark green eyes. She looks straight at the viewer as at her new husband, holding a small, tender smile while tears spill down her cheeks and her eyes hold no light, only farewell; her head tilts softly forward. One hand reaches out at chest height to draw him closer; the other is hidden in the folds of her gown at her waist. A high-collared wedding gown of deep forest green and black with gold trim. A single GOLD FEATHER in her hair. Light: cold silver moonlight, a few dying festival lanterns. Background: a moonlit clearing under a great red oak, the woods beyond withered and stained sickly violet by corruption.
