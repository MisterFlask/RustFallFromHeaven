THESSA, archmage of the elven city of Evermore, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks in her twenties; slender and willowy. Pale skin, long straight black hair, long pointed elf ears, dark green eyes shadowed with grief, the sorrowful, haunted yet resolute expression of one who paid a terrible price to heal her forest. Archmage's robes of deep forest green and black with fine gold trim and a high collar. A single GOLD FEATHER worn in her long black hair. Background: a sunlit forest clearing beneath a great red oak, the woods around it lush, green and teeming with life.
