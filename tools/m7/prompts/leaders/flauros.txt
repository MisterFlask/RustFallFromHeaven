FLAUROS, Grand Prince of the vampires and younger brother of Alexis, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; man; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and sleek. A pale, strikingly handsome face, swept-back dark auburn hair, piercing gold-yellow eyes. A high-collared black velvet coat over a loosely tied crimson silk cravat. Long white FANGS just showing in his smile. He leans lazily on a stone balustrade, eyelids heavy, a slow private smile of anticipation on his lips, eyes sliding sideways to the sinking sun as if timing it; one hand dangles a goblet of dark red wine over the drop. The last low blood-orange sunlight rakes across his face from the side, half already in shadow. Far below, a crowd with raised fists fills a plaza, and the long black shadows of the palace spires creep across the cobbles toward them like teeth.
