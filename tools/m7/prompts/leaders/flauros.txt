FLAUROS, Grand Prince of the vampires and younger brother of Alexis, leader of the Calabim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: vampire, once human; man; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and sleek. A pale, strikingly handsome face with high cheekbones, swept-back dark auburn hair with a few careless loose strands, piercing gold-yellow eyes and the lazy, self-satisfied half-smile of a lord who believes the whole world belongs to him. A high-collared black velvet coat over a loosely tied crimson silk cravat and a gold collar chain. Long white FANGS showing in his smile. Background: a lavish castle chamber of old tapestries and crimson silk curtains with a tall silver-framed mirror, lit by candles.
