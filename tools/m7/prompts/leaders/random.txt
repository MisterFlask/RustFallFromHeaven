VIBE: the held breath before a story starts: a traveller at the edge of an unmapped world in the first light, hand at the hood, about to draw it back without yet knowing whose face it will be.

THE UNKNOWN RULER, a leader not yet revealed, leader of an unknown people in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a figure of no clear race, sex or age; still and upright, shoulders draped in a heavy cloak of ash-grey wool. A DEEP HOOD whose shadow hides the face completely: no skin, hair, eyes or features, only soft darkness. One grey-gloved hand rises at chest height to the edge of the hood, fingertips just touching it, as if about to draw it back, and not yet. The head turns a little toward the horizon, hushed and expectant. The first gold light of dawn grazes the rim of the hood and one shoulder; the rest is cool pearl-grey. Below the high ridge behind, a valley of morning mist where faint roads wind away toward half-seen shapes, a spire, a forest, a glint of sea, a mountain, all soft and undecided.
