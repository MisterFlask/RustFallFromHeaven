VIBE: The ageless boy-king giving you a warm, genuinely affectionate smile while his hand, without even glancing down, makes the killing move on the board.

CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight, portrayed with dignity. Soft, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a slender gold circlet. He looks up at the viewer with a sweet, warm smile, head tilted slightly, while at chest height his small hand sets a carved jade chess knight on the board without a glance. Only his eyes betray him: cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow slit pupils. Royal robes of emerald green with gold trim and a high stiff collar. Light: clear, cool, innocent morning brightness. Background: a serene marble chamber under green-tiled domes, the last pieces of a lost game on the board, a tall centaur guard in green and gold watching uneasily from a doorway.
