CARDITH LORDA, the ageless Boy-King, leader of the Kuriotates in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human-seeming; boy; looks ten years old but is far older; small and slight. Soft, smooth, boyish fair features, short neat dark-brown hair, a small calm smile that does not reach his eyes, a cold, calculating composure far beyond his years. Rich royal robes of emerald green with gold trim and a high stiff collar, a slender gold circlet on his head. Cold, glinting GOLD-GREEN REPTILIAN EYES with narrow pupils. Background: a bright marble court beneath green-tiled domes, a centaur guard in green and gold waiting in the distance.
