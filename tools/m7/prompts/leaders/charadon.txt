VIBE: the lost boy who watched, expressionless from a hillside, as the wolves he had led home culled his own family, now an old man called back from the dead and still wearing that same blank, approving stare.

CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive and scarred, with weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair, a matted grey beard and cold yellow-grey wolf's eyes. He wears black-dyed leather and dark iron scales with charcoal fur, and a GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, its snarling head on top and its pelt spilling over his shoulders. His face is utterly empty: no snarl, no anger, mouth a flat line, eyes level and unblinking, looking down at you as a wolf watches a straggler. Flat, bleached, shadowless winter daylight drains almost every colour, and snow glare whitens his face. Far below his windswept hilltop, a tribal camp lies scattered in the snow, thin smoke rising, grey wolves loping between its tents.
