CHARADON, the savage founder of the Doviello, called back from the dead by his shamans, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; old and past his prime but still hugely powerful; massive, heavy-shouldered and scarred, with weather-beaten pale skin, shaggy grey hair and a matted grey beard, cold yellow-grey eyes like a wolf's, and a flat, merciless stare with yellowed teeth bared in a half-snarl. He wears black-dyed leather and dark iron scales with charcoal fur, and a GREY WOLF-HEAD HOOD, the snarling wolf's head on top and its shaggy pelt spilling over his shoulders. Background: a frozen tundra under a heavy grey sky, a wolf pack loping along a snowy ridge.
