PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild unkempt black hair streaked with silver, restless gold-flecked eyes that seem to watch several things at once, and a too-wide grin that is half merriment, half menace. A harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds, a tall pleated white ruff and a heavy gold chain across his chest. A smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. Background: the torch-lit heart of a night carnival, striped gold-and-crimson tents and glowing lanterns.
