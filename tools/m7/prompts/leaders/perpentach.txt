PERPENTACH, the Laughing Man, the mad mind-mage king, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; looks about forty though far older; lean and wiry. A pale, sharp-featured face, wild black hair streaked with silver, gold-flecked eyes. A harlequin doublet of gold and crimson diamonds and a tall pleated white ruff. A smiling white porcelain CARNIVAL MASK pushed up on his forehead, one black tear painted under its eye. He leans toward you, head cocked, eyebrows high, lips pressed shut over a grin about to burst, eyes glittering as they search your face for the moment you understand; one long finger raised at his chest holds the pause. A single hard white spotlight falls on him from above; all else is black. In the darkness behind him, faint pale faces of every age, the minds he has swallowed, lean in with the same expectant grin.
