ARENDEL PHAEDRA, queen of the Seelie court, leader of the Ljosalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a wood elf; female; ageless, looks about forty; tall and regal. Fair skin, long wavy copper-auburn hair, long pointed elf ears, soft grey-green eyes. She stands with her back to a tall window, one hand pressed flat over her heart, head tilted as if listening for something far away; brows faintly drawn together, lips parted on an unspoken fear, eyes turned aside toward the glass, troubled and distant. A high-necked gown of deep emerald green with gold leaf embroidery and a mantle of russet autumn leaves. A delicate GOLDEN CROWN of interlaced LEAVES and branches. Light: cold blue-white snow-light from the window on one side of her face, faint warm hearth-glow on the other. Background: old rippled glass, the warped forest beyond in white blossom under falling snow.
