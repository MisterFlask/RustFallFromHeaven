TASUNKE, the most famous raider of the horse tribes and general of their mercenary companies, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; male; in his late forties; tall, broad-chested and rugged, with sun-darkened weathered skin, long black hair in two braids, a drooping black moustache, keen narrowed dark eyes that read terrain and armies at a glance, and a proud, boastful grin. He wears a rust-orange felt riding coat over lamellar armour of brown leather scales, a thick cream sheepskin collar, and a pointed steel steppe helmet topped with a flowing black HORSETAIL PLUME. Background: open grassland under a vast sky, a herd of horses running in the distance.
