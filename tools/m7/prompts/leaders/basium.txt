BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering and massively built. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw, burning pale-gold eyes, a grim, unshakeable, merciless expression. Dented silver-grey plate armour over a torn white tabard, battered grey-white feathered wings with ragged edges behind his shoulders, the iron head of a war hammer over one shoulder. THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow. Background: a war camp of white tents on scorched ground, a red glow of hellfire on the horizon under a smoky sky.
