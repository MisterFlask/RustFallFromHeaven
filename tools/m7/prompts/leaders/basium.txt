VIBE: Pain as prayer: the rebel angel who defied the gods, kneeling alone in falling ash with eyes shut and jaw locked, begging the very gods he abandoned for the strength to finish the war they told him to stop.

BASIUM, the rebel angel who defied the gods to keep fighting demons, leader of the Mercurians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a fallen angel in mortal flesh; male; ageless; towering, massive. Pale, weathered skin, a shaved head, a square jaw. His eyes are squeezed shut, brows knotted, lips moving in a fierce silent prayer, head bowed forward like a bull's; both huge fists clasped at his chest around the hilt of a small ceremonial dagger, held upright like a holy relic. THIN PALE RITUAL SCARS cut in straight lines across both cheeks and his brow, faintly shining. Dented silver-grey plate over a torn white tabard, battered grey-white wings with ragged feathers. Light: flat, colourless ashen twilight, everything grey and white except a dull red glow on one side of his face. Background: scorched ground and grey tents under falling ash, hellfire burning red on the horizon.
