BEERI BAWL, survivor and heir of the clan's founders, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and very strong. Ruddy weathered skin, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two braids, short-cropped red hair, small fierce dark eyes, the stubborn, defiant scowl of a survivor who never yields. A practical ash-grey quilted work jerkin with a dark-brown leather harness and brass buckles. A dented brass MINER'S HELMET with a small lamp on its brow. Background: a mountainside cave mouth opening onto daylight, a towering riveted iron clockwork golem with glowing orange eyes standing behind him.
