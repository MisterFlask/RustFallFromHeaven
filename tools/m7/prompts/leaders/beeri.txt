VIBE: The dwarf who survives out of pure spite: mud-caked in a mad jester-king's cage, a gold coin crushed in his fist, glaring at the laughing court and refusing to die because his death would please them.

BEERI BAWL, survivor and heir of the clan's founders, leader of the Luchuirp in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a gnome-like Luchuirp dwarf; male; middle-aged; short, stocky and strong. Ruddy weathered skin streaked with drying mud, a big hooked nose, a thick copper-red beard in two muddy braids, small fierce dark eyes. He glares straight at the viewer, head lowered like a bull about to charge, brows crushed down, nostrils flared, teeth bared in a snarl of pure defiance; one mud-caked fist held up at his chest, knuckles white around a small gold coin. A torn ash-grey quilted work jerkin with a leather harness. A dented, mud-smeared brass MINER'S HELMET, its little lamp dark. Light: harsh, flickering torchlight. Background: thick iron cage bars at both edges, and beyond them a garish throne room where nobles in grinning masks and gaudy saffron, rose and teal costumes laugh and applaud.
