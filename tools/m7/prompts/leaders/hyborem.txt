HYBOREM, Lord of the Infernals, the demon who broke out of hell to conquer the mortal world, leader of the Infernals in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: demon; male; ageless; towering and hulking, with massive shoulders. Deep crimson skin, burning yellow eyes under a heavy brow and a fanged grin full of impatient menace and cunning. Black obsidian spiked armour with gold trim, a tattered dark red cape, and the hilt of a black greatsword wreathed in orange flame rising beside his shoulder. Two huge curved black HORNS sweeping up from his brow. Background: a drowned city of black obsidian spires rising from a glowing red sea under a smoky crimson sky.
