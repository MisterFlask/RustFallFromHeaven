EINION LOGOS, the ancient head of the monastic order, leader of the Elohim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; very old; lean and upright, a former warrior. Deeply lined, weathered tan skin, a long white beard, close-cropped white hair, steady grey eyes heavy with grief yet calm and merciful, the face of a man who chose peace over vengeance. A plain undyed pilgrim's habit of white and ash-grey wool with a simple silver clasp, and no weapon. A small PAINTED SEASHELL hanging on a cord at his chest. Background: a quiet hillside shrine beside seven tall fir trees under a soft grey dawn.
