VIBE: the far-seeing queen alone before dawn, holding the painted seashell that proves the murder she ordered: no tears, no excuses, only the level acceptance that her hands are as bloody as the hireling's who did it, because she has foreseen what it will save.

VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress queen, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender and very still, with pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, and cool grey eyes. She wears a high-collared deep purple robe with lilac lining and silver piping, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. A small, delicately painted seashell lies in her open palm at her chest; she does not look at it. Her gaze goes past you into the far distance, face composed and unreadable, head inclined in the barest nod. Her face is split evenly in two: one half lit red-orange by a hanging bronze brazier, the other by the cold violet-grey of the hour before dawn from a tall arched window where the night is only beginning to pale.
