VALLEDIA THE EVEN, the far-sighted sorceress who rules the mage nation, leader of the Amurites in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her early fifties; slender, poised and very still, with pale olive skin, black hair streaked with silver and pinned up severely, cool grey eyes that seem to look past the present, and a composed, unreadable face, stubborn and logical, weighed by hard secret choices. She wears a high-collared deep purple robe with lilac lining and fine silver piping, and a slim silver circlet holding a single glowing VIOLET SEEING-STONE on her brow. Background: a quiet stone study with tall arched windows, shelves of books and a bronze brazier hanging on chains.
