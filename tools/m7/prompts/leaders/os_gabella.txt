OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long storm-grey hair blowing loose, dark violet eyes and a cold, dispassionate stare with a faint disdainful smirk. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown. Pale white glowing JEWELS set into her black armour, lighting her face from below. Background: towering black storm clouds split by violet lightning above the dark spires of a city.
