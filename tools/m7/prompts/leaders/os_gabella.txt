VIBE: the first woman, regarding you exactly as she regarded the god sent to claim her on the first morning of the world: one hand raised to halt you, calm as a lioness, faintly amused, the other hidden behind her back.

OS-GABELLA, Queen of Storms, the first woman made by the gods, who rebelled against them, leader of the Sheaim in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: immortal human; woman; ageless, looking about thirty-five; tall and regal. Flawless pale skin, long black hair stirring in the wind, dark violet eyes. Black plate armour with sharp pauldrons and a high gorget over a deep crimson gown. Pale white glowing JEWELS set into her black armour. Half turned away, she looks back at you over her shoulder with the calm, dangerous patience of a lioness surveying her land and the small smile of one who knows how this ends; one gauntleted hand is lifted at her chest, palm out: stop. The strange bright light before rain, low gold sunlight slanting under a slate-dark storm sky, lights her face. Behind her, wide grassland bends in the wind and the trees turn their leaves skyward.
