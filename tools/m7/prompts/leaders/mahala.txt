MAHALA, the cunning chieftain who rebuilt the Doviello after the age of ice, leader of the Doviello in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; about thirty-five; lean, wiry and hard as a huntress, with wind-reddened pale skin, long dark-auburn hair in thick braids woven with small bone beads, sharp amber eyes and a wary, calculating gaze, clever, defiant and fearless. She wears a black-dyed leather jerkin laced with cream rawhide, a charcoal wool hood pushed back, and a heavy BLACK BEAR-FUR MANTLE clasped at the chest with a large bear claw. Background: a smoky tribal great hall of rough timber and hides, lit by a central fire pit.
