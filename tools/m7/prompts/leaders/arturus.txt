ARTURUS THORNE, the dwarf king who carried his crown out of the Underhome, leader of the Khazad in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: a dwarf; male; old; short, barrel-chested and broad, with weathered ruddy skin, a long iron-grey beard in thick braids, bushy grey brows and shrewd deep-set brown eyes with a gruff, cunning glint, the face of an old soldier who wins by wit. He wears steel pauldrons chased with gold over a khaki-gold surcoat and a dark brown leather collar, the iron head of his great axe rising beside one shoulder, and a squat, heavy GOLD DWARVEN CROWN with blunt square points. Background: a torch-lit dwarven hall carved in grey rock, gold glinting on its pillars.
