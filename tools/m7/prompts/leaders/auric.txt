AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Frost-pale skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, ice-blue eyes. A white wolf-fur cloak over a pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, frayed prisoner's rags still at his neck. A jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE rising from his brow. He looks straight at you, utterly calm, with the faintest closed smile of someone keeping a secret you will never be told; his eyes too still and old for his young face. Behind him a broken stone archway pours out a blinding cold white light that rims his hair and leaves his face in soft blue shadow; in the ruined cavern around it, ghostly men in ancient robes kneel.
