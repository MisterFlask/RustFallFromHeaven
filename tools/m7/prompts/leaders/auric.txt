AURIC ULVIN, a farm boy and escaped prisoner who came out of the frozen mountain as lord of the lost men, leader of the Illians in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; man; about twenty; lean and hardened. Pale frost-touched skin, untidy white-blond hair rimed with frost, pale ice-blue eyes and the fervent, faraway stare of a visionary on the edge of madness. A heavy cloak of white wolf fur over a pale sky-blue robe edged in silver, frayed grey prisoner's rags at his neck. A jagged CROWN OF CLEAR BLUE ICE rising from his brow. Background: a collapsed mountain ringed by frozen waves of blue ice under a pale winter sky, faint ivory dragon wings in the clouds.
