FAERYL VICONIA, queen of the Unseelie winter court, leader of the Svartalfar in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: dark elf; woman; ageless, looking about thirty; tall and slender. Pale ash-grey skin, long straight silver-white hair falling past pointed ears, narrow violet eyes and a gracious courtly smile that never reaches her eyes, hiding cold envy and ambition. A high-collared gown of deep purple velvet with black lace and fine silver spiderweb embroidery, a small silver brooch at the throat. A tall black-and-silver CROWN SHAPED LIKE ARCHED SPIDER LEGS. Background: a moonlit forest court of black leafless trees under falling snow, strung with frost-silver webs.
