KEELYN, the unacknowledged daughter of the mad king and a child summoner of demons, leader of the Balseraphs in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; girl; about twelve years old; small and slight. A pale, sharp little face with a dusting of freckles, a tumble of raven-black hair tied back with a gold ribbon, large amber eyes and the sweet, unsettling grin of a child who has never learned to fear monsters. A gold-and-black harlequin dress with a ruffled gold collar and violet ribbons at the shoulders. A tiny grinning red bat-winged IMP perched on her shoulder like a pet. Background: a shadowy palace hallway hung with gold carnival lanterns, wisps of violet summoning mist curling through it.
