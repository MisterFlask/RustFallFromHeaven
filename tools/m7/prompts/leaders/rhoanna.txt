RHOANNA, the Hippus Queen who united the warring horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her late twenties; tall, strong and straight-backed, with sun-browned skin, a long wind-blown chestnut braid, clear green eyes and a determined, shrewd expression, proud and quick-witted with a hint of hidden softness. She wears her family's IMPOSING HEIRLOOM ARMOUR OF BURNISHED BRONZE PLATES, a little too broad for her, over a burnt-orange quilted gambeson, with a rust-brown wool cloak pinned at one shoulder. Background: a camp of round felt tents and tethered horses on rolling hills at golden dusk.
