RHOANNA, the Hippus Queen who united the warring horse tribes, leader of the Hippus in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: human; female; in her late twenties; tall and straight-backed, with sun-browned skin, a long chestnut braid and clear green eyes. She wears her family's IMPOSING HEIRLOOM ARMOUR OF BURNISHED BRONZE PLATES, a little too broad for her, one shoulder held stiff from an old wound, over a burnt-orange quilted gambeson, a rust-brown cloak at her back. She leans in, chin lowered, eyes fixed on someone just beside you with level, amused certainty, one brow raised in a sly half-smile, one hand held palm-up over the fire, offering a deal too good to refuse. Warm orange firelight blazes up from below, gilding the bronze; behind her, a smoky felt council tent at night, the shadows of laughing raiders thrown huge on its walls, indigo night at the open flap.
