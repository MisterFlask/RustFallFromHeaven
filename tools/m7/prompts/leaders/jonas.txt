JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green skin seamed with old battle scars, two thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a short black topknot, small fierce amber eyes under a heavy brow and a grim, cunning glare. Dented iron armour with rust-orange trim and a brown deerskin cloak charred black along its edges. A broad dark BURN SCAR covering the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. Background: a sacred bonfire roaring in a snowy orc camp under a smoky orange dusk.
