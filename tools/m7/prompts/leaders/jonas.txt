VIBE: the blasphemer whose goddess answered him: master of five thousand roaring spears, and servant, forever listening, of the voice in the fire he must carry until he dies.

JONAS ENDAIN, warlord of the Three-Spear tribe and first priest of the fire goddess, leader of the Clan of Embers in the fantasy game Fall from Heaven: orc; male; hard middle age; massive and broad-shouldered. Grey-green scarred skin, thick yellowed lower tusks, a shaved head with a black topknot, dented iron armour with rust-orange trim, a deerskin cloak charred at the edges. A broad dark BURN SCAR over the left side of his face and neck from the sacred fire he walked into. In both hands, held at his chest like something precious and terrible, burns a small, fierce, undying flame. His chin is up for his warriors, but his face has gone ashen, his amber eyes turned sideways to the flame, wide, as if it just spoke. Its blinding white-gold light from below is the only light; behind, in the black night, a forest of raised spears glints orange.
