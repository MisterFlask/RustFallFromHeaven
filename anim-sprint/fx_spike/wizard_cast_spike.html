Pixel art + particles: five ways to draw the same spell

Every panel runs the same particle simulation, the same AI-drawn wizard frames and skeleton hit pose, and the same timing. Only how the particles are drawn changes. Switch how the spell is delivered (a travelling fireball, a very fast one, or no projectile at all), slow it down, and try the day background to see where each one breaks. Turn sound on to hear the synthesized effects; slowing down pitches them down too.
