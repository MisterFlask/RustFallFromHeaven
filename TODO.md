## City specialties: a permanent buff drawn at culture level 3

**Status: design in progress** (2026-11-15). Replaces building tiers (docs/SUITABILITY.md), which
the owner judged the wrong direction.

### The idea

When a city reaches culture level 3, it draws one permanent buff at random. Each buff is one
specific rule that pushes the city toward a function. It is not a tier, a category multiplier or a
choice among charters.

The test for a buff: the owner builds or does something different in this city because of it.

### Agreed so far

- Buffs are specific single rules, in the register of "Melee units trained here get +3 XP".
- A buff that grants access to something a civic also grants adds something on top, so it is not
  blank once the civic is adopted.
- The unit-class XP buff is one pool entry. The class is rolled from what the city can train when
  it draws.
- Draws are without replacement within an empire, so no two of a player's cities share a buff.
- Buffs that need a site (coast, mines, farms, rivers) are drawn only by a city that has it.

### Cut, and why

- Enemy units in the radius cannot heal; +2 first strikes for defenders; bombardment at half
  speed; never revolts or flips. Passive: the value comes from where the city sits, and nothing
  in the city changes.
- This city pays no maintenance. Passive gold.
- This city provides 1 mana. Mana is shared across the empire, so it does not shape the city.
- Cultural radius one ring larger. A one-time head start.
- +3 health; +3 happiness. They raise a cap every city wants equally.
- Buildings are built here at 1.25x speed. Points nowhere.
- This city keeps 50% of its food when it grows. Overlaps the Granary and Smokehouse and the 60%
  cap on food kept.
