# Unit stats: the content pass (draft for review)

Draft of docs/UNIT_STATS.md section 7, generated by `tools/stats_draft.py` from the rules in its
first lines. Nothing here is in the game yet: `python tools/stats_draft.py --apply` writes it
into the data once approved. A cell shows the drafted value, **bold** with the current value in
brackets where it changes.

## What the draft does

- **Hit points, attack, defence and speed stay as they are** (the balance the game has today).
  The draft only moves the new stats: magic defence, accuracy, evasion and crit, and casters'
  magic.
- **Armour against magic.** Heavy infantry keeps three quarters of its defence against spells;
  casters and priests get two and a half times theirs. Mages crack shield walls; archers and
  cavalry, whose hits magic defence does not stop, kill mages.
- **Cavalry and skirmishers dodge, assassins crit, archers aim.**
- **Races and lines are promotions**, so they follow every unit of the race, a civilization's
  default race included (a Khazad Axeman is a Dwarf). Races may differ in worth; the level-up
  promotions are balanced against each other: an always-on pick is worth about a Combat level,
  one that works in some fights about one and a half where it works (the table below).

### By kind and unit

- Assassins have Marksman free, so their crit is 20 + 10 = 30.
- Casters' magic is 110% of their attack, against the Combat line's smaller magic bonus.
- Recon skirmishers (recon units that are not assassins): MDEF 90% of DEF, ACC 95, EVA 10.
- Animals and beasts: MDEF 60% of DEF, EVA +5.
- Large units: EVA 0.
- The Nullstone Golem: MDEF 400% of DEF. The Sentinels keep theirs.

### Level-up promotions: what each is worth

Fighting power (damage dealt per action times hits survived) a promotion adds to the unit that
takes it, in the fights it is made for, against an even enemy (spells 15% of the damage taken).
The aim: an always-on pick worth about a Combat level (+20%), a pick that works only in some fights
about one and a half (+30%) where it works. First strikes, withdrawal and the like are estimated
(a first strike +6%, the next ones less; withdrawal at half its chance).

A city, hills, forest or fortification bonus counts on both sides of a fight in its place (the
owner's rule, 2026-11-05): on the unit's ATK and DEF alike. The last column is its worth when
it counted on one side only (DEF when the unit defended, ATK when it attacked).

## Open questions

- Combat is worth +11% to a caster (+20% to a fighter); casters' main picks are their spheres.
- Hit points, attack and defence are untouched: should tiers or races differ there too (for
  example more HP for heavy infantry, less for skirmishers)?
- Combat I-V give casters +10% magic in the draft (+5% now; +20% before unit stats).
- Heals stay `40 x 50%`: scale them with magic?
- Heroes get +5 ACC, +5 CRIT, +20% MDEF from the Hero promotion; give some their own sheets?
